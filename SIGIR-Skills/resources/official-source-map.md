## Verified SIGIR 2027 facts used in the skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

- SIGIR 2027 is the **50th** International ACM SIGIR Conference, **in person in San Jose,
  California, USA**, July 19-24, 2027 per the home page (calls for Resource and Reproducibility and
  Replicability say July 18-24), with a Tianjin, China satellite location for presenters who cannot
  obtain a visa (sources 1, 2, 4, 5).
- **Full papers: at most 9 pages** including figures, tables, proofs, appendixes and
  acknowledgments — only references are free; `sigconf` with `anonymous=true`; **CCS concepts and
  keywords required for review**; double-anonymous; submitted on **OpenReview**; one extra page
  (10 plus references) after acceptance (source 2).
- **Full-paper dates (AoE):** abstract January 14, 2027; paper January 21, 2027; notification
  April 5, 2027; camera-ready and author registration April 30, 2027 (sources 1-3).
- **Author-list freeze:** the complete author list is fixed at abstract submission; every author
  must hold a complete OpenReview profile before the abstract deadline (source 2).
- **Reviewing duty:** one author per submission agrees to review, a different author for each
  submission (source 2); Resource papers nominate a reviewer in the EasyChair form (source 4).
- **Track lineup published so far:** Full, Short (TBA), Resource, Reproducibility and Replicability,
  Demonstrations, Perspectives, Tutorials, Workshops, Doctoral Colloquium, SIRIP/industry (TBA)
  (source 3). Resource and Reproducibility remain separate tracks, as introduced at SIGIR 2026; the
  latter is renamed **Reproducibility and Replicability** (sources 4, 5).
- **Per-track anonymity:** Full, Reproducibility and Replicability, Perspectives double-anonymous;
  **Resource and Demonstration single-anonymous** (sources 2, 4-7).
- **Per-track budgets:** Resource ≤ 6 pages + references (7 at camera-ready); Reproducibility and
  Replicability ≤ 9 + references; Perspectives ≤ 9 + references with a mandatory "Call for Action";
  Demonstrations ≤ 4 + references (sources 4-7).
- **Platforms:** Full papers on OpenReview; Resource, Reproducibility and Replicability and
  Perspectives on EasyChair (`easychair.org/my/conference?conf=sigir2027`) (sources 2, 4-6).
- **Policy layer:** ACM authorship policy and ACM policy on the use of AI; concurrent submission
  (including to another SIGIR 2027 track) is a violation referred to ACM; anonymous code
  repositories only; a new **Chair for Integrity and Robustness** runs automated checks; no rebuttal
  in the Resource track (sources 2, 4).
- **Leadership:** General Chairs Yi Zhang, Yi Fang, Chenyan Xiong; Program Chairs Mounia Lalmas,
  Qiaozhu Mei, Mark Sanderson (sources 2, 8). SIGIR has **no standing editor-in-chief**; roles rotate
  per edition.
- SIGIR proceedings are published by **ACM in the Digital Library** (source 12); SIGIR 2025's
  record (DOI 10.1145/3726302) is the most recent volume recorded here.
