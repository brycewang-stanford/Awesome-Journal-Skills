## Verified live-cycle facts used in the skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

- The live edition is **S&P 2027, the 48th symposium, May 17-19, 2027 (workshops May 20),
  Le Centre Sheraton Montreal Hotel, Montreal, Canada**. General Chair Adam Aviv; PC Chairs
  Michelle Mazurek (University of Maryland) and Nicolas Papernot (University of Toronto,
  Vector Institute and Google DeepMind).
- Both 2027 cycles are closed. **Cycle 1**: abstract registration June 4, 2026; paper June 11,
  2026; early reject July 27; reviews August 20; interactive period from August 26; rebuttal
  text August 27; interactive rebuttal and revised manuscript September 3; acceptance
  September 11; submission to shepherd October 2; camera-ready October 16, 2026.
  **Cycle 2**: abstract registration November 10, 2026; paper November 17, 2026; early reject
  January 18, 2027; reviews February 11; rebuttal text February 16; interactive period
  February 17-26; acceptance March 5; submission to shepherd March 26; camera-ready
  April 8, 2027.
- All deadlines are **23:59:59 AoE (UTC-12)**. The HotCRP pages display the same instants in
  US Eastern time (e.g., 7:59:59 AM EDT the next morning).
- Registration is substantive: the full abstract, the complete author list with ORCIDs, and
  declared conflicts are due at registration, and neither title, abstract nor author list may
  change afterwards. ORCID records must match the HotCRP names and emails, or the paper is
  desk rejected.
- **At most 6 papers per author per cycle**, enforced at the registration deadline; exceeding
  it desk-rejects all of that author's registrations in the cycle.
- Format: up to 13 pages of text plus up to 5 pages of references and appendices, never more
  than 18 pages in total (SoK references do not count); US letter; IEEEtran.cls 1.8b with
  `conference,compsoc`. Reviewers need not read appendices. Over-length or re-formatted
  papers are rejected without review.
- **Decisions are Accept or Reject only** — in both the 2026 and 2027 CFPs. There is no
  conditional acceptance and no revise-and-resubmit outcome; papers are judged on what was
  submitted plus anything reviewers explicitly requested during an interactive rebuttal.
- Second-round papers get a rebuttal. The PC assigns each paper either a **non-interactive
  rebuttal (750 words**, factual errors and reviewer questions only, no new results) or an
  **interactive rebuttal** (the 750-word text, then a HotCRP comment with requested new
  results plus a revised manuscript PDF). Breaking the rebuttal rules means immediate
  rejection.
- Accepted papers carry a **public meta-review (< 500 words)** with an optional author
  response (< 500 words); a shepherd removes concerns the camera-ready resolves. Camera-ready
  is due within one month of acceptance, and papers appear open access in the Computer
  Society Digital Library immediately.
- Every accepted paper presents a **short talk (5-7 minutes) and a poster**; one author is
  expected to register and present.
- A rejected paper may not return for one year from the original submission date. A paper
  counts as a resubmission when it comes from the same or similar authors with a very similar
  intellectual contribution, such that a reviewer could write a substantially similar summary;
  rewriting or re-angling does not reset the clock. Papers desk rejected for format or
  anonymity without reviews may come back in the next cycle.
- Withdrawal is possible only until reviews are sent to the authors.
- Ethics: every paper completes the "Ethics Considerations" HotCRP field at registration
  (writing "None" plus how the authors concluded that); accepted papers add an Ethics
  Considerations section at camera-ready, outside the page limit; a Research Ethics Committee
  of PC members checks flagged papers and may recommend rejection; the Menlo Report is the
  reference. IRB approval/waiver must be disclosed for human-subjects work; PII-touching work
  must detail harm mitigation; live-system and new-attack work must weigh harms and benefits.
  Vulnerabilities must be disclosed no later than the rebuttal deadline, and submissions omit
  full CVE identifiers to stay anonymous.
- Competing financial and non-financial interests are disclosed in the camera-ready.
- Preprints (e.g., arXiv), talks and vendor disclosure are allowed during review; wide
  advertising is not.
- Artifacts are strongly encouraged at submission in anonymized repositories that are not
  updated after the paper deadline; post-acceptance artifact evaluation is optional with
  Available, Functional and Reproduced badges.
- SoK papers are a first-class submission type, marked by the "SoK:" title prefix and a
  checkbox, judged on systematization value.
- Generative AI: disclosed in the "Generative AI usage considerations" HotCRP field at
  registration; no AI authors; model-generated ideas independently developed and validated;
  AI-introduced limitations and the environmental footprint of experiments discussed.
- The series has run annually since 1980 (Claremont Resort, hence "Oakland"), under the IEEE
  Computer Society TC on Security and Privacy with IACR cooperation, and publishes through
  IEEE; dblp's `conf/sp` stream confirms edition numbering.

## Corrections made 2026-11-24

- **Decision set**: earlier versions of this pack described an Accept / Revise / Reject set
  with a structured "Revise" major revision. The live 2026 and 2027 CFPs list only Accept and
  Reject; the revise-like step is the interactive rebuttal with a revised manuscript.
- **Resubmission rule**: the "roughly 40% overlap" threshold is not in the 2027 CFP; the test
  is a very similar intellectual contribution from the same or similar authors.
- **Deadline clock**: the CFP states 23:59:59 AoE; the earlier "fixed 7:59:59 AM EDT, not AoE"
  reading came from the HotCRP display of the same instant.
- **Symposium dates**: May 17-19, 2027 (workshops May 20), not May 18.
- **Cycle 1 timetable, rebuttal format and length, the per-author cap, the 2027 artifact
  schedule, the PC chairs and REC chairs, and the generative-AI wording** are now confirmed
  (previously 待核实).
