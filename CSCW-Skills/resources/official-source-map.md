This map records the official sources behind every CSCW-specific fact in this pack.

**Edition status, checked 2026-11-24.** CSCW 2026 (Salt Lake City, Utah, USA,
October 10-14, 2026) has met; its rows below are kept as labelled history. The live
edition is **CSCW 2027**, the 30th ACM Conference on Computer-Supported Cooperative
Work and Social Computing, **Philadelphia, PA, USA, October 16-20, 2027**, at the
Loews Philadelphia Hotel (site online since October 16, 2026; pages updated
2026-11-06). There is no CSCW 2027 paper deadline: the CSCW 2027 Submit page says
submissions to PACM HCI/CSCW "are now open", that there is "no secret deadline", and
that a paper accepted before the program is set is invited to present at CSCW 2027.
To present at CSCW a paper must be accepted at one of two journals — **PACM HCI/CSCW**
or **ACM ToCHI** (if it meets the CSCW scope criteria). General Chairs are Louise
Barkhuus (New York University) and Andrew Miller (Indiana University Indianapolis);
Technical Program Chairs are Jed Brubaker (University of Colorado Boulder) and
Elizabeth Churchill (MBZUAI). Registration, student-volunteer and online-experience
details for CSCW 2027 are "posted as available" and not yet out. So this pack now
describes the rolling pathway into CSCW 2027.

## Verified facts used in the skills (as of 2026-11-24)

- CSCW is the ACM SIGCHI Conference on Computer-Supported Cooperative Work and
  Social Computing. Accepted papers are not published in a standalone proceedings
  volume: they appear in **PACMHCI (Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer
  Interaction)**, in the journal's CSCW track, historically as paired issues
  (e.g., Vol. 8, CSCW1 April 2024 with 209 papers; Vol. 8, CSCW2 November 2024
  with 171 papers).
- **CSCW 2027** takes place October 16-20, 2027 in Philadelphia, PA, USA (Loews
  Philadelphia Hotel); General Chairs Louise Barkhuus and Andrew Miller, Technical
  Program Chairs Jed Brubaker and Elizabeth Churchill. **CSCW 2026** took place
  October 10-14, 2026 in Salt Lake City; CSCW 2025 was held October 18-22, 2025 in
  Bergen, Norway.
- **Route to a CSCW 2027 talk:** acceptance at PACM HCI/CSCW (submissions open now,
  no deadline) or at ACM ToCHI if the paper meets the CSCW scope criteria. A paper
  accepted before the year's program is set is invited to that year's conference
  (and may instead request the following year); one accepted after the program is
  set is offered a slot the following year. Presenting is encouraged but not
  required.
- **Rolling tracks (CSCW 2027 & beyond):** Design/Theory, Mixed Methods,
  Qualitative, Quantitative, Systems. Papers combining methods from several tracks
  (e.g., system plus survey) typically go to Mixed Methods.
- **Rolling mechanics (rolling CFP, 2026-07-30 capture):** submit via Manuscript
  Central (https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/pacmhci), not PCS; Editors-in-Chief Amy
  Bruckman and Eric Gilbert, with per-track editors and Associate Editors; a cover
  letter with a scope statement and submission history; decisions roughly 3-4 months
  from initial submission; R&R may run multiple rounds with the same editorial team;
  rejected papers must wait at least four months before resubmitting a substantially
  revised version.
- The **May 2025 cycle** was the final fixed-deadline cycle and fed CSCW 2026:
  submissions due May 14, 2025, 12:00 EDT on PCS; first notification August 19,
  2025; Revise-for-External-Review resubmissions September 16, 2025 (AoE);
  external-review decisions November 11, 2025 (33.8% final decision, 66.2% Revise
  and Resubmit); R&R resubmissions January 13, 2026; final decisions March-April
  2026 (152 accepted, 44 rejected).
- **Formatting:** single-column **ACM Small (acmsmall)** template (Word authors: the
  interim ACM Small template from the PACMHCI submission-templates page); a
  completely different template is desk rejected; no formal length cap; papers
  shorter than about 5,000 words or longer than about 12,000 words receive added
  scrutiny, and papers whose length is incommensurate with their contribution can
  be desk rejected. The 2026-07-30 capture of the rolling CFP carries the same
  template rule and word band.
- Review is anonymous ("double-blind" in ACM usage): author names, affiliations,
  university/company/lab/city references, identifying grant details and identifying
  images must be removed from all submitted materials, supplements included.
- **LLM policy (CSCW 2026 CFP; same text in the rolling CFP capture):** text
  generated by an LLM must be clearly marked where the tool is used beyond editing
  the authors' own text, and the disclosure must be visible in the review version
  (the LaTeX template hides Acknowledgements in review mode); papers with unmarked or
  inappropriate LLM use (e.g., fabricated references) are desk rejected.
- **Open Access:** CSCW papers are published under ACM Open; corresponding authors
  from non-participating institutions pay an APC, subsidized in 2026 to $250
  (ACM/SIG members) or $350 (non-members).
- The decision vocabulary is journal-shaped: Revise for External Review (2026
  internal round), Revise and Resubmit, Conditional Accept with Minor Changes,
  Accept, and Reject.
- CSCW confers a **Lasting Impact Award** on a paper 10+ years old whose
  influence persists; the recipients listed in source 15 anchor the exemplar
  library.
