## Verified cycle facts used in the skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

Every one is a cycle snapshot, not a rule.

- **NDSS 2027 meets at the Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas Hotel, Seoul, Republic of
  Korea, 22-26 March 2027** (sources 1, 5) — a deliberate departure from the San Diego
  February slot that NDSS held through 2026 (source 6).
- **Two submission cycles feed the one symposium**, both now closed: summer papers were due
  **6 May 2026** and fall papers **19 August 2026**, 11:59 PM AoE (UTC-12) (source 2).
- **Summer-cycle timetable**: early reject / Round 2 notification with Round 1 reviews
  18 June; rebuttal 15-17 July; interactive discussion 15-22 July; notification 29 July;
  Minor Revision approval by the shepherd 19 August; Major Revision resubmission 31 August;
  Major Revision notification 16 September; camera-ready 30 September 2026 (source 2).
- **Fall-cycle timetable**: Round 1 reviews 18 September - 13 October; Round 2 review
  deadline 18 November; rebuttal 19-21 November; interactive discussion 19-26 November;
  final notification 2 December 2026; Major Revision resubmission / Minor Revision decision
  5 January 2027; Major Revision notification 25 January 2027; camera-ready 7 February 2027
  (source 2).
- **Review runs in two rounds per cycle**: Round 1 filters, with an early notification that
  shares Round 1 reviews and says whether the paper continues; Round 2 papers get a rebuttal
  plus an interactive discussion with reviewers (source 2).
- **Decisions**: Accept; Minor Revision (accepted if a shepherd approves the revision);
  Major Revision (promising, with explicit "revision tasks" from the original reviewers,
  re-reviewed against them, at most one per paper); Reject (may not be resubmitted to NDSS
  2027) (source 2).
- **A paper rejected in the summer cycle may not reappear with major overlap in the fall
  cycle**; doing so is grounds for desk rejection (source 2).
- **Format**: technical papers must not exceed 13 pages excluding the Ethics Considerations
  section, references and appendices; English; US letter, two columns no more than 9.25 in.
  high and 3.5 in. wide, Times 10-point or larger on 11-point or larger line spacing; NDSS
  templates; PDF that renders in black and white (source 2).
- **Ethics Considerations section is optional**, placed immediately before the references;
  papers touching human subjects, human-derived data or legal risk should disclose any ethics
  review (e.g., IRB) and discuss mitigation; IRB exemption alone may not suffice; high-impact
  vulnerabilities need reporting or a responsible-disclosure plan; an Ethics Review Board of
  TPC members checks flagged papers; the Menlo Report is the recommended baseline (source 2).
- **Review is double-blind**; prior work cited in the third person; arXiv preprints not
  forbidden but wide advertisement is (source 2).
- **Each author may appear on at most six submissions per cycle** — twelve in total; no author
  may be added after the submission deadline; a paper can be withdrawn only before reviews are
  sent (source 2).
- **Generative AI** is permitted for paper preparation, with disclosure at submission
  commensurate with the amount generated (sections and tool versions for whole sections; a
  footnote plus an Acknowledgements disclaimer for a few sentences); grammar-only editing need
  not be disclosed (source 2).
- **SoK papers** are in scope and need no "SoK:" prefix; a "Topic Concerns" sub-committee may
  desk reject papers that fit poorly (pure theory or proofs without implementation, mainly
  ML/AI contributions, no security contribution) (source 2).
- **Proceedings are fully open access** through the Internet Society; authors may post
  camera-ready versions on personal and institutional pages (sources 2, 8).
- **Artifact evaluation is opt-in after (conditional) acceptance**, produces first-page badges
  and a 2-page artifact appendix; 2027 summer AE submission 10 August 2026, fall AE submission
  13 December 2026, fall decisions 1 February 2027 (sources 3, 9).
- **Leadership**: General Chairs Mauro Conti and Roberto Perdisci; Program Chairs Ivan
  Martinovic and Martina Lindorfer (source 12).
- **NDSS paper DOIs use the Internet Society prefix 10.14722** (cross-checked via the
  Kitsune dblp/Semantic Scholar record, DOI `10.14722/NDSS.2018.23204`).
- **Test of Time**: "Automated Whitebox Fuzz Testing" (NDSS 2008) won the 2022 award;
  the SMART remote-attestation architecture (NDSS 2012) won the 2024 award (source 10 plus
  Internet Society and UC Irvine announcements).

## Still cycle-volatile

Dates, page limits, the per-author cap, revision mechanics, ethics-section wording, ERB
procedure, template versions, HotCRP URLs, badge definitions, and the host city itself (as
the 2027 Seoul move proves) can all change per symposium. The controlling source is always
the newest official CFP or a direct message from the chairs; record any conflict you find.
