Internet/network measurement. Its defining mechanics — two submission deadlines per year, the
**One-Shot-Revision** decision, the mandatory **Ethics** appendix, the **artifact-availability

## Verified IMC 2027 facts used in skills

- **IMC 2027 is held in Atlanta, Georgia, USA, October 25-29, 2027** — the 27th IMC, sponsored by
  ACM SIGCOMM (source 4). General Chairs **Alberto Dainotti** and **Mike Bailey** (Georgia Tech);
  Program Chairs **Romain Fontugne** (Internet Initiative Japan) and **Narseo Vallina-Rodriguez**
  (IMDEA Networks Institute) (source 3).
- **Two submission deadlines (all 11:59PM AoE, hard, no extensions):** cycle 1 registration
  **10 November 2026**, submission **17 November 2026**, One-Shot-Revision papers from IMC 2026
  **2 December 2026**, early reject **5 January 2027**, **rebuttal 26 January - 2 February 2027**,
  notification **25 February 2027**, shepherd approval 17 March 2027, camera-ready **14 April 2027**.
  Cycle 2: Replicability EoI **13 January 2027** (EoI notification 10 February 2027), registration
  **31 March 2027**, submission **7 April 2027**, One-Shot-Revision papers from cycle 1 28 April
  2027, early reject 19 May 2027, notification **22 July 2027**, shepherd approval 12 August 2027,
  camera-ready **2 September 2027** (sources 1, 2, 4).
- **Decision categories: Accept / One-Shot-Revision / Reject.** One-Shot-Revision gives specific
  action points (up to 3 additions/changes) and requires **at least one champion** among the
  reviewers; the paper is resubmitted at the next deadline and judged by the **same reviewers**.
  Consensus-reject papers are notified **early**. **New for 2027: a short rebuttal phase** to
  clarify specific reviewer concerns (source 1; source 2 describes it as for Round 2 papers).
  Rejected papers cannot be resubmitted at the next deadline — approximately **12 months** must
  pass since the last IMC deadline they were submitted to (source 1).
- **Double-blind review**, **ACM `acmart` template**
  (`\documentclass[10pt,sigconf,letterpaper,anonymous,nonacm]{acmart}`), and authors must upload the
  **BBL file** of references on the reviewing system; PDF under 15 MB; abstract recommended at no
  more than 200 words; non-compliant papers desk-rejected (source 2).
- **Page limits (IMC 2027):** full papers **up to 13 pages** of technical content **+ unlimited**
  references and appendices; short papers **up to 6 pages** + unlimited references and appendices;
  any paper with **more than 6 pages** of technical content is reviewed as a full paper (sources
  1, 2). IMC 2026 used the same 13/6 budget (source 11).
- **Three mandatory appendix sections (new for 2027):** **Ethics** (papers without it may be
  rejected; may be the single sentence "This work does not raise any ethical issues"), **Open
  Science** (artifacts to be released, or why they cannot be), and **Generative AI** (if, where and
  how generative AI was used). Human-subjects work must have **IRB** approval/exemption and the
  determination form is submitted; ethics is judged against **Belmont Report** principles; security
  vulnerabilities must be responsibly disclosed before submission (source 2).
- **Generative-AI policy (IMC 2027):** authors may use generative AI in limited ways per ACM's
  author guidelines; use must be disclosed in the mandatory Generative AI appendix section, with a
  footnote for small AI-generated text spans; authors retain full responsibility; the BBL/plain-text
  reference upload supports reference-validity checks. Reviewers may not upload submissions to
  generative AI tools (source 2).
- **Artifact-availability declaration** (full / partial / no availability) is required at
  submission; accepted papers are **shepherded**; legitimate proprietary/privacy reasons are
  accepted if explained (sources 1, 2).
- **Replicability Track:** an **Expression of Interest** (1-page abstract, deadline 13 January 2027)
  is screened by a small committee; strong abstracts are invited to full submission at the cycle-2
  deadlines, judged by the TPC against main-track criteria; **priority to replicability over
  reproducibility** studies (sources 1, 7).
- **Awards:** **Best Paper**, **Best Student Paper**, and **Community Contribution Award** (data or
  code public by the camera-ready deadline) (sources 1, 10).
- **Publication cost (2027):** ACM proceedings are fully Open Access; authors at ACM Open
  institutions pay nothing, others pay an APC unless waived, subsidized for 2027 at **$500**
  (ACM/SIG members) / **$750** (non-members) (source 1).

## The two-deadline / One-Shot-Revision model (honest reading of the cycle)

Since the recent restructuring, IMC runs **two deadlines a year** with a shared review pipeline.
The distinctive round is **One-Shot-Revision**: not a full journal-style major revision, but a
tightly bounded revise-and-resubmit — a limited number of concrete action points, a required
reviewer champion, and re-judgement by the *same* reviewers at the *next* deadline. This is
IMC's answer to giving good-but-incomplete measurement work a second chance without a rolling
journal. Whether a given year keeps exactly this cadence, the exact number of action points, and
the registration-to-submission gap are per-edition decisions — **verify on the current call and
Important Dates before advising**.

## No editor-in-chief; conference registration model

IMC has **no standing editor-in-chief**; the rotating analogue is the per-edition General Chairs
and Program Chairs appointed under ACM SIGCOMM. Proceedings are published in the **ACM Digital
Library**; the cost model is conference registration, and at least one author presents each
accepted paper. From 2027 ACM proceedings are fully Open Access, so authors outside ACM Open
institutions face an APC (2027 subsidized rate: $500 ACM/SIG members, $750 non-members; source 1).
