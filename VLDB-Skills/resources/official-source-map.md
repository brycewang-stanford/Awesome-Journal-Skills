# Official Source Map - VLDB / PVLDB

Access date: 2026-07-08. PVLDB Volume 20 submission guidelines, the VLDB 2027 research,
industrial and demonstration calls, important dates, formatting guidelines, officers, the
PVLDB reproducibility page and the VLDB 2028 site **re-verified 2026-11-24** against the live
pages (direct fetch of `vldb.org`).

**Edition status, checked 2026-11-24.** The live volume is still **PVLDB Volume 20**, feeding
**VLDB 2027** (53rd VLDB, Athens, Greece, August 23-27, 2027); its monthly deadlines run from
April 1, 2026 to March 1, 2027. The **VLDB 2028** site is up — 54th VLDB, **Marrakech,
Morocco, August 28 - September 1, 2028**, with its officers listed (General Program Chairs
Bettina Kemme and Mohamed Mokbel) — but its Research Track call, Important Dates, Formatting
and Submission Guidelines pages all read "This page is under construction. Information for
VLDB 2028 will be available soon." PVLDB Volume 21 guidelines are therefore not published,
and this pack stays on Volume 20 / VLDB 2027. Volume 20 papers accepted after July 1, 2027
roll over to VLDB 2028.

This map records the official sources behind every VLDB-specific fact in this pack.
VLDB research papers are submitted to PVLDB (Proceedings of the VLDB Endowment) on a
rolling monthly calendar, and each PVLDB volume re-publishes its own submission
guidelines. Reopen the guidelines page of the volume you are actually submitting to —
not last volume's page — before giving deadline-ready advice.

**Access-method note.** On 2026-07-08 direct fetches of `vldb.org`, `dl.acm.org`,
`dblp.org`, and `web.archive.org` returned HTTP 403, so that build relied on
search-engine renderings and a GitHub mirror of the Vol 16 formatting instructions. On
2026-11-24 every `vldb.org` page in rows 1-7 and 13-14 was fetched directly and read in
full; rows still dated 2026-07-08 have not been re-read since.

## Verified facts used across the skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

- PVLDB, not the conference site, is where VLDB research papers are submitted: acceptance
  into the volume is what earns the conference slot (source 2).
- The live volume on 2026-11-24 is **Volume 20** (deadlines April 1, 2026 through
  March 1, 2027, feeding VLDB 2027 in Athens, August 23-27, 2027). Volume 20 papers
  accepted by July 1, 2027 present at VLDB 2027; the last revision deadline that still
  counts for VLDB 2027 is June 1, 2027; later acceptances roll over to VLDB 2028
  (Marrakech, August 28 - September 1, 2028) (sources 1, 2, 13).
- The monthly rhythm: abstract by the 25th, CMT opens the 20th, paper due the 1st at
  5:00 PM Pacific Time (not AoE), reviews around the 15th of the following month
  (sources 1, 3).
- Outcomes are accept, revise, or reject; a revision verdict is one-shot, with **up to
  2.5 months** to return the revised paper (by the 1st of one of the following three
  months) — the July build's "up to three months" is superseded (source 1).
- Per-author caps in Vol 20: two submissions per month and twelve per volume, one CMT
  account per author; each submission nominates a qualified author as a possible
  Light Load Reviewer (source 1).
- Research-track categories in Vol 20: Regular Research (12 pp), EA&B (12 pp), Scalable
  Data Science (8 pp), Vision (6 pp), all excluding references; references are
  unlimited, but appendices and acknowledgements count against the limit; non-regular
  categories carry a title-suffix tag (source 1).
- Review is **single-blind**: "VLDB is a single-blind conference. Therefore, authors MUST
  include their names and affiliations on the first page of the manuscript" (source 1).
- A research-track rejection carries a **12-month resubmission embargo**; withdrawing
  after a revision decision counts as rejection; the embargo does not apply across
  tracks (source 1).
- The platform is Microsoft CMT (https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/PVLDBv20_2027/).
- Reproducibility: supplemental material is mandatory unless its absence is explained;
  availability earns an official ACM badge; the PVLDB Reproducibility Evaluation is
  opt-in for most categories, mandatory for EA&B papers (which must link the full
  package at initial submission), and carries a Best Reproducible Paper Award
  (sources 1, 12).
- Demonstrations (VLDB 2027): 4 pages inclusive of everything, single-anonymous,
  mandatory video, at most 3 proposals per author (source 6). Industrial track: 12 pages
  excluding references, single-blind, March 15, 2027 deadline (source 7).
- VLDB is run by the VLDB Endowment; PVLDB papers are openly accessible on vldb.org and
  there is no APC — the cost model is conference registration, with at least one author
  of each accepted paper required to attend. The per-volume PVLDB Editors-in-Chief
  double as conference PC chairs: Katja Hose and Matthias Boehm for Vol 20 / VLDB 2027
  (source 4).
