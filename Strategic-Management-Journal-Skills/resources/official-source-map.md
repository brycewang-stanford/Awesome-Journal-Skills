# SMJ Official Source Map

Verified official and authoritative sources used to make this skill pack specific to the
**Strategic Management Journal (SMJ)** — the flagship journal of the **Strategic Management
Society (SMS)**, published by **Wiley**. All entries accessed **2026-05-30**.
Submission rules, fees, review mode, data and AI policy were **re-verified 2026-11-23** against the
SMJ Author Guidelines PDF (last updated 23 May 2025), which was read directly (curl + pdftotext).
Access-method note: on 2026-11-23 the Wiley author hub, funded-access and editorial-board pages
(sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com) and the SMS site (strategicmanagement.net) returned HTTP 403 to curl and
WebFetch, so the Co-Editor slate and the OnlineOpen APC were **not** re-read. Changes found on
2026-11-23: the PDF confirms verbatim the **no-submission-fee** line and the **40-page / 20-page**
length rules (both moved out of UNVERIFIED); new submissions go to **Wiley Research Exchange**
(wiley.atyponrex.com/journal/SMJ), so pack lines calling ScholarOne the portal were fixed; the
**cover letter must answer three questions** (same-database papers, citing all prior work), which
the submission skill had omitted; article types are Research Article, Commentary, Prospective,
Special Issue Article, and Editorial (the pack's "Research Note" label was dropped).

> Volatile specifics (editor roster, fees, page/word limits, decision times, acceptance rate)
> change. Re-verify on the official pages below before relying on any number.

## Representative / landmark SMJ papers (verified as SMJ publications)

| Paper | Locator | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Wernerfelt, B. (1984). A resource-based view of the firm. | *SMJ*, 5(2): 171–180 | Foundational resource-based view (RBV) statement; published in SMJ |
| Teece, D. J., Pisano, G., & Shuen, A. (1997). Dynamic capabilities and strategic management. | *SMJ*, 18(7): 509–533 (doi:10.1002/smj.4250160303 is the Wernerfelt 1995 retrospective) | Canonical dynamic-capabilities paper; published in SMJ |
| Wernerfelt, B. (1995). The resource-based view of the firm: Ten years after. | *SMJ*, 16(3): 171–174 (doi:10.1002/smj.4250160303) | RBV retrospective; published in SMJ |

> Note: Barney (1991) "Firm resources and sustained competitive advantage" is frequently
> associated with strategy but was published in **Journal of Management**, not SMJ — do not
> cite it as an SMJ landmark. Use Wernerfelt (1984) and Teece, Pisano & Shuen (1997) as the
> verified SMJ exemplars of RBV and dynamic capabilities.

Sources: the **Wikipedia SMJ article** read directly 2026-06-23 (<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_Management_Journal>), which lists exactly these four with affiliations, corroborated by multiple **Wiley/SMS** search snapshots tied to the 2026 issues. This resolves the earlier 2026-06-22 conflict: the prior reading of the **official SMS editors page** (<https://www.strategicmanagement.net/smj/overview/leadership/editors>) had shown **Sendil Ethiraj** plus an unnamed fourth — that captured the **prior/transitioning** masthead; **Ethiraj has since rotated off** in favor of Agarwal and Silverman. Co-editorships rotate, so **confirm the current slate on the live SMS masthead / Wiley editorial-board page before naming an editor in a cover letter.** Published by **Wiley** for the **Strategic Management Society (SMS)**; submit via the **Wiley Research Exchange** portal (editorial office SMJedoffice@wiley.com). **Median time to first decision ~54 days.** (Wiley SMJ / SMS)
