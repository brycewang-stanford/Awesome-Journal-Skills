Access date: 2026-08-26 (re-anchored from the ICLR 2026 cycle to ICLR 2027)

This map records the official sources used for ICLR-specific facts in this pack. ICLR policies are
cycle-specific; reopen the current CFP, Author Guidelines, Reviewer Guidelines, AI policy, Code of
Ethics, Code of Conduct, and OpenReview group before giving submission-ready advice.

**Anchored to ICLR 2027** — the 15th International Conference on Learning Representations,
April 26-30, 2027, California. Unlike most re-anchors in this repository, this one is **live**: the
abstract deadline is 2026-09-18 and the full-paper deadline 2026-09-25, both ahead of this access
date. The CFP flags new policies for this cycle in three areas — co-authorship quotas, reciprocal
reviewing, and AI use — and each is a desk-rejection risk rather than a style preference, so they are
recorded in full below.

## Verified ICLR 2027 facts used in skills

- Submissions use OpenReview (`ICLR.cc/2027/Conference`) with double-blind review and public review
  and discussion. Any author identity revealed in the main text **or the supplementary material** is
  a desk rejection.
- Timeline: abstracts 2026-09-18 AOE, full papers 2026-09-25 AOE, reviews released 2026-11-05,
  author-reviewer discussion 2026-11-05 to 11-18, reviewer-AC discussion and meta-reviewing
  2026-11-19 to 12-16, final decisions 2026-12-16. The conference runs April 26-30, 2027 (main
  conference 26-28, workshops 29-30) in California. The deadlines are final; no accommodations.
- Abstracts must be genuine — they drive reviewer bidding before the paper deadline, and placeholder
  or duplicate abstracts are removed. Titles and abstracts may be edited until the paper deadline;
  the title is then frozen until the discussion period ends.
- **No authors may be added after the abstract deadline.** Author order may still change up to the
  full-paper deadline; after it, no author changes at all.
- Main text is capped at **9 pages at submission**, rising to **10 pages** for the discussion/rebuttal
  phase and camera-ready. Strictly enforced — over-length main text is desk-rejected. References are
  unlimited and outside the cap; appendices after the bibliography are unlimited, and reviewers are
  not required to read them.
- **A mandatory AI use statement is required in the paper** and does not count toward the page limit;
  the submission form also solicits it. The policy separates tasks whose disclosure is *required*
  (synthetic data, theoretical models, mathematical claims, proof assistance, hypotheses, methodology
  or experiment design, implementing methods, translation, dataset cleaning, qualitative analysis,
  interpreting results) from those where it is *recommended* (figures, code, drafting, literature
  search, brainstorming, titles, reference formatting and similar). Authors are responsible for the
  result: an LLM-produced falsehood, plagiarism or misrepresentation is a Code-of-Ethics violation by
  the authors and may lead to desk rejection.
- Ethics statement (optional, at the end of the main text before references, at most one page) and
  reproducibility statement (optional, same position) are both outside the page limit.
- **Quotas, new this cycle:** no author may appear on more than **20** papers, and each author may
  appear on **at most one** paper where no author is an eligible reciprocal reviewer. Excess
  submissions are flagged during the abstract period, and if still over quota at the paper deadline,
  excess submissions are **randomly desk-rejected** until every author is under quota.
- **Reciprocal reviewing, new this cycle:** every author on **3 or more** submissions must review at
  least **6** papers, and failing to file complete, high-quality reviews by the rebuttal stage can
  desk-reject their submissions. Every submission must additionally have at least one author
  registered to review at least **3** papers, qualified by an accepted primary-conference publication
  at a listed venue (ICLR/NeurIPS/ICML/UAI/AISTATS/JMLR/TMLR; ACL/EMNLP/EACL/NAACL/IJCNLP-AACL/CL/
  TACL; COLM; CVPR/ICCV/ECCV/PAMI/3DV; AAAI/IJCAI/JAIR; ICRA/IROS/RSS/CoRL; KDD; COLT). Workshop
  papers, position papers, tiny papers, blog posts, demos and industry-track papers do **not**
  qualify. Eligibility is judged on papers accepted **as of the abstract deadline** — an accepted
  NeurIPS 2026 paper does not qualify. Teams where no author qualifies are exempt but capped at one
  submission. AC/SAC/chair service exempts an author. Incorrect OpenReview profile information is
  grounds for desk rejection, and a submission with no registered reviewer among its authors is
  desk-rejected.
- Supplementary text should be a single file with the paper, marked as an appendix and placed after
  the references. Code may be uploaded as supplementary material and is encouraged; reviewers are
  encouraged but not required to read it.
- Dual submission: identical or substantially similar work under review or accepted elsewhere is
  prohibited for the whole review period. arXiv preprints and non-proceedings workshops do not
  violate it, and posting to arXiv during review is allowed.
- Withdrawal: before the paper deadline a withdrawn paper is deleted; after it, the paper stays
  publicly visible in OpenReview's withdrawn-papers section and is **de-anonymized immediately**.
  Submissions cannot be deleted or modified after the deadline.
- Every submitter needs an OpenReview profile. Profiles created without an institutional email go
  through moderation that can take **up to two weeks** — which makes profile creation a task for the
  weeks before the abstract deadline, not the day of it.
- Submissions affiliated with entities on the US OFAC sanctions list are desk-rejected.

## Organizers and fee model

- ICLR is run by the International Conference on Learning Representations organization. Like every
  CS/AI conference in this collection, it has NO standing editor-in-chief; the rotating analogue is
  the per-edition General Chair, Senior Program Chair, and Program Chairs.
- **No ICLR 2027 committee roster is recorded here.** 待核实 — `iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/Committees`
  returns 404 at this access date, so no official page names them. The ICLR 2026 committee was
  General Chair Carl Vondrick (Columbia), Senior Program Chair Bharath Hariharan (Cornell), and
  Program Chairs Colin Raffel, Lerrel Pinto, Diyi Yang and Aleksandra Faust; those appointments rotate
  every cycle and must not be carried forward. Program chairs are reachable at program-chairs@iclr.cc.
- There is NO article-processing charge (APC). ICLR funds the conference through registration fees;
  at least one author must register to present. Accepted papers are published open-access on
  OpenReview.
