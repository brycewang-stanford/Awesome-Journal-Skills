# DAC Camera-Ready

Use this after an accept decision to produce the archival version. For DAC 2027 the copyright
holder is **IEEE**: authors complete the **IEEE Copyright Form** (emailed to the contact author in
March), confirm the manuscript by 22 March 2027 and submit the proceedings manuscript by **31 March
2027**, after the 8 March 2027 notification. Publication in **IEEE Xplore and the ACM Digital
Library** is contingent on registering (one full Speaker Registration per paper, no student rate,
by 10 April 2027) and presenting at the poster session (source: `resources/official-source-map.md`).
DAC 2026 was an ACM-copyright year — do not reuse its ACM rights block. The camera-ready is a controlled edit of the accepted paper, not a rewrite —
scope, claims, and QoR numbers are fixed by what was reviewed.

## De-anonymize systematically

The review version was double-blind; the camera-ready restores identity. Do it as a checklist so
nothing is missed and nothing new leaks in:

- **Author block** — full author list, affiliations, and contact, in the order and form you want
  cited for years.
- **Self-citations** — convert third-person references to your own prior work back to first person
  where natural ("in our earlier work [7]"), now that identity is public.
- **Acknowledgements and funding** — restore the acknowledgements and **grant numbers** you stripped
  for review; funders often require exact wording.
- **Tool/repository names** — restore the real tool name and any public artifact/OpenROAD-flow link
  you anonymized.

## IEEE copyright form and notice

- Complete the **IEEE Copyright Form** when it arrives (one per accepted paper); questions go to
  IEEE, and authors are bound by the IEEE Publications Policies.
- Apply whatever copyright notice and final-version instructions the DAC 2027 proceedings
  instructions specify for the IEEE template (exact wording **待核实** until they are published);
  a missing or wrong notice is a common production bounce.
- Confirm the **conference/proceedings string** for the 64th DAC so the citation and DOI resolve
  correctly in IEEE Xplore and ACM DL.

## The page budget still binds

- The final version keeps the **6-page body + 1 references-only page** budget; de-anonymization adds
  the author block and acknowledgements, which can push the body over — reclaim space editorially,
  do **not** shrink fonts or alter the template.
- The seventh page remains **references only**; acknowledgements belong in the body, not on the
  reference page.
- Re-check every figure is legible at final column width after any reflow.

## Archival metadata (IEEE Xplore and ACM DL)

- **Title and author metadata** must match the PDF exactly — mismatches break indexing and citation.
- Supply **keywords / index terms** if the template or portal requests them; they drive
  discoverability. (ACM CCS concepts belonged to the ACM-template years such as DAC 2026.)
- **Authorship** follows IEEE's criteria (significant intellectual contribution, drafting or
  revising, approval of the final version); contributors who do not meet all three go in the
  Acknowledgment section.
- Keep the **"Content Generated by AI"** section if LLM-generated content is in the paper.
- Verify **references resolve** (DOIs where available) — the archival version is what the field will
  cite.
- If your paper is a Best-Paper candidate or in a special session, confirm any additional
  presentation or final-version requirements the track adds.
- Plan the **in-person** talk (draft slides 15 April, final slides 21 May 2027; international
  authors name an alternate presenter) and the required **poster**.

## Production checklist

```text
[De-anon]     authors/affiliations/acks/funding restored; tool + artifact links de-anonymized
[Rights]      IEEE Copyright Form submitted; any required notice on page 1
[Template]    IEEE double-column; 6+1 budget still met after reflow
[Figures]     legible at final size; grayscale/color-blind safe
[Metadata]    title/authors match PDF; keywords supplied; references resolve
[Fonts]       all fonts embedded; PDF passes the portal's readability check
[Deadline]    final manuscript + copyright by the end-of-March proceedings cutoff; registration by April 10
```

## Reverify each cycle

- The exact proceedings/copyright deadline and which sponsor's template is required (DAC 2027:
  IEEE, 31 March 2027; DAC 2026: ACM, 14 April 2026).
- The publication outlets (DAC 2027: IEEE Xplore and ACM DL, contingent on registration and
  poster-session presentation).
- The AI-content disclosure rule (DAC 2027: "Content Generated by AI" section if used).

## Output format

```text
[Camera-ready status] ready / blocked
[De-anonymization]    authors/acks/funding/tool links restored? yes/no
[Rights]              IEEE Copyright Form done? notice applied? yes/no
[Budget]              6-page body + references-only page still met? yes/no

[Registration]        full Speaker Registration by April 10? presenter + poster planned? yes/no
[Fix queue]           <ordered items before the March 31 proceedings deadline>
```
