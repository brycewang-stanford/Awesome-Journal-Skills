# External Tools - ATC (ACM SIGOPS Annual Technical Conference)

Access date: 2026-07-09; links moved to ATC 2027 and re-verified 2026-11-24 by direct fetch.

Use these surfaces after reopening the current ATC cycle pages. **USENIX ATC is retired** (USENIX
ATC '25, July 2025, was the last USENIX edition); the conference continues as the **ACM SIGOPS
Annual Technical Conference**, and it keeps re-deciding its mechanics: ATC 2026 (Hong Kong) used a
two-page extended abstract and a June deadline, while ATC 2027 (Rennes) reviews the first two pages
in round one and closes on 19 January 2027. In July 2026 the `sigops.org`/`usenix.org` gateway
blocked direct fetches (read via search renderings); on 2026-11-24 both answered directly. Do not
confuse this ATC with the *American Transplant Congress* or an *Academic Tax Conference*.

## Author-side checks

- HotCRP profile, conflicts, coauthor emails, and topic tags on `atc27.hotcrp.com`; the author list
  cannot change after submission.
- **Author cap:** every author on at most **2** ATC 2027 submissions, whatever the length or track.
- **First two pages:** round one reads only them (reviewers may read on) — scope, motivation, idea,
  delta over prior work, related-work placement, and evaluation must all be visible there.
- Two-column format (178×229 mm text block, 10pt Times Roman on 12pt leading, A4/US letter,
  numbered pages, hyperlinked references), `\documentclass[sigplan,10pt]{acmart}`; page budget
  **≤12** long / **≤6** short excluding references but **including appendices**.
- Double-blind sweep: paper ID instead of names, anonymized system name if the real one is known,
  third-person self-citation, concurrent related submissions cited anonymously and emailed to the
  PC chairs. Operational Systems Track papers may identify the organization.
- Resubmission of a rejected paper: prior-submission information plus an anonymized change summary
  (about one page) on the form.
- Artifact-evaluation intent; the ATC 2027 artifact call (badges, dates) is 待核实.

## Do not infer

- Do not carry ATC 2026 mechanics into 2027: the extended abstract is gone, appendices now count
  toward the page limit, and a two-paper author cap is new.
- Do not assume USENIX's free-video / no-APC model persists; SIGOPS editions use ACM Open Access with
  an APC for authors outside ACM Open (the ATC CFP quotes no rate).
- If pages disagree, treat the newest SIGOPS call, the ATC HotCRP deadline page, or a direct chair
  announcement as controlling, and record the conflict.
