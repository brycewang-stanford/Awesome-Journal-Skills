## Verified facts from official pages

- [官] ET publishes original, rigorous theoretical econometrics across major areas of the field, including probabilistic and statistical foundations for modeling, estimation, prediction, and inference.
- [官] The journal welcomes theory connected to large-scale data, high-performance computing, artificial intelligence, probability, stochastic processes, statistics, mathematical finance, and computational economics.
- [官] Accepted article types listed by Cambridge include Articles, Miscellanea, and ET Interviews.
- [官] Miscellanea is for short papers with one or two key innovations, stated and proved concisely; Cambridge describes typical length as fewer than 20 pages and often around 15 pages.
- [官] Peer review is single-anonymous: reviewers know author identity, authors do not know reviewer identity. (Re-read on the author-instructions page 2026-11-24.)
- [官] Supplementary material can hold long derivations, supporting lemmas, tables, figures, and similar material when directed by the editor/coeditors; it should be clearly labeled and only include material that has been reviewed before acceptance.

## Re-verified 2026-11-24 (Cambridge Core "Preparing your materials", "Submitting your materials", "Fees and pricing", editorial board)

- [官] Editors-in-Chief Guggenberger / Su / Sun and Founding Editor (1985–2025) Phillips unchanged on the editorial-board page.
- [官] Regular Articles **should not exceed 50 pages, including the title page, tables, figures, references, and any appendices**; longer papers need prior approval from the journal manager (econometric.theory@cambridge.org). Miscellanea: typically fewer than 20 pages, often around 15.
- [官] Format for the entire manuscript: standard paper size, minimum **11**-point font, at least **1.5** line spacing, margins no less than **1.25** inches, pages numbered.
- [官] Title page: concise abstract of **no more than 200 words**, a **list of keywords**, complete affiliations. Accepted manuscripts add a running head of no more than **40** characters.
- [官] **Disclosure statements** expected before the references: a **competing-interest statement** (or a declaration that none exist) in the main manuscript file, and a **funding statement** (or a declaration of no specific funding).
- [官] **AI policy** (Cambridge): AI tools cannot be authors; text/image generation (including translation) and AI-assisted data analysis must be declared where used (acknowledgments/footnote, methods, or figure captions) with tool name, version, dates and description; minimal non-generative uses need no declaration.
- [官] **Style:** APA author-date.
- [官] **Supplementary material** is submitted at the same time as the main article so it is reviewed with it; the main paper, including all proofs, should be as self-contained as possible.
- [官] **Data and code:** simulations and empirical applications encouraged but not required; where present, authors provide as supplementary material a URL to an institutional replication website or public repository with all simulation code, data and programs — the editorial board may require it as a condition for publication; data-sharing restrictions are raised with the journal manager before submission.
- [官] **Revisions** normally within **6** months; after **12** months a revision is treated as a new submission.
- [官] **Fees:** the "Fees and pricing" page lists only the optional Gold OA APC (**£2,610 / US$3,655**); no submission fee is listed.
