## Verified Interspeech 2027 facts used in the skills

- Interspeech 2027 is the ISCA flagship conference, São Paulo, Brazil, 29 August – 2
  September 2027 — the first South American edition; theme "Speech, Society, and
  Minorities"; General Chairs Plinio A. Barbosa, Sandra Madureira and Christopher Shulby;
  Technical Program Chairs Khiet Truong, António Teixeira, Theodora Chaspari and Florian
  Metze (sources 1-4).
- Timeline (source 2): portal opens 19 January 2027; paper deadline 9 February 2027;
  paper-update deadline 16 February 2027; **rebuttal period 13–20 April 2027**; acceptance
  notification 2 June 2027; camera-ready 9 June 2027.
- Format (source 2): **regular papers up to 4 pages** in two columns **plus 2 pages for
  references and acknowledgments**; **Long Papers up to 8 pages plus 2**, targeted
  acceptance below 30%; English; double-blind review; the same work may not be submitted
  as both long and regular.
- Limits and desk rejections (source 2): at most **two papers per author as first author**;
  papers entirely developed or written by AI, papers citing non-existing references, and
  papers violating the format are desk-rejected.
- Every accepted paper (regular or long) must be presented **in person** by an author
  (source 2).
- Proceedings are published open access in the ISCA Archive (www.isca-archive.org) with
  DOIs under prefix 10.21437; there is no article-processing charge — the cost model is
  registration (source 10).
- ISCA confers three Best Student Paper awards per Interspeech and the annual ISCA Medal for
  Scientific Achievement; Interspeech has no standing editor-in-chief — Technical Program
  Chairs and organizing committees rotate per edition (sources 3, 12).
- Interspeech 2028: San Antonio, Texas, 3–8 September 2028 (source 11).
