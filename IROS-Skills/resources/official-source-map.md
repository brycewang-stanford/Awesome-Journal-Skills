This map records the official sources behind every IROS-specific fact in this pack.

Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) and the Robotics Society of Japan (RSJ) — so its
deadlines, page rules, review model, and video specs are reset per edition. Reopen the
current year-site (`<year>.ieee-iros.org`), its call-for-papers and important-dates
pages, and the PaperPlaza portal before giving submission-ready advice.

## Verified 2026-cycle facts used in the skills

- IROS 2026 is the 2026 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and
  Systems, in Pittsburgh, PA at the David L. Lawrence Convention Center, September 27 -
  October 1, 2026. IROS is the fall IEEE-RAS mega-venue, paired with ICRA in the spring.
- Submission runs on PaperPlaza (`ras.papercept.net`), the shared RAS portal; the initial
  PDF is capped at 6 MB, in English.
- **Page limit: up to 8 pages, US letter, including references and appendices.** IROS 2026
  replaced the old 6 + 2 model, and there is **no option to pay for additional pages**.
- The 2026 cycle review model is **double-anonymous** — author names and affiliations are
  redacted from the manuscript (the CFP suggests the LaTeX `censor` package), with the
  author list held in PaperPlaza metadata.
- The optional video attachment is **1 minute, up to 10 MB, MPG/MPEG/MP4**. Its deadline is
  stated two ways on the official pages: the Important Dates page and the CFP key-dates box
  say **March 7, 2026**, while the CFP's Video Supplement paragraph says upload "no later
  than March 5, 2026 (23:59 PST)". Treat the earlier date as binding when planning.
- Timetable: paper March 2, 2026 (edits but no new submissions through March 5); video
  March 7 (or March 5, as above); notification June 16, 2026; oral-format preference
  June 30; final paper **July 16, 2026**; author registration also July 16, 2026.
- Papers rejected from ICRA 2026 could be resubmitted in an "ICRA-IROS transfer" category
  with an anonymous Author Response File; they get a regular review. There is no rebuttal
  phase in the regular IROS review described by the CFP.
- Generative AI may not be an author; AI-generated content (text, figures, images, code)
  must be disclosed **in the acknowledgments section**, naming the AI system and the
  sections concerned; reviewers may not process manuscripts through AI.
- All accepted papers are presented in person (talk plus poster); unregistered authors'
  papers are removed from the program and the proceedings.
- IROS proceedings are published in IEEE Xplore; publication requires the IEEE electronic
  copyright form.
- IROS 2026 does not offer a combined "RA-L + IROS" submission option; instead, published
  RAM and non-evolutionary RA-L, T-ASE and T-RO papers are eligible for presentation at
  IROS during an eligibility window.
- Review runs through the RAS Associate-Editor / Editor structure inside PaperPlaza, under
  an **IROS Editor-in-Chief** (2026 organizing committee: Eiichi Yoshida) and the Senior
  Program Committee; the general and program chairs rotate each edition.

- **Whether double-anonymous review persists** for IROS 2027, and whether IROS's long
  single-blind tradition returns; the ML-conference reflex to trust either is a bug.
- **The exact final camera-ready page/format rules** (whether the 8-page cap is unchanged
  at camera-ready).
- **No-show policy wording beyond the 2026 contingency page**, and registration-to-paper
  ratios (the registration table lists paper uploads per category; read it before
  registering).
- **IROS 2027 CFP, deadlines, page limit and committee** — only the venue, dates and theme
  are published as of 2026-11-24.
- **Arxiv/preprint wording** — the 2026 CFP does not address preprints; read the RAS
  double-anonymous rules before posting.
