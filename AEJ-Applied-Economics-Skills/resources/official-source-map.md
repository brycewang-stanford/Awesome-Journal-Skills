pack. Sources were checked on **2026-06-20** against official American Economic
Association (AEA) pages and AEA article pages; the **editor and fee facts were

## Official AEA / AEJ: Applied pages

- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/app> — AEJ: Applied journal home. Verifies
  title, print ISSN 1945-7782, online ISSN 1945-7790, quarterly publication, and
  scope: empirical applied microeconomics including labor, development, health,
  education, demography, empirical corporate finance, empirical trade, and
  empirical behavioral economics.
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/app/editors> — AEJ: Applied editorial board.
  Re-verified 2026-11-23: Editor **Raymond Fisman** (Boston University);
  coeditors **Marika Cabral** (UT Austin), **Richard Hornbeck** (Chicago), **Peter
  Hull** (Brown), **Xavier Jaravel** (LSE), and **Namrata Kala** (MIT); Board of
  Editors roster; Data Editor **Lars Vilhuber**; Managing Editor Michelle
  DeBlasi; Assistant Managing Editor Justin Shapiro; past editors including
  Ben Olken (2020-2025) and past coeditors Leah Boustan (2022-2026) and Nicholas
  Ryan (2024-2026).
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/app/editorial-policy> — Peer-review and
  editorial policy. Verifies (re-read 2026-11-23; the page still says so) **single-blind** peer review; editor/coeditor
  assignment; return without referee review; second-coeditor consultation before
  first R&R; data-replication package requested at R&R; disclosure, AI, data
  legality, data/code, RCT registration, and transfer policy from AER/AER:
  Insights. Its AI section adds that reviewers must disclose any AI use in their
  cover letter.
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/app/submissions> — Submission guidelines.
  Verifies original-work/no-concurrent-submission rules, CrossRef Similarity
  Check, disclosure statements, AI disclosure, field-experiment RCT registration,
  submission fees by AEA membership and country income tier, publication fee,
  length recommendations, manuscript preparation, ScholarOne submission, AER/AER:
  Insights transfer option, Chicago Manual author-date preference, AEA templates
  in review mode, and random-order coauthor option. **Re-verified 2026-11-23:**
  "The AEA has launched a three-year randomized controlled trial of the effects of
  author-anonymized peer reviewing"; manuscripts **must be anonymized** — title and
  abstract at the top of the first page; author names, affiliations, contact
  information and the acknowledgement footnote in a separate PDF labelled "title
  page"; self-citations by name without "my/our"; no identifying locations, IRB
  approvals, personal-website links, or RCT-registration reference entries;
  supplementary appendices and response letters anonymized. An **abstract of 100 or
  fewer words is required**; PDF under 10 MB when reasonably possible; style guide
  optional at submission, required for accepted articles. Fees unchanged (members
  USD 200 / 100 / 0, nonmembers USD 300 / 200 / 0 by country income; 50 percent
  refund if rejected without review; publication fee USD 15 per typeset page for
  papers first submitted on or after 1 February 2024). Recommended length: average
  article 32-33 typeset pages; manuscripts strongly recommended not to exceed 40
  pages (11-point, 1.5 spacing) or 45 pages (12-point, 1.5 spacing), including
  figures, tables, references and appendices. A cover letter "is not required and
  should only be used to provide substantive information" (coeditor conflicts,
  data-access limitations). Each (co)author's **Disclosure Statement** PDF must be
  provided with the manuscript — submissions without them are incomplete and not
  reviewed. AI use in preparing the manuscript must be briefly described during
  submission. Field experiments supply the AEA RCT identification number.
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/news/rct-announcement> — AEA announcement, 10 September
  2026 (re-read 2026-11-23): three-year RCT of author-anonymized ("double-blind")
  reviewing at the AER, AER: Insights and the four AEJs, designed by Amy Finkelstein
  and J-PAL North America; treatment-arm reviewers receive no author-identifying
  information; launch expected fall 2026 (the submissions page says it has launched).
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/app/reviewers> — Reviewer guidelines.
  Verifies that reviewers remain anonymous to authors, AI-use disclosure and
  no-training-AI rules for reviewers, and ScholarOne use for review tracking
  (re-read 2026-11-23; the page no longer describes the review as single-blind).
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/app/style-guide> — AEJ: Applied style guide.
  Verifies title/byline placement at the top of the first page, acknowledgement
  footnote contents, 100-word abstract cap, section-heading format, author-date
  citations, table/figure rules, standard-error and significance-star practice,
  and appendix numbering. Re-read 2026-11-23: abstract "must not exceed 100
  words"; "Do not use asterisks to denote significance of estimation results.
  Report the standard errors in parentheses"; tables no more than 9 columns wide;
  footnotes, not endnotes; Chicago author-date citations.
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/app/accepted-article-guidelines> — Accepted
  article guidelines. Verifies final manuscript/native-file requirements,
  supplemental appendix rules, signed data/code documents, disclosure statements,
  publication agreement, and final data/code deposit expectations.
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/data/data-code-policy> — AEA Data and Code
  Availability Policy, February 2026 version (re-read 2026-11-23, still current). Verifies that papers with
  empirical, simulation, or experimental work must provide data, code, and
  computational details before acceptance; materials must be retained in a
  trusted repository such as the AEA Data and Code Repository; Data Availability
  Statement, raw/analysis data, code, README, unzipped deposit, and Data Editor
  compliance/reproducibility checks.
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/disclosure-policy> — AEA Disclosure
  Policy. Verifies funding-source disclosure, interested-party support threshold
  of at least USD 10,000 over the past three years (re-read 2026-11-23), relevant paid/unpaid
  positions, close-relative/partner disclosures, third-party review rights,
  disclosure PDF requirement, referee access to statements, publication of
  statements, and IRB disclosure.
- <https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/app.20220451> — AEJ: Applied
  article page for Harju, Jaeger, and Schoefer, "Voice at Work." Verifies the
  DOI `10.1257/app.20220451`, volume 17 issue 3 pages 271-309, and the `app`
  DOI stem rule used in the exemplars library.

## UNVERIFIED / 待核实

- **JEL codes at submission.** The 2026-06-20 map listed JEL codes as expected at
  submission; on 2026-11-23 none of the AEJ: Applied submissions, style-guide,
  accepted-article, or editorial-policy pages mentions JEL codes. The skills now call
  them customary rather than required; the ScholarOne form itself was not inspected.
- **How long the anonymized-submission rule lasts.** The RCT is announced as three
  years from fall 2026; whether the editorial-policy page's single-blind wording will
  be updated was not stated as of 2026-11-23.

  Data Editor in advice.
