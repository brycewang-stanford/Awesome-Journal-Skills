Access date: 2026-07-08 (first build). Submission rules, review process, calendar,
APC and policies **re-verified 2026-11-24** against the live CHI 2027 pages.

**Edition status, checked 2026-11-24.** The live edition is **CHI 2027**, the ACM CHI
Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems, **Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, USA,
May 10–14, 2027**. Its Papers cycle is in progress: full papers were due
**September 10, 2026**, reviews were released **November 5, 2026**, resubmissions are
due **December 3, 2026** and final notification is **December 17, 2026**, so this pack
continues to describe CHI 2027. The next edition (the 2028 conference) is announced on
`chi2028.acm.org` for **Cape Town, South Africa, May 8–12, 2028**; the site carries
only a volunteer call (open to November 27, 2026) — no Papers call, dates, chairs or
policies are posted yet, so nothing in this pack is projected onto the 2028 cycle.

This map records the official sources behind every CHI-specific fact in this pack.
CHI policy is re-posted per cycle by that year's Papers Chairs and Technical Program
Chairs; reopen the live `chi<year>.acm.org` pages, the SIGCHI author guides, and PCS
before giving submission-ready advice.

## Verified facts used across the skills

- CHI = the ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems, SIGCHI's
  flagship venue; one Papers cycle per year; submission and review run on PCS
  (Precision Conference Solutions), not OpenReview or CMT; publication runs through
  ACM TAPS into the ACM Digital Library.
- CHI 2026: April 13–17, 2026, Barcelona, Spain. CHI 2027: May 10–14, 2027,
  Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, USA. The 2028 conference: May 8–12, 2028, Cape Town, South Africa
  (announced only).
- CHI 2027 Papers: site opened 2026-08-13; due 2026-09-10 with videos and
  supplementary material on the same deadline; no abstract deadline; single-column
  ACM template for review; words-not-pages length regime (5,000–8,000 encouraged;
  <5,000 short papers; >12,000 desk-reject unless justified); abstract ≤150 words.
- Reviewer routing (new for 2027): descriptors instead of subcommittees; one AC per
  paper picks reviewers with matching support; Subcommunity Chairs oversee ACs;
  RRP requires four reviews per submission.
- Review: A/ARR/RR/RRX/X; DR and rubric-based ADR screening (ADR notices around
  October 25); round-1 groups minor revisions / major revisions / reject; 4-week
  highlighted revision + response letter; round 2 Accept or Reject.
- Camera-ready: eRights (2027-01-07), TAPS source upload, then a PCS
  publication-ready package; mandatory author-supplied figure descriptions with
  TAPS-added accessibility tagging; optional 30-second video preview; ACM open
  access as of 2026-01-01; CHI 2027 APC $500 member / $750 non-member.
- Registration: at least one author per accepted paper by 2027-03-04, or the paper is
  withdrawn from the DL; remote presentation by uploaded video is allowed.
