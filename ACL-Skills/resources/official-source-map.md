This map records the official sources behind every ACL-specific fact in this pack.
ACL runs on ACL Rolling Review (ARR), and both ARR mechanics and per-edition
conference rules change between cycles — reopen the current ARR CFP, the cycle
calendar, and the target conference's calls before giving deadline-sensitive advice.

**Edition status, checked 2026-11-23.** ACL 2026 has met (San Diego, July 2-7, 2026), so
every 2026 figure below is a historical anchor. The live edition is **ACL 2027**, the 65th
Annual Meeting, **Kyoto, Japan, August 17-22, 2027** (tutorials August 17, workshops
August 18-19, main conference August 20-22). Its **main-conference call is now published**
(first call posted September 29, 2026): the route in is the **ARR January 2027 cycle, ARR
submission deadline January 4, 2027** (11:59 pm UTC-12), and papers reviewed in that cycle
*or an earlier one* may be committed. The ACL 2027 commitment deadline, notification date
and camera-ready deadline are still **TBA** on the official site. The ARR calendar itself
still lists ACL 2027's final submission only as "January, 2027" with a blank commitment
column, and publishes no 2027 cycle rows yet — so the January cycle's review, response
and meta-review dates are not yet knowable. An author asking today which deadline gets
them into ACL 2027 is asking about January 4, 2027 — not about anything on the 2026
calendar, whose last slot (October 12, 2026) committed to NAACL and COLING 2027.

## The ARR calendar and what each cycle commits to (re-verified 2026-11-23)

ARR is a review pipeline, not a conference deadline. A paper is submitted to a *cycle*,
gets reviews, and is then **committed** to a venue whose own program committee decides.
So "the ACL deadline" is really two dates in different months, and which conference a
cycle can reach is fixed by ARR, not chosen by the author.

| 2026 cycle | Submission | Reviewer registration | Reviews due | Author response | Meta-reviews | Cycle end |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| March 2026 | March 16 | March 18 | April 20 | April 28 - May 4 | May 21 | May 24 |
| May 2026 | May 25 | May 27 | July 2 | July 8 - 14 | July 30 | August 2 |
| August 2026 | August 3 | August 5 | September 7 | September 14 - 24 | October 8 | October 11 |
| October 2026 | October 12 | October 14 | November 16 | November 24 - 30 | December 17 | December 20 |

| Venue | Final ARR submission | Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| EMNLP 2026 · AACL 2026 | May 25, 2026 | August 2, 2026 |
| EACL 2027 | August 3, 2026 | October 11, 2026 |
| NAACL 2027 · COLING 2027 | October 12, 2026 | December 23, 2026 |
| **ACL 2027** | **January 2027** (January 4, 2027 per the ACL 2027 call) | not yet posted (待核实) |

Read together, the two tables answer the question an author actually has. As of
2026-11-23 the October cycle is in its author-response window (November 24-30), with
meta-reviews on December 17 and commitment to NAACL or COLING 2027 by December 23.
**Nothing on the 2026 calendar reaches ACL 2027 as a first submission**; that route is
the January 2027 cycle (submission January 4, 2027), whose internal dates ARR has not yet
published. Because the ACL 2027 call accepts packages from "the January 2027 cycle or an
earlier cycle", a paper already reviewed in an earlier cycle may also be committed to
ACL 2027 once its commitment form opens.

## Verified facts used in the skills

- **ACL 2027 is the 65th Annual Meeting**, in **Kyoto, Japan, August 17-22, 2027**
  (tutorials August 17, workshops August 18-19, main conference August 20-22).
  General Chair **Yusuke Miyao** (University of Tokyo); Program Chairs **David Adelani**
  (McGill), **Arianna Bisazza** (Groningen), **Jan Buys** (Cape Town), **Ximena
  Gutierrez-Vasques** (UNAM), **David Schlangen** (Potsdam).
- **ACL 2027 main-conference call** (re-verified 2026-11-23): ARR submission deadline
  **January 4, 2027**; packages from the January 2027 cycle or earlier may be committed;
  commitment, notification and camera-ready dates TBA; long **8** / short **4** pages,
  **one extra page at camera-ready** (9 / 5); double-blind, reviews not public; author
  list frozen at commitment; dual submissions not allowed.
- **ACL 2027 theme track: "Homogenization and knowledge collapse in LLMs"** — output
  diversity, cultural flattening and knowledge collapse (metrics; causes in training
  data, alignment and decoding; exploration vs. convergence; human vs. model diversity;
  cross-lingual homogenization; ideological polarization). A special session and a
  Thematic Paper Award are anticipated.
- **Presentation (ACL 2027):** accepted papers must be presented to appear in the
  proceedings, with in-person and virtual options; papers without at least one
  registered presenting author by the early-registration deadline may be rejected.
  Papers not selected for the main conference are automatically considered for
  **Findings of ACL**.
- ACL 2026 was the **64th Annual Meeting**, held **July 2-7, 2026 in San Diego,
  California** (hybrid). Historical anchor.
- ACL 2026 reviewing ran through **ARR**, with submissions accepted from the
  **October 2025 and January 2026 cycles**; **commitment deadline March 14, 2026**,
  reviews/meta-reviews to authors by ~March 9-10, **notification April 4, 2026**,
  **camera-ready April 19, 2026**. Final decisions were made by the conference's
  senior area chairs and program chairs, on OpenReview.
- ARR January 2026 cycle submission deadline: **January 5, 2026**; all ARR
  deadlines are 11:59 pm UTC-12 (AoE). ARR moved to **10-week cycles** in May 2025.
- Paper formats: **long = 8 content pages, short = 4**, unlimited references,
  required Limitations section and optional ethics statement outside the count,
  appendices after the references and outside the count;
  accepted papers get **one extra content page** (9/5).
- The **Responsible NLP checklist** is mandatory; incorrect/incomplete/misleading
  answers are desk-rejection grounds. Resubmissions must link the prior submission
  and include a change summary with point-by-point responses.
- **AI assistance** (re-verified 2026-11-23): generative AI cannot be an author; use for
  writing or coding, and its scope, must be disclosed in the Responsible NLP checklist
  (section E) with details in the Acknowledgements; proofreading-only tools (grammar or
  spell checkers) need no disclosure; prompt injections aimed at AI readers may lead to
  desk rejection.
- **Sustainable reviewing policy** (re-verified 2026-11-23; replaces the August 2026
  all-authors reviewing obligation from the October 2026 cycle on). A submission is
  guaranteed review only if it names a **qualified service contributor**, who may serve
  on at most **2** submissions and must register within **48 hours** after the submission
  deadline. Submissions without one enter a **lottery** for spare capacity; those not
  drawn are desk rejected. Per cycle, an author may appear on at most **20** submissions,
  and at most **5** as first or joint-first author. A contributor who fails to deliver
  gets every submission they serve on or co-author desk rejected and may be temporarily
  disqualified from ARR service. All co-authors need full OpenReview profiles (with
  ORCID), completed at most 1 week after the submission deadline.
- **Concurrent submissions and text re-use** (re-verified 2026-11-23): concurrent related
  submissions with overlapping authors must cite each other, explain the differences in
  related work, and be uploaded anonymized in the "concurrent submissions" field; re-used
  text from the authors' other publications is capped at **10% of total tokens**.
- The **anonymity period was abolished 2024-01-12**; submissions stay anonymized,
  preprints are permitted, and the binding "no non-anonymous preprint" option (no
  preprint until meta-reviews are released; breaking it is a desk reject) earns
  award/priority incentives.
- **Findings of ACL** is a real archival acceptance tier published in the ACL
  Anthology; the **Anthology** is open access, CC BY 4.0 for post-2016 materials,
  with no author fees (ACL's cost model is conference registration).
- ACL 2026 ran a special **theme track on explainability** with a Thematic Paper
  Award, plus separate industry, demo, and student-research-workshop tracks with
  their own deadlines (industry: submissions Feb 14, notification Apr 12, 2026).

## Cycle-volatile by construction

Cycle dates and count per year, commitment windows per conference, checklist
wording, review-form structure, reviewing-obligation rules, preprint-incentive details,
page policies, and theme tracks all change between editions. The 2026 facts above are
historical anchors, not standing rules.
