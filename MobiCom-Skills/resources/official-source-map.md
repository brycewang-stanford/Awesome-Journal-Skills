## Verified facts used in the skills (MobiCom 2027, re-verified 2026-11-24)

- MobiCom 2027 is the **33rd** ACM International Conference on Mobile Computing and
  Networking: **October 18-22, 2027, Athens, Greece** (source 1).
- MobiCom is the **ACM SIGMOBILE** flagship for mobile computing and wireless networking and
  has run a **multi-deadline model** since its 25th edition (2019) (sources 1, 4).
- MobiCom 2027 **summer round**: abstract registration **August 26, 2026**, paper submission
  **September 2, 2026** (both **23:59 AoE**; HotCRP printed them as 7:59:59 AM EDT the next
  day), early-reject notification **October 23, 2026**, rebuttal period **November 3-6,
  2026**, acceptance notification **November 19, 2026** (sources 1, 2, 3). **Winter round:
  TBD** on 2026-11-24.
- **No paper categories:** MobiCom 2027 has no separate challenge, experience, or verification
  categories; all submissions are regular papers under one set of criteria (originality,
  technical soundness, significance, rigor of evaluation, impact) (source 2).
- **Review:** two rounds per deadline; papers not advancing after round 1 get an **early
  notification with reviews**; round-2 reviews are released for an **optional rebuttal of at
  most 500 words**, entered in the submission form and limited to correcting factual errors or
  answering reviewer questions — **no new experiments, data, or figures**, and no promises of
  future work; more reviews may arrive after the rebuttal (source 2).
- **Decisions:** **accept** (initially conditional, final after anonymous shepherding that can
  last **4-6 weeks** and may require new experiments); **one-shot revision** (at most **three**
  major changes, resubmitted at the next deadline, may take more than 4-6 weeks; the paper stays
  under review at MobiCom meanwhile); **reject** (no resubmission to MobiCom until **11 months**
  after the last MobiCom deadline it was submitted to) (source 2).
- **Reciprocal reviewing (new):** each submission designates one senior **reviewing author**
  who reviews up to **three** submissions in the same round (source 2).
- **Registration abstract:** a meaningful title and an abstract of **at least 100 words**;
  "TBD" placeholders are desk-rejected; no deadline extensions; all authors declared at
  submission (source 2).
- **Format:** at most **12** single-spaced, numbered pages including figures, tables and all
  other non-bibliographic material — content beyond 12 pages is not reviewed; references extra;
  appendices after the references do not count, must start on a new page with lettered section
  numbers, and reviewers are not required to read them; font no smaller than **10** points; two
  columns 3.33 x 9.25 inches, 0.33-inch gap, at most **55** lines per column; US letter; PDF
  only; **under 15 MB**; LaTeX `\documentclass[sigconf,10pt]{acmart}`; no author names and **no
  embedded hyperlinks** in the PDF (source 2).
- **Double-blind:** names, affiliations and funding acknowledgments removed; prior work cited in
  the third person, never "reference deleted"; anonymized links to code allowed but reviewers
  need not follow them; arXiv/technical reports are not prior publication but the submission
  must not refer to them, and advertising the preprint on social media or in the press is
  strongly discouraged (source 2).
- **Previous submissions:** a one-shot revision, or a paper rejected by MobiCom within the last
  12 months, must include an anonymized summary of the prior reviewers' major concerns and how
  they were addressed; for rejects elsewhere it is encouraged (source 2).
- **Policies:** ACM Policy on Authorship (including AI use); ACM policy on research involving
  human participants and subjects; ORCID iDs for all authors; no simultaneous submissions;
  workshop/poster/demo extensions with an anonymous self-citation and a difference statement
  (source 2).
- **Accepted papers:** a **90-second teaser video** with the camera-ready; at least one author
  registers and attends to present; a TPC-chosen subset gets full talks, the rest lightning
  talks plus posters, all deemed equal; all accepted papers are considered for the Best Paper
  Award (source 2).
- **Open access and APC:** ACM publications are 100% open access; authors at institutions not
  in ACM Open pay an APC unless waived, at subsidized **2027** rates of **$500** for ACM/SIG
  members and **$750** for non-members (source 2).
- **Artifact evaluation (2027 call):** open to accepted and conditionally accepted papers;
  three independent ACM badges — Artifacts Available, Artifacts Evaluated - Functional, Results
  Reproduced; summer round artifacts due **January 17, 2027** (notification February 17,
  2027), winter round **July 24, 2027** (notification August 24, 2027); best-effort double-blind;
  artifact description at most three pages (source 5).
- **Chairs:** General Co-Chairs Dimitrios Koutsonikolas and Symeon Papavassiliou; TPC Co-Chairs
  Joerg Widmer and Nirupam Roy (source 6).
- The **SIGMOBILE Test-of-Time Paper Award** recognizes impact over at least a decade (2026
  awardees: the MobiCom 2003 interference paper and the MobiCom 2015 power-delay-profiling paper)
  (source 7).
