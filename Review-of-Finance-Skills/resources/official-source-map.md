## Official journal & society pages

- **https://academic.oup.com/rof/pages/General_Instructions** — Author Guidelines / General Instructions (Oxford Academic; search-engine rendering 2026-11-24). Verifies:
  - Submission via **Editorial Express**.
  - Manuscript must **not exceed 60 pages total, including appendices, bibliography, figures, and tables**.
  - **Abstract must not exceed 150 words.**
  - Formatting: **at least 12-point font, at least 1.5 line spacing, 1-inch side margins, 1.5-inch top/bottom margins; submitted as a PDF with the first page numbered page 1.**
  - **Chicago** citation/reference style.
  - **Double-blind anonymous** peer review (no author-identifying references).
  - Code provided as a **condition of publication**.
  - **Fast-Track**: editorial decision within **14 days**.
- **https://revfin.org/for-authors/manuscript-guidelines/** — Submit a Paper (revfin.org; **read directly 2026-11-24**). Verifies, for all manuscripts: font **at least 12 points**, **at least 1.5** line spacing, **1-inch** side and **1.5-inch** top/bottom margins; **abstract must not exceed 150 words**; title page numbered **page 1**; **PDF** file; **no references to the author(s)** by name, institution, or other distinguishing characteristics (double-blind); **must not exceed 60 pages including appendices, bibliographies, figures, and tables**, with an Internet Appendix that does not count toward the limit but a paper that must be self-contained (60 pages is "an extreme upper limit, not a target"); **"No cover letter is typically necessary"** — cover letters only for unusual circumstances (e.g., potentially conflicted referees), and not to explain the contribution; optional editor suggestion via a drop-down, subject to the conflict-of-interest policy; submission is not complete until the post-payment confirmation step.
- **https://revfin.org/formatting-guidelines/** — Formatting Guidelines (read directly 2026-11-24). Verifies: **any format for sections and bibliographies at initial submission**; the RoF house bibliography/citation format (e.g., "Graham, J. R., Harvey, C. R., and Rajgopal, S. (2005) ...") applies **after conditional acceptance**.
- **https://editorialexpress.com/rof** — RoF submission portal (Editorial Express). Verifies: submission system is Editorial Express.
- **https://revfin.org/** — Review of Finance journal site (European Finance Association). Verifies: RoF is the **official journal of the EFA**; published by **Oxford University Press** on behalf of the EFA; sidebar **statistics for July 2025 to June 2026**: acceptance rate **7.18%**, Fast-Track acceptance rate **3.77%**, median turnaround **30 days**, mean turnaround **34 days** (read 2026-11-24).
- **https://revfin.org/submission-fees-categories/** — Submission fees and categories (**re-verified 2026-11-24**). Verifies:
  - **Regular submission fee EUR 350 (EUR 300 for EFA members for the current year)**; regular resubmission same fees. Unchanged.
  - **Fast-Track first submission EUR 900; Fast-Track resubmission EUR 500.** Unchanged.
  - **Refund schedule — CHANGED (effective 1 November 2026).** A desk rejection *with comments* is **not refunded**; a desk rejection *without comments* is refunded **EUR 250** (regular), **EUR 200** (regular, EFA member), **EUR 800** (Fast-Track), **EUR 400** (Fast-Track resubmission). Decision over 14 days: **EUR 550** (Fast-Track) / **EUR 150** (Fast-Track resubmission). Decision over 120 days: **EUR 175** (regular) / **EUR 150** (EFA member, incl. member resubmission) / **EUR 725** (Fast-Track); none for non-member regular or Fast-Track resubmissions. (Previous schedule, superseded: regular desk rejection EUR 150 / EUR 100 members; Fast-Track desk rejection EUR 600.)
  - The refund clock starts at editor assignment or confirmed full payment, whichever is later; EU authors must supply VAT information.
- **https://revfin.org/editorial-policy/** — Editorial policy (re-verified 2026-11-24). Verifies:
  - **Two-round decision philosophy**: the journal aims to reach a final decision by the second round and will likely ignore new round-two referee concerns not raised in round one (and unrelated to changes in the paper).
  - Referees asked to apply **top-three-finance-journal standards** and to separate comments into (1) **major concerns required for publication** and (2) **suggestions**.
  - **Screen Reports** from trusted referees/Associate Editors inform desk-rejection decisions. **From 1 November 2026** a desk rejection is either *summarily rejected with comment* (substantive comments; **fee not refunded**) or *summarily rejected without comment* (one-to-two-sentence explanation; **RoF retains EUR 100** and refunds the rest), for all submission types.
  - Authors (not referees) are framed as the **owners of the paper**; the **accepting editor and submission/acceptance dates are published** with the paper.
  - **2-year submission ban** for resubmitting a previously rejected paper without cover-letter disclosure, for simultaneous submission elsewhere, and (in clear-cut cases) for **knowingly failing to cite relevant work** — the citation ban applies to all authors of the offending submission.
  - AI tools such as ChatGPT are **not authors** (OUP authorship rules).
- **https://revfin.org/policy-on-the-use-of-large-language-models-llms-and-ai-tools/** — Policy on the use of LLMs and AI tools (**updated 30 October 2026**; read directly 2026-11-24). Verifies: AI tools may play a supportive role (language, coding, data handling) but **any use must be disclosed in the cover letter and acknowledged in the manuscript**; papers whose methods use LLM- or AI-agent-generated inputs/outputs must report **model name, version, and access dates** at submission, and at acceptance include prompts, parameters, and raw outputs in the replication package; **hidden text, altered encodings, or embedded instructions** aimed at automated systems → rejection **without refund** and a **one-year** suspension of submission privileges; the policy supplements and prevails over OUP's AI policies; referees may not substitute an LLM for their own evaluation.
- **https://revfin.org/for-authors/first-choice-policy/** — First Choice policy (available from 15 April 2024; read 2026-11-24). Verifies: papers not yet reviewed by any finance journal may opt for a streamlined process with only two outcomes (conditional acceptance or rejection); confirm in the cover letter; not eligible for Fast-Track; regular fee applies; misuse → 2-year ban.
- **https://revfin.org/code-sharing-policy/** — Code Sharing and Data Availability Policy. Verifies:
  - Applies to all papers **first submitted on or after 1 January 2022**.
  - Authors of empirical/simulation/experimental work must provide **replication programs** (with software details and comments), the **actual data if non-proprietary** (or a **pseudo-dataset plus log files** if proprietary), a **Data Availability Statement**, and **formatted dataset citations**.
  - **Condition of publication**: accepted papers are published online only **after the code has been received**, unless an **exception is requested in the cover letter at initial submission**.
  - Replication packages hosted on the **Oxford University Press website as supplementary material** (no separate named archive).
- **https://revfin.org/faq/** — FAQ. Verifies: **2-year ban** for non-disclosed resubmission or simultaneous submission; **author-responsibility citation norm** (lack of awareness of a relevant paper is not a valid defense; omissions can be sanctioned); referees apply top-three-finance-journal standards.
