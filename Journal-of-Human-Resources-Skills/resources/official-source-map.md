## Official journal pages (UW Press / IRP)

- **https://jhr.uwpress.org/content/authors** — Authors / submission instructions (re-verified 2026-11-24, unchanged). Verifies:
  - Submission via **http://jhr.msubmit.net**; authors must **NOT** send submissions directly to the editor, coeditors, managing editor, or editorial office.
  - **USD $175 nonrefundable submission fee** for all initial submissions; **no fee** for invited revisions; economic-hardship exemption may be requested; fee does **not** guarantee outside review and is **not refunded** for out-of-scope (management/personnel) papers.
  - Length is **page-based, not word-based**: **maximum 40 pages** including references, tables, and figures (12-point font, 1.5-line spacing for body text, 1-inch margins); **up to 50 pages** if double-spaced; footnotes may use smaller type and single spacing; an **Online Appendix** holds overflow (online-only) but should not be excessive.
  - **Per-author Disclosure Statement** required at submission (funding/support totaling >= USD $10,000 over 3 years, positions, relatives/partners, right-to-review, IRB status), placed up front before the body; failure to disclose can reverse acceptance or trigger retraction.
  - **Author-order randomization** via a superscript "r" on the title page; formal name-change-on-published-papers policy via **ORCID**.
  - A **replication-policy exemption** must be requested **at the time of initial submission**, with reasons.
  - **First-round turnaround statistics** (posted on the page, read 2026-11-24): average time to decision **30.55 days**, median **9 days**, acceptance rate **4%**; "we reject more than half of all manuscripts without sending them to outside reviewers".

- **https://jhr.msubmit.net/cgi-bin/main.plex?form_type=display_ejp_tips** — Manuscript Preparation (msubmit.net; read directly 2026-11-24). Written for accepted final manuscripts ("For instructions on initial submission ... see the Author Instructions"). Verifies:
  - Required title-page elements: title; author names; **"Abstract of 100 words or fewer"**; affiliations and acknowledgments footnote (no acknowledgment of JHR editors/staff); **Disclosure Statement**; **Data Replication Statement** ("required for all papers upon submission"); **JEL codes**.
  - Citations are author-date ("Jones (1990)", "(Jones and Jackson 1990)", "Jones et al." for more than three authors); **no numbered references**; page ranges and heading capitalization follow **Chicago style**.
  - Final files: Word manuscript, matching PDF, original figure files, Online Appendix (must have been submitted during peer review), License to Publish.

- **https://journals.uwpress.wisc.edu/PDFs/JHR-Editorial-Policies.pdf** — Editorial Policies (dated September 2024). Verifies:
  - **Field focus**: empirical microeconomics — labor, development, health, education, discrimination, retirement; oriented toward research relevant to **public policy**; **will NOT consider management or personnel ("HR") research** despite the journal's name.
  - **Founded 1965**; published by **University of Wisconsin Press**; housed at the **UW-Madison Institute for Research on Poverty (IRP)**.
  - **Review model**: single-anonymous — "Referees are anonymous, but author names are not kept from reviewers"; managed by editor, coeditors, associate editors; **rapid desk-reject WITHOUT outside review** at the handling editor/coeditor's discretion for papers judged unlikely to survive.
  - **Reconciliation requirement**: manuscripts judged partly on whether they reconcile results with prior published work; authors may be required to run comparative estimation and present sensitivity tests.
  - **Review-recycling**: authors may submit a prior journal's decision letter and referee reports, which JHR may use (and may contact the prior journal's editor/referees) to speed review.
  - **Data & Replication policy** (effective Aug 1, 1989; latest revision **July 2022**): accepted manuscripts must preserve data and post it online in a well-curated **public repository** (examples: **ICPSR, the Dataverse Project, or an institutional repository**) in the **public domain under a CC0 1.0 Universal license**; an **archive plan** is required as a footnote at submission with a persistent (preferably **DOI**) link; complete replication materials required (data, code, models, other materials, plus a **read-me** file); a **Data Availability Statement** on the title page for all published papers; waiver requests must be made at initial submission and require (i) a method by which other researchers can obtain the data and (ii) author commitment to provide guidance; for **RCTs**, authors must state whether a **pre-analysis plan** exists, where it is registered, and any deviations.
  - **Submission fee stated as USD $150 in this Sept-2024 PDF** (see discrepancy note below).
  - **Page charges $60/page** apply to accepted papers **only if the author has grant income**; optional **gold open access (CC BY-NC-ND) costs USD $2,500**; preprints may be posted in institutional/funder repositories but **NOT on ResearchGate or Academia.edu** unless the paper is open access.

- **https://jhr.uwpress.org/content/editorial-board** — Editorial board (re-verified 2026-11-24). Verifies:
  - **Editor: Michael Lovenheim (Cornell University, ILR School / Jeb E. Brooks School of Public Policy)** since **2026-07-01**, succeeding Anna Aizer (Brown University). Re-verified 2026-09-14 via Cornell Chronicle and ILR announcement (see Editor transition section).
  - Coeditors include Abi Adams-Prassl, Aline Butikofer, Michael Darden, Jeffrey Denning, Taryn Dinkelman, Josh Goodman, Andrew Goodman-Bacon, Lena Hensvik, Ethan Lewis, Isaac Mbiti, Matthew Neidell, Maya Rossin-Slater, Judith Scott-Clayton, Emilia Simeonova, Kosali Simon, Sergio Urzua, Tara Watson, Junsen Zhang, Ben Zou.

## Discrepancies and 需复核 (to-be-verified) items

- **Submission fee $175 vs. $150 — partially reconciled.** The live Authors page states **$175** (accessed 2026-06-01); the September-2024 Editorial Policies PDF still states **$150**. This pack treats the live Authors-page **$175** as current and the PDF as not yet updated. Re-confirm the exact amount on the live page before paying.
- **Editor of record (re-verified 2026-09-14).** **Michael Lovenheim (Cornell, ILR School)** is the Editor of record since **2026-07-01** (transition completed). Anna Aizer (Brown) was the predecessor Editor. Cornell ILR + Cornell Chronicle announcements re-confirmed 2026-09-14, ≥2 sources. Re-check the live editorial-board page at time of use for coeditor changes or other roster updates.
- **Abstract length / JEL — partially resolved 2026-11-24.** The msubmit.net Manuscript Preparation page lists an abstract of **100 words or fewer** and **JEL codes** as required title-page elements, but that page is addressed to accepted final manuscripts; the Authors page and Editorial Policies PDF state no abstract limit or JEL requirement for initial submission. The pack advises drafting to 100 words with JEL codes from the start. **Keywords: still unspecified (需复核).**
- **Manuscript citation/reference style — resolved 2026-11-24.** The Manuscript Preparation page fixes author-date citations, no numbered references, and Chicago-style page ranges and capitalization; it does not give the style a single formal name.
- **Submission fee re-checked 2026-11-24:** the Authors page still says **$175** and the Editorial Policies PDF (still the September-2024 file) still says $150 — discrepancy unchanged.
- **Publication frequency — 需复核 (conflicting official sources).** The UW Press page returned "six times per year" in one fetch while the Editorial Policies PDF and About text say "quarterly" (four/year). The PDF (primary policy doc) says quarterly; frequency was not fully reconciled. Not used as a load-bearing fact.
- **Submission-platform vendor — 需复核.** Only the canonical URL **http://jhr.msubmit.net** is confirmed; the underlying vendor (e.g., Editorial Manager vs. another system) behind msubmit.net was not separately confirmed.

## Notes on scope discipline

- JHR's **name is misleading**: it is an **empirical microeconomics** journal and explicitly **does not** publish management or personnel ("HR management") research. The non-refundable fee is **not** returned for out-of-scope submissions. This is the single most consequential fit check in the pack and is sourced from the Editorial Policies PDF and Authors page above.
