## Verified 2026-cycle facts used in the skills

- UIST 2026 was the 39th Annual ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and
  Technology, held November 2-5, 2026 at the GM Renaissance Center in Detroit,
  Michigan, USA. The whole 2026 cycle is closed as of 2026-11-24; new Papers
  submissions target UIST 2027 (see Edition status above).
- The Papers track ran on PCS with abstracts due March 24, full papers due March 31 and
  materials (video, supplementary files, alt text, subtitles) due April 9, 2026 (all
  11:59 pm AoE). The author list had to be complete at the abstract deadline.
- The 2026 review pipeline: initial reviews May 28, rebuttals due June 5 (5,000
  characters, self-contained), decisions June 27, final reviews July 3, upload to PCS
  and TAPS July 31, final acceptance decision August 7, video preview August 14.
  Acceptances are conditional: final acceptance depends on delivering the changes
  promised in the rebuttal.
- Format: two-column `acmart` with `[sigconf,review,anonymous]` for review (or the
  two-column Word template); 10-page standard and 5-page short limits excluding
  references and appendices; acknowledgements count toward the limit; exceeding the
  limit is a desk reject; the camera-ready may grow by 10%.
- Files: paper PDF < 20 MB; video figure < 250 MB; all files together < 300 MB.
- Video figures are optional but highly encouraged: no more than 5 minutes, 1080p or 4K,
  MP4 encoded with H.264, subtitles as a separate .srt/.sbv file, and fully anonymized
  for review. Accepted papers also owe a mandatory 30-second video preview.
- Anonymization is "reasonable effort": strip author and institutional identity from
  the paper, the video, and all supplementary files; cite your own prior work in the
  third person with full references; attach an anonymized copy of any heavily
  overlapping prior submission as supplementary material.
- Generative AI: authors should avoid generating content with AI agents/LLMs; using
  them to edit and check the authors' own text is allowed (CFP and Author Guide).
- Open access: all ACM publications are open access from January 1, 2026; authors
  outside ACM Open institutions pay an APC unless waived, subsidised for 2026
  conferences at $250 (ACM/SIG members) / $350 (non-members).
- Camera-ready flows through ACM TAPS with accessible tagging (APTARA); alt text is
  required for every figure, subfigure, and table.
- Demos and Posters are separate adjunct tracks with their own chairs (2026 deadline
  July 10); the same work may not be submitted to both (a double submission is treated
  as a demo and dropped from Posters). Poster extended abstracts (2 pages) are archived
  in the ACM DL adjunct proceedings.
- UIST 2025 (38th edition) took place in Busan, Republic of Korea, September 28 -
  October 1, 2025; its main and adjunct proceedings are DOI 10.1145/3746059 and
  10.1145/3746058. UIST 2026's are 10.1145/3830398 and 10.1145/3830397.
- UIST has no standing editor-in-chief; general, program, and track chairs are
  appointed per edition and rotate (sources 4 and 11 list the 2026 and 2027 committees).
