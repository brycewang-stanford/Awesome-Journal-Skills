## Verified facts encoded in the pack

- **Field / scope:** information systems research across behavioral science, design science, economics, and organizational approaches to information technology. (#10, #12)
- **Publisher / sponsor:** published by the Regents of the University of Minnesota through the Management Information Systems Research Center (MISRC), Carlson School of Management; official journal of the Association for Information Systems (AIS representatives sit on the policy committee); quarterly issues in March, June, September, and December. (#7, #10)
- **Editor-in-Chief:** Susan A. (Sue) Brown, University of Arizona; term January 2024 - December 2028. (#7, #8, #9)
- **Submission system:** ScholarOne Manuscripts is the only route; separate MISQ registration; no fee to submit. Word and LaTeX manuscript templates are provided. (#5, #10)
- **Categories and page limits (re-verified 2026-11-24, CHANGED):** Research Article 55 pages; Research Note 30 pages; Methods Article 55 pages; Research Commentary 30 pages (invited by the EIC); Theory and Review 55-65 pages (Theory Development 55, Theory-Generative Research Synthesis 65); Issues and Opinions 30 pages. All maximum lengths include text, tables, figures, references, and appendices. (#5)
- **First page / keywords:** first manuscript page includes title, abstract, and keywords; keywords should be 5-10 specific terms. (#5)
- **Formatting / style:** Times New Roman 12pt, double-spaced, left-justified, 1-inch margins; references single-spaced with hanging indent, APA 7th edition; heading hierarchy on the guidelines page. (#5)
- **Review model:** double-anonymous review; editors know author identities but reviewers do not; Senior Editor and Associate Editor initial review followed by external reviewers; Senior Editor owns the decision. (#2, #10)
- **Publication policies:** no reject-and-resubmit decision; work already published or under review elsewhere cannot be submitted; related work must be disclosed at submission; submissions must comply with the AIS Code of Research Conduct. (#10)
- **Research transparency:** transparency commitment uploaded at submission (Step 2, Miscellaneous); materials should document design/data/analysis in a genre-appropriate form; final acceptance is contingent on senior-editor and transparency-editor review of those materials. (#4, #5, #10)
- **Preprints:** on acceptance, authors must remove all versions of the manuscript from personal and institutional websites. (#10)
- **Reciprocal service:** submitting authors agree to review up to three papers per year for the journal if invited. (#10)
- **Design-science genre:** MISQ is a canonical venue for design-science research, anchored by the 2004 Hevner et al. guidelines. Design science is a research tradition, not a separate length category. (#11, #12)
