## Official submission & policy pages (APA)

- **https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/apl** — Journal of Applied Psychology home (APA). Verifies:
  publisher is the **American Psychological Association (APA)**; **ISSN 0021-9010 / e-ISSN 1939-1854**;
  scope is **original investigations in applied psychology in work and organizational settings** —
  cognitive, motivational, affective, and behavioral phenomena at individual, group, organizational,
  and cultural levels across business, education, health, government, and military contexts; article
  types include **empirical investigations, meta-analyses, theory-development articles, integrative
  conceptual reviews, and qualitative research**; submission via **Editorial Manager**
  (`www.editorialmanager.com/apl/`). **Editor-in-Chief Mo Wang** (University of Florida), who **began
  the editorship in January 2026**, succeeding outgoing Editor **Lillian T. Eby** (web-verified
  2026-06-22 via UF Warrington announcement, APA new-editor-appointments page, and Wikipedia; re-verify
  before relying).
- **https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/apl** (Submission Guidelines section) — Verifies: manuscripts
  prepared per the **Publication Manual of the APA, 7th edition**; **masked (anonymized) review** with
  author identities withheld throughout consideration; completion of the **APA-published submission
  checklist** is required at upload and signifies agreement to follow the guidelines; full submissions
  enter as **Feature Articles** with length **commensurate with contribution**, and authors may be asked
  to reduce a manuscript to a **Research Report** during review; **proposals** (where applicable) are
  prepared in APA style and limited to **1,000 words** (exclusive of title, references, figures).
  (Superseded 2026-11-24: the current guidelines no longer mention a 1,000-word proposal; Integrative
  Conceptual Reviews start with a **10-page prospectus**, and Research Reports are capped at 19
  manuscript pages — see the `/apl/submit` entry below.)
- **https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/apl/submit** — current JAP Submission Guidelines (the
  submission-guidelines tab moved here), read 2026-11-24. Verifies: submit to the editorial office
  of **Mo Wang** in **Word (.docx) or LaTeX (.tex) as a ZIP with a PDF**; the **submission checklist**
  is required at upload; **APA Publication Manual, 7th edition**; three primary article types —
  **Feature Articles**, **Research Reports** "limited to no more than **19 manuscript pages**
  (excluding title page, abstract, references, tables, and figures)", counting **25 lines** of 12-point
  text with 1-inch margins as one page (request Research Report status in the cover letter), and
  **Integrative Conceptual Reviews**, which start with a **10-page prospectus**; occasional Monographs
  and Commentaries; replications ("A Replication of XX Study"); **"The journal will accept
  submissions in masked review format only"** — names/affiliations in the cover letter, unmasked
  manuscripts returned; a **data transparency appendix** (last page of the manuscript) on prior,
  concurrent and planned uses of the data; TOP levels — **Citation Level 2; Data, Analytic Methods
  (Code) and Research Materials Transparency Level 1 (Disclosure); Design and Analysis Transparency
  Level 2 (JAP methods checklist + JARS); Study Preregistration and Analysis Plan Preregistration
  Level 2** (masked preregistration available at submission); a one-paragraph Method subsection titled
  **"Transparency and openness"**; submissions without data must state why the TOP data/code/
  preregistration items do not apply; **abstract maximum of 250 words** on a separate page and **up to
  five (5) keywords**; not following the methods checklist "can lead to desk rejection"; APA-wide:
  generative-AI use disclosed in the method section and cited, AI not an author, conflicts of interest
  revealed, **submission is free of charge** (APC only for gold open access), preprints allowed.
- **https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/apl/editorial-board** — editorial board, read 2026-11-24:
  **"Incoming (2027) editor (handling all new submissions in 2026)": Mo Wang, PhD, University of
  Florida**; **outgoing editor Lillian T. Eby, PhD, University of Georgia** ("handling invited
  revisions only in 2026").
- **https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/features/apl-manuscript-checklist.pdf** — Manuscript Preparation
  Instructions / submission checklist (APA). Verifies: required preparation and masking steps; the

- **https://www.apa.org/pubs/authors/data-availability-statement** — APA data-availability statement

## Open science (TOP framework)

- Effective **November 1, 2021**, empirical research **including meta-analyses** submitted to JAP must,
  **at a minimum, adhere to the TOP (Transparency and Openness Promotion) Levels** — data, materials,
  and analysis-code availability with persistent identifiers, a data-availability statement, and
  preregistration weighed in evaluation. Confirm the **current TOP level** and exact statement wording
  on the official APA author page.
- **https://www.cos.io/initiatives/top-guidelines** — Center for Open Science, TOP Guidelines.
  Background on the TOP framework that APA journals (including JAP) adopt.

## Owner, publisher, history

- **https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Applied_Psychology** — Verifies: JAP is a peer-reviewed
  academic journal published by the **American Psychological Association**, founded **1917**, and a
  leading outlet in **industrial and organizational (I-O) psychology**.

## Sibling-venue boundary (avoid mis-targeting)

- **Personnel Psychology** (Wiley) — I-O with a personnel selection/assessment/validation center of
  gravity; JAP leads with theoretical mechanism and micro/individual measurement.
- **Academy of Management Journal (AMJ)** — macro/meso management and organizational outcomes; JAP is
  micro/individual + measurement-focused.
- **Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes (OBHDP)** — judgment/decision processes and
  experimental micro-OB; JAP emphasizes I-O theory plus field generalizability and measurement.
- **Journal of Organizational Behavior (JOB)** — broad OB with overlapping micro topics; JAP holds a
  stronger measurement/quantitative-rigor bar.

## Re-verification 2026-11-24 (what changed)

- **Research Report cap now stated:** ≤ **19 manuscript pages** excluding title page, abstract,
  references, tables and figures (25 lines of 12-pt text per page). The earlier rule "do not encode a
  hard Research Report page cap" is retired.
- **Abstract ≤ 250 words and up to five keywords** are now confirmed on the JAP page itself.
- **Masked review only** (unmasked manuscripts are returned) — previously "masked review"; now explicit.
- **TOP levels per dimension** recorded (Data/Code/Materials at Level 1 disclosure; Citation, Design &
  Analysis, and both preregistration items at Level 2); a "Transparency and openness" Method subsection
  and a **data transparency appendix** are required.
- The **1,000-word proposal** rule is no longer on the page; Integrative Conceptual Reviews start
  with a **10-page prospectus**.
- **Masthead:** Mo Wang is listed as the **incoming (2027) editor handling all new submissions in 2026**;
  Lillian T. Eby handles invited revisions only in 2026.

## Bounded live-check rules

- Treat Mo Wang (University of Florida) as the Editor-in-Chief handling new submissions from January 2026,
  with Lillian T. Eby as outgoing editor (web-verified 2026-06-22 via UF Warrington announcement + APA
  new-editor-appointments page); re-open the APA masthead before naming the full term window.
- Research Reports are capped at 19 manuscript pages (stated on the live page 2026-11-24); Feature
  Article length remains "commensurate with contribution" with no numeric cap.
- The 250-word abstract and five-keyword limits are JAP-specific rules as of 2026-11-24.
- TOP adherence and the "Transparency and openness" subsection are load-bearing; copy the example
  wording from the live APA page during submission.
- Submission is free; the gold open-access APC amount is not stated on the guidelines page — do not
  assert a figure.
- Do not use third-party acceptance-rate estimates as policy.
