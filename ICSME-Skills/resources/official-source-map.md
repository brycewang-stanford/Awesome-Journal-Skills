## Verified ICSME 2027 facts used in skills

- **ICSME 2027 is the 43rd IEEE International Conference on Software Maintenance and Evolution**,
  in **Toronto, Canada**, hosted by **York University**, **7-11 September 2027** (sources 1, 2). The
  artifact track still serves SCAM and VISSOFT papers too (source 8), so the co-location continues.
- **Research-track calendar (23:59 AoE):** abstract **19 February 2027**, full paper **26 February
  2027**, early decisions **26 April 2027**, author response **27-30 April 2027**, final notification
  **25 May 2027**, camera-ready **25 June 2027** (sources 1, 3). Submission is through the ICSME 2027
  **EasyChair** instance; the link was still "TBD" on 2026-11-23.
- **Format and budget:** IEEE two-column conference format, `\documentclass[conference]{IEEEtran}`
  (Word: US Letter template); **10 pages** including all figures, tables, and appendices, **plus up
  to 2 pages containing only references**. Non-compliant or out-of-scope papers are **desk-rejected**
  (source 1). All papers must be full papers, in English.
- **Review is double-anonymous**: no author names or affiliations, third-person self-reference, a
  title different from any arXiv preprint, and no public use of the title during review. Each paper
  gets at least three PC reviewers; replicability is evaluated where applicable (source 1).
- **Early decisions + author response** continue "as in 2025": at the start of the response period
  some papers already receive Accept or Reject, the rest "Response Recommended"; authors see full
  reviews and scores and answer the PC's specific questions (source 1).
- **AI use:** authors follow IEEE's guidelines for AI-generated content — all AI-generated content
  (text, figures, images, tables, code) must be disclosed, the AI system identified, and the
  sections using it noted with a brief explanation of the AI's involvement (sources 1, 7).
- **Open science:** sharing data/replication packages is expected by default and non-sharing must be
  justified; at submission authors share via an archival host, explain why not, or promise release
  on acceptance (source 5).
- **Publication:** IEEE Xplore, electronic IEEE Copyright form; at least one author must register by
  the early-registration date and present, or the paper is withdrawn; selected papers are invited to
  an **EMSE special issue** (source 1).
- **Journal-First (J1C2)** admits partner-journal papers accepted between 1 Jan 2025 and 30 May 2027,
  not previously presented and not extensions of conference papers; presented, not re-reviewed, and
  not in the proceedings (source 6).
- **Registered Reports** (with EMSE) runs Stage 1 at ICSME and Stage 2 at EMSE, with In-Principle
  Acceptance or Continuity Acceptance outcomes (source 7).
- **Track change:** the 2027 track list adds a **Benchmark & Dataset Track** (OpenReview; 4-page
  short / 6-8-page long, references included) and has **no RENE track** — RENE ran through ICSME
  2026 (sources 2, 9, 13). Replications now go to the research track, where replicability is an
  explicit criterion, or to Registered Reports.
- **Artifacts** are evaluated in the **Artifact Evaluation track / ROSE Festival** (single-anonymous,
  EasyChair) and earn the **IEEE badges "Open Research Object" and "Research Object Reviewed"** — the
  IEEE scheme, not ACM's (source 8).

## The single-round IEEE model (honest reading of the cycle question)

Unlike the journal-style PACMSE model at ESEC/FSE, ICSME runs a **conference-style single annual
research round**: one abstract-then-paper deadline, double-anonymous review, an early-decision cut,
an author-response rebuttal, and a final accept/reject — there is **no journal-style Major Revision
round** on the research track. A paper that needs deep rework is rejected and resubmitted next
cycle (or rerouted to SCAM, a journal, or the Journal-First path once a journal accepts it). The
maintenance/evolution community also gives unusual weight to **replication and negative results**
and to **open science** (the ROSE Festival and the open-science policy), so evidence culture, not a
revision round, is where author effort concentrates.

## No editor-in-chief; conference registration model

Like every conference in this collection, ICSME has **no standing editor-in-chief**; leadership is
the per-edition General and Program Co-Chairs appointed by the ICSME steering committee under IEEE
TCSE (2027: General Co-Chairs Gias Uddin and Song Wang; Program Co-Chairs Shiva Nejati and Lei Ma,
source 4). Proceedings publish in **IEEE Xplore**; the cost model is conference registration, and at
least one author registers and presents each accepted paper (including Journal-First contributions).
