## Verified 2026 facts used in skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

- CCS 2026 was held at the World Forum, The Hague, Netherlands, November 15-19, 2026.
- CCS 2026 used HotCRP with two review cycles, all deadlines 11:59 PM AoE (UTC-12).
  Cycle A: abstract registration Jan 7, full paper Jan 14, early-reject notification Feb 20,
  rebuttal Mar 17-20, notification Apr 9, minor-revision approval June 5, camera-ready
  August 9, 2026. Cycle B: abstract registration Apr 22, full paper Apr 29, online-artifact
  deadline May 2, early-reject notification June 12 (moved twice from June 3), rebuttal
  June 29-July 1, notification July 17, minor-revision approval Sept 4, camera-ready
  Sept 13, 2026.
- Registration is mandatory and precedes the full-paper deadline by one week each cycle. It
  must carry the definitive title, abstract, track, and a track justification (about 200
  words); none of these may change afterwards. The author list may change until the
  submission deadline and is frozen from then on.
- Submissions are anonymized: self-cite in the third person, blind references only where
  third-person phrasing is infeasible; no "we" self-references, no funding acknowledgements,
  no deanonymizing repositories. Improper anonymization risks rejection without review.
- Main text is limited to 12 pages in the unaltered ACM `sigconf` two-column template,
  excluding bibliography, well-marked appendices, and supplementary material. Template
  tampering (negative vspace, savetrees, titlesec, font or margin changes) is forbidden; the
  CCS concepts, keywords and rights-management block must be retained.
- **Open Science appendix (mandatory).** Every paper carries an appendix titled "Open
  Science", after the 12-page body and the bibliography, listing where the artifacts needed
  to evaluate the contributions are (anonymous URLs, also entered on HotCRP) or stating that
  there are none and justifying anything withheld. Artifacts must be available at most 3 days
  after submission; papers whose contributions rely on an implementation, experiments, a
  tool, or a dataset are expected to provide artifacts.
- **Ethical Considerations appendix.** Papers with potential ethical concerns (human subjects,
  user data, real-world vulnerability analysis) must include it after the 12-page body and
  bibliography; it does not count toward the page limit. IRB/ERB approval is neither strictly
  necessary nor always sufficient. CCS follows the USENIX Security '26 ethics policy.
- **Generative AI.** CCS follows the ACM authorship policy: AI tools cannot be authors and
  all use must be disclosed — minor editorial help in the Acknowledgements, substantive
  generation in a "Generative AI Usage" section after the body and bibliography.
  Hallucinated citations or fabricated results may be desk rejected; prompt injection is a
  desk-reject trigger. Reviewers may not upload submissions to public AI tools.
- CCS does **not** accept SoK or survey papers.
- All authors must supply ORCIDs on HotCRP by the submission deadline.
- Decisions are accept, minor revision (revise within the same cycle), and reject; a
  first-cycle reject may not be resubmitted to the second cycle, except an administrative
  reject that received no reviews. Withdrawal is prohibited before the final decision.
- Authors may submit at most 7 papers per cycle (2026).
- At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present in person.
- Artifact evaluation is optional and post-acceptance; ACM badges (Artifacts Available,
  Artifacts Evaluated - Functional, Artifacts Evaluated - Reusable, Results Reproduced)
  appear on the paper's first page.
- Accepted papers publish open access in the ACM Digital Library. Since January 1, 2026
  authors whose institutions are not in ACM Open pay an APC; the 2026 subsidized rate was
  USD 250 for ACM/SIG members and USD 350 for non-members.
- CCS 2026 General Chairs: Georgios Smaragdakis and Kaitai Liang. Program Chairs: Véronique
  Cortier (Université de Lorraine) and Zhiqiang Lin (The Ohio State University).
- CCS 2027 is announced for Atlanta, USA, October 11-15, 2027 (source 9); its call is not
  published as of 2026-11-24.
