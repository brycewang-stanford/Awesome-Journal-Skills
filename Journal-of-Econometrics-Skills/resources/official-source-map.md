## Official journal pages

- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-econometrics/publish/guide-for-authors** —
  ScienceDirect Guide for Authors (HTTP 403 on 2026-11-24; read via search-engine rendering of this page
  and its mirror https://www.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-econometrics/0304-4076/guide-for-authors).
  Re-verified 2026-11-24: review is **single anonymized** with editor screening and usually at least
  two reviewers; the submission fee is **USD $75**, nonrefundable, for new submissions and resubmissions
  over one year, with no exceptions (the elsevier.com mirror adds that proof of payment is uploaded
  before submitting); abstracts concise and factual and **not exceeding 250 words**, stating purpose,
  main findings and principal conclusions; **1 to 7 keywords** in English (the elsevier.com mirror
  still says a maximum of 5 — see open issues). From the 2026-06-20 read (not re-rendered on
  2026-11-24): the **Submit your article** link points to **Editorial Manager**; regular full-length
  papers, short papers under 15 pages, themed issues, invited "how to" papers, and Annals Issues;
  no student discounts/refunds and VAT for European authors; initial source files editable
  (`.doc/.docx` or `.tex`), PDF not an acceptable source file; dataset references use the
  **`[dataset]`** tag; optional **Data in Brief / MethodsX** co-submission via Editorial Manager.
- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-econometrics** — ScienceDirect journal home
  (Internet Archive capture of 2026-10-17; the live page returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-24). Verifies: two
  submission types — regular open submissions (full-length papers, or **short papers less than 15
  pages**; Themed Issues of regular submissions approved by the Co-Editors) and invited papers ("how to"
  papers; Annals Issues); open-access **APC USD 4,970** (excluding taxes), subscription route with no
  publication fee; Managing Editor **Michael Jansson**; Co-Editors **Cavaliere, Fan, Honoré, Todorov**;
  Aureo de Paula among former editors; open call "After the Credibility Revolution: Causal Analysis in
  Contemporary Econometrics" (guest editors Peter Hull and Christopher Walters; submission deadline
  1 March 2027).
- **https://www.editorialmanager.com/econom/default.aspx** — Editorial Manager site for the Journal of
  Econometrics (read directly 2026-11-24; the landing page loads under the journal's name).
- **https://sites.google.com/view/journalofeconometrics/guidelines** — The journal's own
  self-administered author guidelines (read directly 2026-11-24). Verifies: the page still says "All
  submissions must be made through **Editorial Express**"; proof of payment is required; the linked
  guideline buckets are **Regular Issues, Annals Issues, and Themed Issues**.
- **https://sites.google.com/view/journalofeconometrics/themes** — Themed (Theme) Issues page (read
  directly 2026-11-24). Verifies: Themed Issues go through the regular editorial process; proposal
  organizers may serve as **Guest Associate Editors** who make recommendations to the Co-Editors.
- **https://editorialexpress.com/cgi-bin/e-editor/e-submit.cgi?dbase=je** — JoE submission portal on
  **Editorial Express** (database `je`; read directly 2026-11-24). Verifies: the page still says **"This
  page is for resubmissons only!"** and links new submissions elsewhere; within the Editorial Express
  flow, authors confirm the main text is around 40 pages at least 1.5 spacing and 11pt font,
  manuscripts must be uploaded in PDF format only, and the form has a six-step submission process.

## History, scope, editors, metrics

- **https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Econometrics** — Verifies: publisher **Elsevier**;
  **founded 1973**; peer-reviewed; **"Fellow of the Journal of Econometrics"** designation for authors
  who publish four or more articles in the journal. Scope covers econometric theory and applied
  methodology. (Its editor line — Jansson and de Paula as Co-Editors-in-Chief — is older than the
  ScienceDirect masthead above and is no longer relied on.)

## Scope summary (used as load-bearing framing)

- **Field focus:** econometric methodology spanning theory and applied methods — identification,
  estimation, testing, decision, and prediction problems arising in economic research, plus
  applications of econometric techniques to economics. Source: scope statements on the Elsevier /
  ScienceDirect journal homepage and Wikipedia (above), accessed 2026-06-01; the aims-and-scope text
  was unchanged in the 2026-10-17 capture.

## Open issues to confirm live (UNVERIFIED / 待核实)

- **Portal routing is an official-page conflict, not an inference.** ScienceDirect links new JoE
  submissions to **Editorial Manager** (as of the 2026-06-20 read; the Editorial Manager `econom` site is
  live on 2026-11-24). The Editorial Express `dbase=je` page says it is for resubmissions only. The JoE
  Google Sites Guidelines page still says all submissions are made through **Editorial Express**
  (re-read 2026-11-24). This pack tells users to start from ScienceDirect/current official links for new
  submissions and to use Editorial Express for resubmission/track materials when the live official page
  directs them there.
- **~40-page / PDF-only guidance is Editorial Express-form guidance.** Treat it as a practical
  resubmission-flow norm, not as a ScienceDirect Guide hard limit for new submissions.
- **Masthead read from an archive capture.** The Managing Editor / Co-Editor structure above comes from
  the 2026-10-17 Internet Archive capture of the ScienceDirect page because the live page and the
  editorial-board page returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-24. Confirm on the live board page before naming a
  handling editor.
- **Keyword count.** Search-engine rendering of the ScienceDirect Guide gives 1 to 7 keywords; the
  elsevier.com mirror of the Guide says a maximum of 5. Not resolved on 2026-11-24.
- **Editable-source-file, VAT, and Data in Brief / MethodsX details** were read on 2026-06-20 and could
  not be re-rendered on 2026-11-24.
- **Replication policy is encouragement-first.** The official guide verifies dataset citation,
  data-linking support, and Data in Brief / MethodsX co-submission. The pack should recommend a
  strong archive and data-availability statement, while avoiding any claim that JoE has a universal
  Data-Editor-vetted archive comparable to JAE or Econometric Society journals.
