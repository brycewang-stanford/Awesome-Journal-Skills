## Official journal & society pages

- **https://academic.oup.com/ectj/pages/general_instructions** — Oxford Academic general
  instructions. Verifies: published by **Oxford University Press on behalf of the Royal
  Economic Society (RES)**; journal **established 1998**; scope emphasises original
  contributions of direct or potential **applied value** and **leading-case
  (path-breaking)** articles rather than exhaustive treatment; **conformance-rejection
  rule** (submissions not conforming to guidelines are rejected routinely); editorial
  office contact **ectj@res.org.uk**. (HTTP 403 to automated access on 2026-11-24; the same
  facts were re-read on the RES journal page below.)
- **https://res.org.uk/journals/the-econometrics-journal/** — RES journal page
  (**re-read 2026-11-24**). Verifies: **Editor-in-Chief Dennis Kristensen** (appointed **13
  April 2026**); published by Oxford University Press; established 1998 by the RES; **rapid
  review** (assigned to an editor or screen-rejected within one week, peer reviewed within
  three months); only submissions conforming to the guidelines are accepted; editorial office
  ectj@res.org.uk. 2025 impact measures (JCR 2026) appear here only as context, not
  load-bearing submission policy.
- **https://res.org.uk/journals/the-econometrics-journal/editors/** — RES editors page
  (**read 2026-11-24**). Verifies: Editor-in-Chief **Dennis Kristensen** (University College
  London); Editors **Flávio Cunha** (Rice), **Iván Fernández-Val** (Boston University),
  **Raffaella Giacomini** (UCL) and **Christoph Rothe** (Mannheim); **Data Editor Damian
  Clarke** (Universidad de Chile and University of Exeter); a long associate-editor list.
- **https://res.org.uk/journals/the-econometrics-journal/submissions/** — EctJ submission
  guidelines page (**re-read 2026-11-24**; the journal-specific copy of the guidance cited
  below). Verifies the same 150-word summary / 20-page / LaTeX-template / empirical-application
  / one-page-simulation rules, the cover-letter list, the exemptions, and links the LaTeX
  template (EctJ_Style_Instructions_Template260505.zip, May 2026) and the Editorial Express
  portal **https://editorialexpress.com/ectj**. It now says the journal **encourages a
  replication package with the initial submission** but only requires one later in review.
- **https://res.org.uk/submissions/** — RES submission guidance (**re-read 2026-11-24**,
  unchanged). Verifies: page limit
  **not normally exceeding 20 pages including the printed appendix**; **summary/abstract
  no more than 150 words**; PDF in journal style via the **RES/EctJ LaTeX template**;
  **empirical application required** even for theory; simulation results summarised
  **within ~one page** in the main text; **mathematical proofs in main text/printed
  appendix, not the online appendix**; cover letter only for conflicts / exemptions /
  prior reports; submission via **Editorial Express**.
- **https://editorialexpress.com/cgi-bin/e-editor/e-submit_v18.cgi?dbase=ectj** —
  EctJ EditorialExpress e-editor submission portal (entry point https://editorialexpress.com/ectj,
  **re-read 2026-11-24**). Verifies: submission system is **Editorial Express**; seven steps
  including "Pay Submission fee"; states "a fee for each new submission: £75", VAT at 20%, no
  fee for resubmissions; authors must confirm they accept the Submission Guidelines,
  Replication Policy, Disclosure Policy and Submission Fee Policy before proceeding.
- **https://res.org.uk/disclosure-policy/** — EctJ disclosure policy (**read 2026-11-24**).
  Verifies: submission implies the work is original, unpublished and not under review
  elsewhere; co-authors share joint responsibility; **any sources of research funding are to
  be acknowledged on the manuscript**; at submission authors reveal **in a cover letter** any
  financial or other interests that might raise a question of bias (held in confidence; part of
  the published article on acceptance).
- **https://res.org.uk/conflicts-of-interest/** — EctJ editorial conflict-of-interest rules
  (**read 2026-11-24**). Verifies editor recusal rules and that the journal does not accept
  papers (co-)authored by the Editor-in-Chief.
- **https://res.org.uk/supporting/** — EctJ supporting-information page (**read 2026-11-24**).
  Verifies: supporting information normally includes the replication package and often an
  online appendix, must be inessential to understanding the paper, and the handling editor has
  final discretion over what is included.
- **https://res.org.uk/review-process/** — RES review-process page (**re-read 2026-11-24**,
  unchanged; also notes editors can ask for a replication package at any stage and the data
  editor checks it with expert research assistants). Verifies: papers
  conforming to guidelines reviewed by the **Editor-in-Chief (and possibly an Editor)**;
  within **one week** either desk-rejected or assigned to an editor; **decision within
  three months**; **resubmission normally within one month (sometimes three)**;
  replication package required **conditional on acceptance**.
- **https://res.org.uk/submission-fee-policy/** — RES submission-fee policy (**re-read
  2026-11-24**, unchanged; also: resubmissions carry no fee). Verifies:
  **flat £75 submission fee for all authors per new submission, plus VAT at 20%**; **NO
  reduced fee for RES members** (contrast: The Economic Journal has tiered member-
  discounted fees); fee introduced **spring 2024**; **non-refundable except for genuine
  payment errors**; desk rejections are not refunded.
- **https://res.org.uk/replication-policy/** — RES replication policy (**re-read
  2026-11-24**; also asks for packages to bundle non-standard software packages, suggests a
  makefile, discourages fully proprietary data and requires simulated or synthetic data plus
  code where data cannot be shared). Verifies:
  replication package required **conditional on acceptance**; package must contain a
  **README** describing contents and **software versions**, **data with complete variable
  documentation (ASCII files if proprietary formats are used)**, and **all code to
  reproduce tables, figures, supplements, appendices, and simulations (with seeds set)**;
  posted alongside the article as **Supporting Information**; **exemption / usage-
  restriction requests must be stated clearly at first submission** and the editorial
  board decides whether the submission can be considered; board **may refuse publication
  for non-compliance**. Cross-journal reproducibility coordination via the **Data Editors
  of the AEA, Review of Economic Studies, Canadian Journal of Economics, the Econometric
  Society, and The Economic Journal & The Econometrics Journal**. The live replication-policy
  page read on 2026-11-24 no longer carries that coordination wording or names a person; the
  current **Data Editor is Damian Clarke** per the RES editors page (2026-11-24).

## Bounded live-check rules

- **Peer-review blinding model** (single-blind vs double-blind / anonymous): the official
  OUP and RES author pages document a screening + ~3-month-decision rapid-review process
  but **do not name the anonymity model**. Skills do not claim a blinding model.
- **Exact citation / reference style name** (e.g., Harvard author-date): not stated in
  plain text on the official author pages; the mandated LaTeX template presumably encodes
  it, but the style name itself is not load-bearing. Skills defer to the template.
- **Keyword count and mandatory JEL classification codes**: not explicitly required on the
  official author pages reviewed (the OUP page lists JEL categories only for browsing).
  Skills do not assert a required keyword/JEL rule.
- **Data editor** — resolved 2026-11-24: the RES editors page names **Damian Clarke** as
  Data Editor; the earlier Florian Oswald attribution came from older cross-journal
  materials and is no longer used.
- **2025 metrics (JCR 2026: Journal Impact Factor 4.1, 5-Year 6.5, Article Influence
  5.533, Journal Citation Indicator 2.26)**: shown on the RES journal page on 2026-11-24 but
  not central to submission norms and not independently re-verified against Clarivate.
  Context only.
- **Peer-review blinding model** was re-checked on 2026-11-24 on every RES page listed
  above and is still not stated (待核实; the block records it as null).
- **AI / LLM-use policy**: no EctJ- or RES-specific AI policy was found on the RES pages
  read on 2026-11-24; the OUP general-instructions page was not reachable (HTTP 403).
  待核实 — do not assert one.

## Explicitly NOT applicable (checked, to avoid misattribution)

- The **JAE Data Archive** (Journal of Applied Econometrics) is a *different* journal's
  archive and is **NOT** referenced by The Econometrics Journal. EctJ posts replication
  packages as **OUP Supporting Information** and coordinates via the **EJ/EctJ Data
  Editor**. Do not point EctJ authors to the JAE Archive.
- **The Economic Journal's tiered, member-discounted fees** do **NOT** apply to EctJ;
  EctJ charges a **flat £75 (+VAT) with no member discount**.
