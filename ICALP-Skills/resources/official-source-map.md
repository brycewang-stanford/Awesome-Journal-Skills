Proceedings in Informatics, Schloss Dagstuhl). ICALP has a **two-track** structure — Track A
(Algorithms, Complexity and Games) and Track B (Automata, Logic, Semantics and Theory of

## Verified ICALP 2027 facts used in skills

- **ICALP 2027 is the 54th EATCS ICALP**, **Kraków, Poland, July 5-9, 2027**, hosted by
  Jagiellonian University and PTM, in person (sources 1-5).
- **Two tracks:** Track A — Algorithms, Complexity and Games; Track B — Automata, Logic, Semantics
  and Theory of Programming (sources 1, 2, 13).
- **Calendar:** Track B round 1 — submissions **15 September 2026** (AoE), rebuttal **5-9 November
  2026**, notification **8 December 2026** (all past). Track B round 2 and Track A's only round —
  **TBA**; DMANET says "as usual, early-mid February 2027" (sources 1, 6). Conference July 5-9, 2027.
- **Submission format (Track B call):** title page (title, no author information, abstract); **no
  page limit**; full version encouraged; first **ten** pages after the title page must present
  merits, contributions and key ideas; the rest read at the committee's discretion; readable fonts
  (e.g. 11-point), single column, ample spacing; LIPIcs class **recommended**; pdflatex or similar
  mandatory (source 1). ICALP 2026 used the same format for both tracks (source 8).
- **Review:** lightweight double-blind; **Track B has a rebuttal** (in each round). Track A's 2027
  process is not yet published; in ICALP 2026, Track A authors were contacted only on correctness
  issues (sources 1, 7, 8).
- **No prior publication and no simultaneous submission** to a conference or a journal (source 1).
- **AI disclosure:** AI use in conducting the research must be disclosed in the submitted paper,
  with the specific uses described (source 1).
- **Publication:** LIPIcs, open access; accepted papers must follow the LIPIcs style (sources 1, 11).
- **Chairs:** Track A Saket Saurabh and Jukka Suomela; Track B Manuel Bodirsky and Ichiro Hasuo;
  Proceedings Chair Gabriele Puppis (source 3).
- **Awards:** best paper and best student paper per track (student-only papers marked at
  submission); EATCS Award, Presburger Award, EATCS Distinguished Dissertation Award at ICALP;
  Gödel Prize alternates between ICALP and STOC (sources 1, 13, 14).

## The pure-theory model (why there is no artifact evaluation)

ICALP is a **pure theoretical-computer-science venue**. There is **no artifact-evaluation track and
no ACM/IEEE-style badge scheme** — the paper *is* its proofs. The analogue of an artifact here is the
**full version**: complete, checkable proofs in the submission itself (there is no page limit) and/or
an arXiv/ECCC/HAL posting. This pack therefore adapts the usual "artifact" and "reproducibility"
skills to **proof rigor and full-version norms** rather than runnable code. A paper whose central
contribution *is* an implementation or an experiment is usually mis-routed and belongs at an
experimental-algorithms or systems venue (see `icalp-topic-selection`).

## No editor-in-chief; the LIPIcs open-access model

Like every conference in this collection, ICALP has **no standing editor-in-chief**; the rotating
analogue is the per-edition track chairs and the local Organizing Committee, under the **EATCS** and
the ICALP steering committee. LIPIcs is **open access** with no reader paywall; the cost model is
conference registration plus Dagstuhl's publication charge, and at least one author presents each
accepted paper in person.
