# External Tools - ICALP (EATCS)

Access date: 2026-07-09; official links re-verified 2026-11-24 (direct fetch).

Use these surfaces after reopening the current ICALP cycle pages. ICALP mechanics — the
February deadline (and, since ICALP 2027, a September round for Track B), the format (no page limit
since ICALP 2026; the first ten pages after the title page must carry the paper), which track carries
a rebuttal, the AI-disclosure rule, the LIPIcs production timeline — are decided per edition. The
official hosts loaded directly on 2026-11-24; if a gateway returns 403, read via search renderings
and cross-check dblp and DROPS. ICALP is EATCS's European theory flagship;
do not import STOC/FOCS (ACM) or SODA (SIAM) rules.

## Author-side checks

- HotCRP profile on the **correct track server** (Track A vs Track B are separate HotCRP sites),
  conflicts, coauthor emails, the Track B **round** (one only), and any **abstract-registration**
  step that precedes the full-paper deadline (ICALP 2026 had one, three days earlier).
- Format: **no page limit** (since ICALP 2026); a title page with title, abstract and no authors; the
  first ten pages after it must present the merits and key ideas; readable fonts (e.g. 11-point),
  single column, ample spacing; the LIPIcs class is recommended (ICALP 2027 Track B) and the LIPIcs
  style is mandatory for the camera-ready.
- **AI-use disclosure** in the paper if AI tools were used in conducting the research (ICALP 2027).
- **Lightweight double-blind** sweep: no author names/affiliations/acknowledgements, third-person
  self-citation, no identifying links, and an arXiv posting that does not undercut anonymity.
- Student-only authorship flagged at submission for the **best student paper** award.
- No prior publication and no concurrent submission elsewhere.

## Do not infer

- Do not assume both tracks behave identically: **Track B has a rebuttal** and, since ICALP 2027,
  two rounds; **Track A** contacted authors only for correctness issues in ICALP 2026. Confirm per
  cycle (the ICALP 2027 Track A call was not yet posted on 2026-11-24).
- Do not assume the page limit, the mandatory-class rule, the rebuttal window, or any AI-disclosure
  wording carry over between editions.
- Do not treat ICALP like STOC/FOCS/SODA: it is EATCS/LIPIcs, two-track, and open-access — a different ecosystem with different norms.
- If pages disagree, treat the newest CFP, the track HotCRP deadline page, or a direct PC-chair
  announcement as controlling, and record the conflict.
