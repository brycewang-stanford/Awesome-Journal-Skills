## VERIFIED facts injected into the skills (with source #, re-verified 2026-11-23 unless noted)

- **Mission (quote):** "to publish empirical research that tests, extends, or builds management theory and contributes to management practice." (#10, #2)
- **All empirical methods welcome:** qualitative, quantitative, field, laboratory, meta-analytic, and mixed methods. (#10, #2)
- **Three contributions:** strong empirical contribution + meaningful theoretical contribution + practical relevance; submissions without a substantial empirical component will not be reviewed. (#2)
- **Length:** "Typically, papers should not exceed **40** double-spaced pages (Times New Roman 12-point font, one-inch margins, inclusive of all content)"; longer manuscripts may be considered when warranted; incorrectly prepared papers may be returned before review. Tables, figures, references, and appendices therefore **count** toward the 40 pages. AOM uses a page guideline, not a word cap. (#2; supersedes the older #3 reading that excluded tables and figures)
- **Formatting:** Word file; body double-spaced, headings 1.5-spaced; Times New Roman 12-pt; Letter page; 1-inch margins; **no page numbers or running heads**; no title page with a paper under review. (#5)
- **Abstract:** no more than **200** words, with the title on page 2. (#5)
- **Keywords:** **three** keywords required at submission ("We require that you submit three keywords"). (#2)
- **Submission system:** ScholarOne Manuscripts at mc.manuscriptcentral.com/AMJ; six-step process; acknowledgments go in the Cover Letter section; all author identifiers removed. (#2, #4)
- **Review:** "All submissions are blind-reviewed"; editor desk screen (desk reject or desk edit), then an action editor and three reviewers; feedback targeted "within 60 days"; authors are expected to reciprocate by reviewing. (#2)
- **Style:** AOM Style Guide for Authors (author-date citations, three heading levels, exhibits at the end). (#5)
- **AI policy:** authors must disclose any AI use in the cover letter and acknowledgements and confirm they read the AOM AI policy; language-model disclosures name the model and purpose. (#2, #13)
- **Integrity:** no simultaneous submission; prior drafts posted online must be taken down before submission and stay down during review. (#11)
- **Fees:** AOM charges **no submission fee** (#12) and offers **no Gold (author-funded) OA / APC** — "Currently, AOM does not support Gold, author-funded OA" (#11). Green OA: pre-copyedited manuscript self-archiving; Version of Record openly postable only after a **12-month embargo**.
  - **STALE-FIX (2026-06-23):** an earlier version of this map claimed an AMJ APC of US$3,500. That was incorrect; do not state any AMJ APC.
- **Cadence/metrics:** bimonthly (Feb/Apr/Jun/Aug/Oct/Dec); AOM displays **12.8** Impact Factor, **14.3** Five-Year Impact Factor, **7.69** Article Influence Score (Clarivate 2026). Founded 1958 (#8). (#10)
- **Editor:** **Quinetta M. Roberson (Michigan State University)**, Editor, on the live AMJ editorial-team page. (#7b)
  - **STALE-FIX:** Gary A. Ballinger is the incoming **Annals** Co-Editor-in-Chief, not the AMJ editor. Two skills (amj-workflow, amj-review-process) still named him and were corrected 2026-11-23.

- **Desk-review turnaround (~1-2 weeks)** — no longer stated on the AMJ page re-read 2026-11-23; only the 60-day feedback target is official.
- **AOM membership requirement to submit** — no membership gate is stated on the official AMJ submission/author-resources pages (re-checked 2026-11-23).
- **Full current editorial-board roster** and associate editors (rotates on multi-year terms).
