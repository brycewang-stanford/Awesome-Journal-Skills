### Open access, APC & licensing
- **Fully gold open access.** Every article is OA on publication; there is **no subscription/hybrid option**. **CONFIRMED.** Verbatim: "As a gold open access journal, PNAS Nexus complies with all major funder mandates, and all articles are freely and publicly available to read."
- **APC (article processing charge): indicative only ≈ US$2,200–4,200 — confirm live.** OUP's author page publishes **no fixed standard research-article APC**; it prices charges through a **region/category-personalized calculator**, so the amount billed depends on **article category, the corresponding author's region/country, and any membership/institutional discount** — and **LMIC waivers or Read & Publish agreements can reduce or eliminate it.** Public third-party trackers **disagree**: the **DOAJ** record reports ≈**US$4,202** while another tracker reports ≈**US$2,200**; neither is confirmable on the live OUP calculator, and **neither is clearly more authoritative**. So treat the APC as **indicative only and confirm the exact charge live in the OUP calculator at submission.** No separate submission/editorial fee is documented (the APC is the only charge — LIKELY). *(DOAJ and the OUP calculator both returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch on 2026-06-23; the figures here are third-party-tracker values surfaced via web search, not eyeballed on the live pages — re-confirm before relying on them.)*
- **Licenses:** **CC BY 4.0** and **CC BY-NC 4.0** offered; re-checked 2026-11-23 on the general-instructions page, which names **no default** and tells authors to check funder requirements before choosing. (As of February 2024, PNAS Nexus changed from CC BY + CC BY-NC-ND to **CC BY + CC BY-NC**.) **CONFIRMED.** The two-license menu with author choice was re-corroborated — search-rendering verified 2026-07-08 (direct fetch blocked); re-confirm live before relying — via renderings of https://www.pnas.org/post/update/pnas-nexus-expands-license-options and https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/pages/general-instructions. The named technical default is still **待核实** — no rendering names a default license; authors select between the two, so confirm at license signing. CC BY is the funder-compliant choice. CC BY-NC-ND is **no longer offered**.
- **Waivers/discounts:** **Full waiver** for corresponding authors in **low-income** countries and a **discount** for **middle-income** countries under OUP's low- and middle-income-countries initiative; discretionary waivers on request (openaccess@oup.com) at submission; **Read & Publish / transformative agreements** at participating institutions; a **membership-discount** option exists in the calculator — on 2026-11-23 it showed a **20% society-membership discount** (displayed against the calculator's default book-review category, charge 3625 USD); eligibility for research articles 待核实. The LMIC discount "is applied prior to any other discount"; charges exclude taxes and are "subject to change". Changing the corresponding author to access Read & Publish funding is not permitted. **CONFIRMED** (mechanisms); specific amounts 待核实.
- **Funder compliance & copyright:** the journal states it "complies with all major funder mandates"; a CC BY route in a fully-OA journal satisfies Plan S/cOAlition S and CC-BY funder requirements (per-funder endorsements inferred, not individually quoted → 待核实). **Authors retain copyright.** **CONFIRMED** (general).

### Scope, positioning & process
- **Scope (verbatim):** "PNAS Nexus is a gold open access journal dedicated to publishing high-quality original research from across the biological, medical, physical, social, and political sciences, and engineering and mathematics, promoting collaboration across diverse fields." **CONFIRMED.**
- **Positioning:** **sibling / complementary** to PNAS, differentiated by **broader, cross-disciplinary scope and a broader editorial base** (three academies) — **not** by an explicitly stated "lower-tier" or "sound-science-only" quality bar. Any "sound-science only / not-top-1%" framing is **absent from official language — search-rendering verified 2026-07-08 (direct fetch blocked); re-confirm live before relying.** Renderings of https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/pages/what-makes-pnas-nexus-unique and the launch editorials ("Introducing PNAS Nexus," PMC9802291; "Welcome, PNAS Nexus!," PMC9681191) consistently frame the journal around **multidisciplinary/interdisciplinary convergence across the three academies (NAS/NAE/NAM)** and work "at the nexus" of science, engineering, and medicine — none uses "sound-science-only" or tiered-quality language. Do not assert such framing.
- **PNAS → PNAS Nexus transfer/cascade:** **CONFIRMED.** Manuscripts declined by PNAS may be offered transfer into the PNAS portfolio (incl. PNAS Nexus), and **prior PNAS reviews transfer with the manuscript**. Verbatim: "Any previous reviews of your PNAS submission will be transferred to PNAS Nexus alongside your manuscript, potentially reducing the number of reviews required." Note the qualifier: "Research Reports declined by PNAS for reasons other than soundness or technical merit may be recommended for transfer to PNAS Nexus." Transfer is not a guarantee of acceptance; PNAS Nexus assesses independently; author consent required.
- **Peer review (re-verified 2026-11-23):** "PNAS Nexus uses single anonymous peer review" — reviewer names stay confidential unless reviewers agree to share them; manuscripts are not anonymized.
- **Submission tracks:** **NO Contributed (NAS-member-communicated) track and NO Direct/prearranged-editor track.** **CONFIRMED.** All submissions undergo the same editor-assigned peer review: assigned first to an Editorial Board member, then to a handling editor from the Board of Reviewing Editors (or a guest editor), with **at least two independent expert reviews**.

### Article types & length (CONFIRMED, verbatim where quoted)
- **Research Report:** preferred **6 pages**, **maximum 12 pages**. "A standard 6-page article is approximately **4,000 words, 50 references, and 4 medium-size graphical elements** (i.e., figures and tables)."
- **Brief Report:** "limited to **3 pages**" (≈**1,600 words, 15 references**). (Official label is **"Brief Report,"** not "Brief Communication.")
- **Registered Report — Stage 1:** "should not exceed **3 pages**" (≈**1,600 words**). **Stage 2:** preferred 6 pages / 4,000 words, max 12 pages.
- **Perspective:** "up to a maximum of **10 pages**." **Review:** "up to a maximum of **10 pages**." Perspectives and Reviews may be submitted unsolicited.
- The page limit counts the **whole package** — abstract, significance statement, main text, figure captions, and table legends all count. A separate hard word cap for the 12-page maximum is not stated; a "6,000-word cap" seen in third-party guides is **not on the official page** (re-checked 2026-11-23; the official limit is page-based).

### Front matter, structure & policy (CONFIRMED, verbatim where quoted)
- **Significance Statement:** required for **all Research Reports and Stage 2 Registered Reports**. Verbatim: "The maximum length is **120 words**, and minimum length is **50 words**." (Note the **50-word minimum** — distinct from flagship PNAS, which emphasizes only the ≤120 ceiling.) The flagship-PNAS audience phrasing ("undergraduate-educated scientist outside their field") does **not** appear on the PNAS Nexus page (re-checked 2026-11-23: only the 50–120-word limits are stated) — write for a broad scientific audience.
- **Abstract:** verbatim "Abstracts have a maximum length of **250 words**. They should appear as a **single paragraph with no headings**."
- **Classification:** authors select from the **three major research-area categories** above, each with subcategories. Exact subcategory counts are **待核实** (do not state precise numbers).
- **Materials and Methods in the main text:** verbatim "The main text must contain a materials and methods section ... This information cannot be provided solely as supporting information." (Shared with flagship PNAS.)
- **Data & code availability (strict):** verbatim "Authors must make all materials, data, and associated protocols, including code and scripts, used in the analysis of the study available to readers in a **public repository upon publication**." "All data and any direct outputs from imaging systems must be retained in their **raw, unprocessed versions**." "Failure or refusal to provide data upon request may be grounds for **rejection of the manuscript or retraction of the article**." Data citations use the **`[dataset]`** tag (FORCE11 Data Citation Principles).
- **References:** verbatim "You may format references in any **readable style at submission**." (A key contrast with flagship PNAS, which enforces numbered-by-appearance up front.) **Production style re-verified 2026-11-23:** the journal follows the "Council of Science Editors parent style", with US spelling outside quotations and references — prepare one consistent style that converts cleanly. *(2026-07-08 attempt: renderings of the OUP general-instructions page re-confirm only the "any readable style at submission" rule; a third-party guide (manusights.com) reports the published style is a numbered, PNAS-portfolio/Vancouver-like format with abbreviated journal names — third-party, not official; no official rendering specifies the production style, so the flag stands.)*
- **Templates:** Word template and LaTeX (Overleaf) offered. **CONFIRMED** (re-checked 2026-11-23: Word templates for the research article and SI Appendix; the PNAS Nexus Overleaf template preferred for LaTeX).
- **Title page / keywords (re-verified 2026-11-23):** keywords and classification are required title-page items; the page states no keyword count.
- **Required statements (re-verified 2026-11-23):** data-sharing statement in the submitted manuscript ("A statement detailing sharing plans must be included in the submitted manuscript"); competing interests disclosed at submission in the submission system; author contributions using the CRediT taxonomy; funding statement ("The authors declare no funding." if none); any AI use in preparing the manuscript disclosed in Materials and Methods, and AI cannot be an author.
- **Indexing & metrics (re-verified 2026-11-23, OUP About page):** DOAJ, Scopus, Web of Science ESCI, PubMed Central (MEDLINE not mentioned); Clarivate 2025 Impact Factor **4.8**; Scopus 2025 CiteScore **6.7**.
