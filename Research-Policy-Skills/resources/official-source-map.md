# Research Policy Official Source Map

Primary source map for facts used in the Research Policy skill pack. **Access date: 2026-06.** Submission rules (article length, abstract, keywords, highlights, double-blind files, declarations), APC and editorial team **re-verified 2026-11-24**. Re-check editor names, fees, exact length limits, portal forms, data policies, and style details before relying on them. Items not confirmed from an official page are marked **待核实** in the skills.

Access-method note (2026-11-24): www.sciencedirect.com answers automated clients (`curl`, the fetch tool) with a captcha/HTTP 403, and Elsevier's legacy guide-for-authors PDF for the journal is not public. The journal home page was therefore read from the **Internet Archive capture of 2026-10-17** (`web.archive.org/web/20261017133235/https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/research-policy`), and the Guide for Authors, RP FAQ and editorial-board pages were read via **search-engine rendering restricted to sciencedirect.com / elsevier.com**. **Changed since 2026-06:** the gold-OA APC shown on the journal home is now **USD 4,500** (excluding taxes), not "around USD 3,700"; keywords are now **1 to 7** (the 2024 guide said a maximum of 6).

## Official pages

- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/research-policy** — journal home (Internet Archive capture 2026-10-17). Verifies: all papers are subject to a "double-blind" review process; online submission at https://www.editorialmanager.com/RESPOL/default.aspx; Article Publishing Charge **USD 4,500** (excluding taxes; actual APCs may differ by author circumstances); Print ISSN 0048-7333, Online ISSN 1873-7625; CiteScore 17.6, Impact Factor 8.9; editorial-office contact Alice Livingston Ortolani (Research Policy Secretariat); authors should first check fit with the Editorial Strategy in the Guide for Authors. Access date: 2026-11-24.
- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/research-policy/publish/guide-for-authors** — Guide for Authors (search-engine rendering, 2026-11-24). Verifies: Research Articles are full-length papers of up to 8-10,000 words; Research Notes typically 3-5,000 words (with a cover letter explaining the choice); abstract concise, factual, stand-alone, not exceeding **250 words**; **1 to 7 keywords** in English; **highlights required at submission**, 3 to 5 bullet points of at most 85 characters including spaces, in a separate editable file; a "Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies" section when AI was used; data availability stated at submission (reasons may be given in the submission form if data cannot be shared); declaration of competing interests (in the manuscript when a separate declaration file is not submitted); double-anonymized peer review section. Access date: 2026-11-24.
- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/research-policy/about/policies/rp-faqs** — RP FAQ (search-engine rendering, 2026-11-24). Verifies: the 8-10,000-word maximum counts footnotes, references and text in tables; less central material may go to appendices published as online supplementary material; author names and institution go in a separate upload, the manuscript itself is anonymized; self-citations are kept; overlapping submitted or forthcoming work is disclosed and may be cited as "Author (forthcoming)". Access date: 2026-11-24.
- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/research-policy/about/editorial-board** — editorial board (search-engine rendering, 2026-11-24). Verifies: Maria Savona (SPRU, University of Sussex) listed under Editors; 104 editors and editorial board members in 22 countries/regions. Access date: 2026-11-24.
- **https://www.elsevier.com/journals/research-policy/0048-7333/guide-for-authors** — legacy Elsevier guide URL (now redirects to ScienceDirect). Access date: 2026-06.

## Verified on official pages (2026-06; re-verified 2026-06-22 and 2026-11-24)

- **Distributed editorial team (no single Editor-in-Chief)**, anchored at SPRU (Science Policy Research Unit, University of Sussex); editors include Ben Martin, Ammon Salter, Maryann Feldman, Elisa Giuliani, Keun Lee, Reinhilde Veugelers, and Maria Savona, among others. (Elsevier Research Policy editorial board)
- **Coordinating Editor: Maria Savona** (SPRU, University of Sussex / Luiss University) — confirmed via WebSearch 2026-06-22 (>=2 corroborating results). On 2026-11-24 the editorial-board rendering still lists her under Editors but does not show the "Coordinating Editor" title (see 待核实). This is the closest analogue to an Editor-in-Chief; the day-to-day model remains a distributed team of handling editors, so route to the editor whose area fits.
- Published by **Elsevier**; submit via Editorial Manager (https://www.editorialmanager.com/RESPOL/default.aspx).
- **Review model (re-verified 2026-11-24):** double-blind — upload a title page with author details separately and an anonymized manuscript.
- **Length and front matter (re-verified 2026-11-24):** Research Articles up to 8-10,000 words including footnotes, references and table text (editors may allow latitude in exceptional cases); Research Notes 3-5,000 words; abstract ≤ 250 words; 1 to 7 keywords; highlights required (3 to 5 bullets, ≤ 85 characters each, separate file).
- **Fees (re-verified 2026-11-24):** the subscription route carries **no APC / no submission fee**; the gold open-access option shows an Elsevier APC of **USD 4,500** (excluding taxes) on the journal home; Elsevier quotes a *personalized* APC by country/affiliation at submission, so confirm the exact amount live.

## Unverified / volatile items (待核实)

- **JEL codes:** the 2024 Guide for Authors asked for up to 6 JEL codes; the 2026-11-24 search rendering of the guide did not surface a JEL instruction, so whether JEL codes are still requested is 待核实.
- **"Coordinating Editor" title** for Maria Savona: not visible in the 2026-11-24 editorial-board rendering (she remains listed under Editors).
- The full rotating editor roster and author-selects-editor step (described in the 2024 guide) could not be read directly (sciencedirect.com captcha).
- Exemplar articles must be verified in the official archive or DOI page before being cited as real examples.
