## Verified facts encoded in the pack

- **Publisher / identity:** Elsevier / ScienceDirect journal; print ISSN 0304-3932 and online ISSN 1873-1295. (#1, #2)
- **Scope:** modern macroeconomic research across empirical, methodological, and theoretical lines, including monetary policy/theory, business cycles, fiscal policy, macroeconometrics, asset pricing, banking/credit, expectation formation, growth, trade, labor markets, and real investment. (#1, #2)
- **Editors:** S. Boragan Aruoba (University of Maryland) and Eric Swanson (UC Irvine) are co-Editors-in-Chief. Re-verified 2026-11-24 on #3 (direct) and #1 (search-engine rendering); earlier 2026-06-22 (ScienceDirect editors listing + UMD appointment note + Wikipedia all agree). (#1, #3, #9)
- **Issue rhythm:** eight regular issues per year, with Carnegie-Rochester Conference Series issues; the official JME site also describes occasional additional macro-policy proceedings. (#1, #3)
- **Review model:** single-anonymized review (re-confirmed 2026-11-24 via search-engine rendering of #2); suitable submissions are typically sent to at least two reviewers (2026-06-21 reading; see UNVERIFIED). (#2)
- **Submission fee:** USD 350 for unsolicited new manuscripts; USD 200 reduced fee for full-time students (#4 says "PhD students"); no resubmission fee under the current Guide for Authors; article-transfer exception applies; one half of the fee refunded when a manuscript is returned after a brief editor review (refund takes up to three months). Re-verified 2026-11-24 on #4 (direct) and #2 (search-engine rendering). (#2, #4)
- **Submission route:** Editorial Manager (`monec`). (#2, #3, #4)
- **Length/style:** "we do not enforce a page number limit at the submission stage", but accepted papers must not exceed 40 pages of text/references/footnotes and should not include more than 10 tables and figures combined (F+T<=10); double-spaced, 12-point type, 1-inch margins; figures and tables placed in the text near first reference; JME does not publish appendices (supplementary material on ScienceDirect). Re-verified 2026-11-24 on #4. (#4, #6)
- **Abstract / keywords / JEL:** JME style checklist: abstract no more than 100 words and should not start with "This paper" or "We"; up to five keywords; at least one JEL code. (#6, archived) The current Elsevier Guide for Authors (#2, search-engine rendering 2026-11-24) instead caps the abstract at **250 words** and asks for **1 to 7** keywords. The pack keeps the stricter JME house rules, which satisfy both.
- **First revision:** first invited revision operates under the "up or out" policy; resubmission is invited only when editors see at least a 50 percent chance of ultimate publication. (#4, #7)
- **Data and supplemental materials:** editors may require proofs, data sets, and computational programs as a condition of publication; Mendeley Data/ScienceDirect repository pathways support replication materials. (#2, #8)
- **AI policy:** generative-AI use in manuscript preparation must be declared; AI tools cannot be authors; reviewers/editors are not permitted to use generative AI for peer-review/manuscript evaluation under current Elsevier policy. (#2)

## UNVERIFIED / 待核实 (as of 2026-11-24)

- **Abstract and keyword limits — conflicting sources.** The 100-word / up-to-five-keyword rules come from the JME style checklist, whose host (jmeonline.org) is offline; the live Elsevier Guide (search-engine rendering only) says 250 words and 1-7 keywords. No live JME-run page restates the 100-word rule.
- **JEL codes** — required only by the archived JME style checklist; not surfaced from the current Elsevier Guide.
- **"Minimum of two reviewers"** — not re-confirmed 2026-11-24; the EIC interview (#10) says papers typically go to one referee.
- **Generative-AI declaration and data-statement wording** in the Elsevier Guide — not re-read 2026-11-24 (ScienceDirect blocked; search-engine rendering did not surface the section text).
