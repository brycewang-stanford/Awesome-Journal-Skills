## VERIFIED facts injected into the skills (with source #)

- **Field focus:** archival/empirical and analytical accounting across financial accounting, capital markets, auditing and assurance, management accounting, taxation, accounting information systems, and all areas of accounting broadly defined. (#1)
- **Publisher:** American Accounting Association (AAA), aaahq.org. (#6)
- **Senior Editor:** Mohan Venkatachalam (Duke University, Fuqua School of Business), **sitting Senior Editor since June 2026** (succeeding Kathryn Kadous, Emory University); Lead Editors Paul Hribar (Iowa), Edward Maydew (UNC), Brian Mittendorf (Ohio State), and Kristina Rennekamp (Cornell) share Senior-Editor duties; Ad hoc Editors appointed for specialized topics/methods. (#4, #7)
- **Submission system:** Editorial Manager at https://www.editorialmanager.com/accr. (#2, #5)
- **Submission fee:** non-refundable; **$285 AAA members / $680 non-members** per the current Guide for Authors (re-verified 2026-11-24 against the live AAA Guide-for-Authors page), paid via the AAA portal at submission; beginning June 1, 2026, half of the fee is refunded on a desk rejection. (#1, #2)
- **Length:** initial submissions and revisions should not exceed **55 pages** including references, figures, tables, and appendices; 12-pt Times New Roman, double-spaced, 1-inch margins, serially numbered pages. (#1, #3)
- **Abstract:** no more than **150 words**; the article file begins with the abstract. (#3)
- **AI disclosure:** every submission carries an AI disclosure statement (titled "Declaration of Generative AI and AI-Assisted Technologies in the Writing Process") placed immediately after the abstract and before the body, stating whether AI tools were used and, if so, the tools, extent and reasons; updated on every resubmission; AI used for data generation/analysis is also described in the methods section. (#2, #3)
- **Review model:** **double-blind** peer review; manuscripts passing initial screening go to an editor and a **minimum of two reviewers**; protecting author and reviewer anonymity is described as one of the most critical goals of the process. (Among AAA journals, only the Journal of Financial Reporting uses single-blind review.) (#1, #2)
- **House style:** The Chicago Manual of Style (16th ed.); Webster's Collegiate Dictionary for spelling; full author anonymization (no direct or indirect self-identification). (#3)
- **Data-authenticity / code-access regime (re-verified 2026-11-24):** differentiated data-description requirements by data type (subscription databases with download dates, public-source collection decisions, simulated/AI-simulated data, proprietary data); for TAR manuscripts conditionally accepted on or after July 1, 2026, a separate editors-only **Data Description Disclosures** list and **all computer code** (or a detailed description where code cannot legally be shared) are required at conditional acceptance; materials kept in a private AAA-managed repository; final datasets archived by authors for at least 7 years; public data posting strongly encouraged but not required. (#2)
- **Per-author submission cap:** individual authors limited to **eight (8) first-round submissions** over a rolling 24-month period; R&R papers do not count against the cap. (#1)
- **Author-byline cap:** no more than **10 authors** on the byline. (#2)
- **Research-integrity timing rule:** an Expression of Concern may be issued if an institutional investigation exceeds **180 days**. (#2)
- **ORCID + COI:** ORCID iD required for all authors; a completed Conflict of Interest form required at submission, conflicts stated in the cover letter, and a financial COI disclosure for each author on the separate title page; plagiarism-detection and Research Signals screening applied. (#1, #2, #3)
- **IRB / human subjects:** approval or exemption noted in a manuscript footnote and a copy uploaded at initial submission for experimental, survey and interview work (relevant to TAR's behavioral/experimental stream). (#1, #3)
- **Keywords / JEL:** keywords (minimum of three, maximum of 10), JEL classifications and a Data Availability Statement go on the **final** title page after acceptance; they are not initial-submission requirements. (#3)

## Bounded live-check rules

- Treat the current Guide for Authors fee amounts, **$285 (member) / $680 (non-member)** (re-verified 2026-11-24), as the pack's source of record, and re-open the AAA shop/payment flow only when preparing an actual submission.
- Use the aaahq.org Editorial Policy and Guide for Authors pages (#1, #2) as the stable policy authorities; the separate AllenPress/publications article mirrors are not required for the pack's load-bearing guidance.
- Do not enumerate the full standing-editor roster unless the current masthead page is open in the browser; the skills rely only on Senior Editor and Lead Editor facts.
- Do not describe archival/empirical work as TAR's official preferred methodology. The official policy is method-open; any mix-of-published-work statement is contextual only.
- Do not claim a named mandatory public repository deposit. The verified policy is data description, code submission at conditional acceptance (TAR, from July 1, 2026) into a private AAA repository, and 7-year author archiving, as described in #2.
