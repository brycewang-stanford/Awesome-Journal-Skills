## Verified KDD 2027 facts used in skills (re-verified 2026-11-23)

- KDD 2027 is held **August 1-5, 2027** at the **San Jose McEnery Convention Center**,
  San Jose, USA (source 1). The homepage does not state the edition number.
- KDD 2027 runs **two submission cycles**. Cycle 1 (all tracks): abstract **July 19,
  2026**, full paper **July 26, 2026**, rebuttal **September 29 - October 13, 2026**,
  notification **November 14, 2026**; end-of-day AoE, no extensions. Cycle 2 is "the
  February 2027 deadline", date not yet posted (sources 2-5).
- Four tracks: Research, Applied Data Science (ADS), Datasets and Benchmarks, AI for
  Sciences. A paper goes to Research **or** ADS, never both. KDD 2026's Blue Sky Ideas
  track is not linked from the 2027 homepage (sources 1, 3).
- **Review mode differs by track**: Research is **double-blind**; ADS, Datasets and
  Benchmarks, and AI for Sciences are **single-blind** with author names and
  affiliations in the PDF (sources 2-5).
- **Per-author caps per cycle**: Research 7, ADS 5, AI for Sciences 4, Datasets and
  Benchmarks 2; papers beyond the cap (by submission ID) are desk-rejected (sources 2-5).
- OpenReview manages submission and review; every listed author needs a complete
  OpenReview profile (affiliations back at least five years, homepage, DBLP if any prior
  publications, ORCID, advisors, recent publications) or the paper is desk-rejected;
  non-institutional-email profiles can take up to two weeks to be moderated; the author
  list, including order, is frozen at the abstract deadline (sources 2, 3).
- Research Track format: single PDF, English, double-column ACM template, recommended
  `\documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}`; **8 content pages** as the main
  paper, then references and an optional appendix with **no page limit** (reviewers need
  not read past page 8); format violations are desk-rejected (source 2).
- Accepted papers in every track get **12 pages total, of which 9 are content pages**,
  with references plus appendix capped at **3 pages** (sources 2-5).
- ADS submissions must **quantify post-launch performance**; without it they are
  desk-rejected, and offline-only tests on real data do not count. Exceptions exist for
  deployments demonstrably blocked (source 3).
- Authors respond to each review in the rebuttal period; **rebuttals and discussion do
  not allow hyperlinks**, and nothing can be shared with reviewers after the deadline, so
  the code repository must be referenced in the submission (sources 2, 3).
- Code: an anonymized repository is optional but "highly recommended"; on acceptance,
  public release is strongly encouraged, the ACM "Artifacts Available" badge needs an
  archival repository with a DOI, an optional artifact pledge can be made at submission
  (reneging can retract acceptance), and an artifact evaluation committee checks
  artifacts after acceptance (source 2).
- **Resubmit**: a paper that got Resubmit in 2026 Cycle 2 could return to 2027 Cycle 1
  with the prior OpenReview forum id and a one-page summary of changes as PDF page 1;
  some July 2026 submissions may get Resubmit for the February 2027 deadline (source 2).
- Every submission nominates at least one qualified reviewer (an author with at least
  three KDD-or-related papers); authors on several papers may be signed up as reviewers
  automatically (sources 2-4).
- Generative AI: the 2027 Research CFP defers to ACM's authorship policy and generative-AI
  FAQ and states **no separate author-side disclosure step** (the KDD 2026 Research CFP
  had one in the submission form); reviewers must disclose any generative-AI use and may
  not upload any passage of a paper to a generative-AI tool. The Datasets and Benchmarks
  track still requires disclosure in the submission form, and AI for Sciences requires a
  "Generative AI Usage" section (sources 2, 4, 5).
- Anonymized preprints (e.g., arXiv, SSRN) may be submitted without citing them; work
  under review at or published in a venue with proceedings may not (source 2).
- Every accepted paper must be presented, with a Full Conference (5-day) in-person
  registration at the standard (non-student) rate (sources 2, 3).
- ACM publishes KDD proceedings in the Digital Library. Under ACM Open, about 76% of
  ACM-sponsored conference papers need no APC; other authors pay unless waived, and the
  **2027 subsidized APC is USD 500 for ACM/SIG members and USD 750 for non-members** (a
  29% discount off list) (sources 2, 3, 13).
- KDD 2027 chairs are listed in source 7. Chairs rotate per edition.

## KDD 2026 (history, verified 2026-07-08)

- KDD 2026 was the 32nd ACM SIGKDD Conference, August 9-13, 2026, ICC Jeju, Korea.
  Cycle 1: abstract July 24, 2025, full paper July 31, 2025; Cycle 2: abstract February
  1, 2026, full paper February 8, 2026. It fielded Research, ADS, Datasets and
  Benchmarks, AI for Sciences, and Blue Sky Ideas tracks; the 2026 Research CFP required
  generative-AI disclosure in the submission form, and ACM announced a temporary 2026 APC
  subsidy of 65%. These are superseded by the 2027 facts above (sources 8-12).
