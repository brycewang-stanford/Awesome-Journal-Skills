# External Tools - KDD

Access date: 2026-07-08; links re-verified 2026-11-23 against the KDD 2027 pages.

Open these after confirming which submission cycle and which track (Research or Applied
Data Science) the paper targets. KDD's dual-cycle calendar means "the current CFP" is
ambiguous unless the cycle is named first.

## Official workflow

- KDD 2027 conference site: https://kdd2027.kdd.org/
- Research Track CFP: https://kdd2027.kdd.org/research-track-call-for-papers/
- Applied Data Science (ADS) Track CFP: https://kdd2027.kdd.org/applied-data-science-ads-track-call-for-papers/
- Datasets and Benchmarks Track CFP: https://kdd2027.kdd.org/datasets-and-benchmarks-track-call-for-papers/
- AI for Sciences Track CFP: https://kdd2027.kdd.org/ai4sciences-track-call-for-papers/
- Organizing committee: https://kdd2027.kdd.org/acm-kdd-2027-organizing-committee/
- OpenReview groups (per track x cycle), as printed in the KDD 2027 Cycle 1 CFPs:
  `KDD.org/2027/Research_Track_Cycle_1`, `KDD.org/2027/Applied_Data_Science_Track_Cycle_1`,
  `KDD.org/2027/Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track_Cycle_1`,
  `KDD.org/2027/AI4Sciences_Track_Cycle_1` (Cycle 2 group ids not yet posted; KDD 2026
  history: https://openreview.net/group?id=KDD.org/2026/Research_Track_Cycle_2)
- ACM Digital Library KDD proceedings series: https://dl.acm.org/ (e.g.
  https://dl.acm.org/doi/proceedings/10.1145/3770854 for KDD '26)
- SIGKDD organization site: https://kdd.org/

## Author-side checks

- Track choice locked before writing: Research vs ADS vs Datasets and Benchmarks vs AI
  for Sciences — the same PDF cannot go to Research and ADS simultaneously.
- Cycle choice: which abstract/paper deadline pair applies, in AoE time (KDD 2027
  Cycle 1 closed July 26, 2026; Cycle 2 is "the February 2027 deadline", date not yet
  posted).
- Per-author cap for the chosen track (KDD 2027: Research 7, ADS 5, AI for Sciences 4,
  Datasets and Benchmarks 2 per cycle) and the reviewer nomination.
- OpenReview profile completeness for **every** author: five-year affiliation history,
  homepage, DBLP, ORCID, advisors, recent publications.
- ACM sigconf template with the anonymous/review options; 8-content-page budget plus
  references and optional appendix (Research Track, KDD 2027). Research is
  double-blind; ADS, Datasets and Benchmarks and AI for Sciences are single-blind in
  KDD 2027, so their PDFs carry author names (use `\documentclass[sigconf,review]{acmart}`).
- Code/data repository referenced inside the submission, because rebuttals cannot carry
  links.
- ADS only: post-launch performance quantification present, or a documented
  blocked-from-deployment justification.
- Resubmissions: previous OpenReview forum id declared and a one-page change summary
  prepended to the PDF.
- Generative-AI: follow ACM policy; the KDD 2027 Research CFP has no separate author
  disclosure step, but Datasets and Benchmarks requires disclosure in the submission
  form and AI for Sciences a "Generative AI Usage" section.
- After acceptance: ACM e-rights form, TAPS source upload, ACM Open APC or subsidy
  status, registration, and presentation obligations.

## Do not infer

- Do not project KDD 2026 cycle dates onto KDD 2027 Cycle 2 or later editions; even
  the number of cycles can change.
- Do not assume Research Track page budgets apply to ADS, Datasets and Benchmarks, or
  AI for Sciences submissions — each track publishes its own CFP.
- Do not assume rebuttal mechanics (per-review response, link ban, Resubmit option)
  survive unchanged between cycles of the same edition.
- If a track CFP and an OpenReview announcement disagree, treat the newer OpenReview
  instruction or direct chair communication as controlling, and record the conflict in
  the project log.
