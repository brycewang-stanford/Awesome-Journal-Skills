## Verified facts
- **Editor-in-Chief (re-verified 2026-11-23):** H. Holden Thorp, EiC of the *Science* family of journals (in this role since 28 Oct 2019); chemist, on leave from George Washington University. (#2, #3, #9)
- **Publisher / owner:** American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS), Washington, D.C. (UK office in Cambridge). General multidisciplinary weekly. (#1, #6)
- **APC / fees:** *Science* is subscription/hybrid — there is **no required author APC to publish** a Research Article under the standard model. AAAS offers paid open-access/CC-BY options on selected article types and supports transformative agreements; **figures vary by article type and agreement — confirm the live charge on the AAAS author/open-access page before relying on a number.** (#1)
- **ISSN:** 0036-8075 (print) / 1095-9203 (online). (#4, #5)
- **Submission portal / review model:** Submit via the Content Tracking System at https://cts.sciencemag.org. Editor-triaged: most papers are rated by the Board of Reviewing Editors; papers selected for in-depth review go to at least two outside reviewers, followed by a cross-review step in which reviewers read the other (still anonymous) reviews. External review is single-blind by convention (recorded 2026-06-23; the author pages do not use the term). (#1, #7)

### Research Articles — re-verified 2026-11-23 (#7, #8)
- **Length:** "Research Articles should not exceed 3000 words (for the main text), plus an abstract, 3 to 5 display items (figures or tables) with brief legends, about 50 main-text references, and a structured acknowledgments section." Materials and Methods go in the supplementary materials. Extended online format: main text up to 6000 words, as many as 6 display items (may include videos), up to 100 main-text references; the cover letter should say why the extra length is merited. Papers not fitting print (often more than 4 figures) appear online in full with a ~500-word one-page print summary.
- **Abstract:** "The abstract should be 125 words or less"; no citations or abbreviations; one paragraph (no paragraph breaks) moving through background, objectives/methods, results, conclusions.
- **Title:** no more than 96 characters including spaces; Short Title 40-character maximum.
- **Acknowledgments (required headings):** Funding (all authors); Author contributions (initials, CRediT encouraged); Competing interests (declared even when none; all authors also fill the Conflict of Interest form); Data and materials availability (all data, code and materials available for reproduction).
- **Cover letter:** the only required file on the Related Documents tab; states the main point, related manuscripts, data availability and any MTA restrictions, consent / animal-care statements where relevant.
- **Files:** single .docx (Word template preferred) with embedded figures plus a combined PDF; LaTeX users convert to .docx or submit PDF; supplementary materials as one .docx/PDF file ≤50 MB.
- **References:** numbered in order of citation, italic numbers in parentheses, single combined list.
