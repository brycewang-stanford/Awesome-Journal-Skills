# RFS Official Source Map

Source-of-truth map for the RFS-specific facts encoded in this skill pack. Each row lists an
official (or, where flagged, third-party) URL, what it verifies, and the access date. RFS
policies, editors, and fees change — re-verify against these URLs before relying on a number.

**All access dates: 2026-05-30** unless a row says otherwise. Submission rules, fees, the code
and data policy, statistics and the editorial team were **re-verified 2026-11-23** against the
live sfs.org pages (read directly). The academic.oup.com/rfs pages return HTTP 403 to plain
`curl` from this environment; on 2026-11-23 the Instructions to Authors were read through the
WebFetch tool, and the OUP Registered Reports page returned **HTTP 404**. Changes found on
2026-11-23: a new **Code and Data Sharing Policy** (papers conditionally accepted on/after
1 October 2025) with RFS Data Editors; explicit **anonymous-manuscript, alt-text, no-endnote and
Internet-Appendix-inside-the-PDF** rules on the SFS Guidelines for Submissions; an
**AI-Assistance Author Responsibility Statement**; David Sraer and the two Data Editors on the
masthead; the Registered Reports page is gone (see UNVERIFIED).

## Verified on official SFS pages (corrects earlier aggregator guesses)

- **Submission system: SFS Editorial Express** (not ScholarOne or Editorial Manager) — sfs.org/review-of-financial-studies/submit-a-paper.
- **Fee (CONFIRMED 2026-06-22, hedge resolved):** the SFS schedule effective **8 July 2024** is tiered by SFS membership and World Bank income band — **$400 SFS member / $460 non-member** for high-income economies (the figure that superseded the older flat $260/$320, now the middle-income tier). Re-verify the exact current schedule on sfs.org/review-of-financial-studies/submit-a-paper before submitting.
- **Abstract ≤ 100 words; double-spaced, ≤ 28 lines/page, font ≥ 11; anonymous manuscript; alt text under figure captions; no endnotes; Internet Appendix inside the manuscript PDF** (SFS submission-format and Guidelines for Submissions pages, re-verified 2026-11-23). **Chicago Manual of Style author-date** references come from the OUP Instructions to Authors, which SFS says govern accepted papers.
- **Code-release condition** (academic.oup.com/rfs Instructions to Authors), implemented for papers conditionally accepted on/after 1 October 2025 through the SFS **Code and Data Sharing Policy** with Data-Editor reproduction checks.

  academic.oup.com/rfs/pages/registered_reports returned HTTP 404; the OUP Instructions to Authors
  do not mention Registered Reports; on sfs.org the only "Registered Reports Repository" link

  on the OUP RFS open-access page before relying on it.

## Notes

- The exact current membership/fee schedule, abstract limit, and reference edition should be
  re-checked on the official RFS/SFS pages before each submission.
- The Karolyi 2014 editorial title and RFS's exact "first in finance" Registered Reports
  claim are stated cautiously in the skills; the Registered Reports track itself is
  confirmed via the RFS Registered Reports / Submit a Report pages.
