## Verified on official pages (2026-08-09)

- **Editor-in-Chief: Monideepa Tarafdar** (effective 2024-09-01). (AIS announcement / JAIS editorial board)
- Published by the **Association for Information Systems (AIS)**; **AIS Green Open Access** (authors may self-archive the accepted paper with no embargo), and JAIS articles are free to AIS members. The official AISeL format page currently links authors to the JAIS ScholarOne review system for submission.
- The official format page currently directs articles to the JAIS ScholarOne review system and describes double-blind review; it also asks SEM manuscripts to supply a full correlation/covariance matrix and permits editors/reviewers to request the dataset for validation.
