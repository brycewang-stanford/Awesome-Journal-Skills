# AEJ: Microeconomics — Official Source Map

Primary and authoritative sources behind every AEJ: Micro-specific fact injected into
this skill pack. **Access date for the original entries: 2026-06.** AEJ: Micro is owned and
operated by the **American Economic Association (AEA)**; replication is administered by
the **Office of the AEA Data Editor** and deposited in the **AEA Data and Code Repository
on openICPSR**. Submission rules, review mode, fees, style guide, data/code and disclosure
policies, and the masthead were **re-verified 2026-11-24** against the live AEA pages listed
below (all read directly). Changes found on 2026-11-24: **submissions must now be
author-anonymized** (AEA three-year RCT of author-anonymized review, launched fall 2026), so
the pack's earlier "single-blind — no anonymization required" advice is wrong for the
manuscript itself; the length guidance is now a strongly recommended **maximum of 45 pages**
at a minimum of 12-point font, 1.5 spacing and 1-inch margins, all pages numbered (average
article 36–37 typeset pages); JEL codes are still not stated as a requirement (see 需复核);
the portal is **ScholarOne Manuscripts**. Editor, coeditors and fees were unchanged.
Re-verify volatile items before relying on them — AEA pages change. Items not confirmed
from a primary fetch are marked **需复核 (to be verified)**.

## Official AEA / AEJ: Micro pages

- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mic** — AEJ: Micro journal home (re-read 2026-11-24).
  Verifies: published by the **American Economic Association**; **Print ISSN 1945-7669 /
  Online ISSN 1945-7685**; scope — the journal "focuses on microeconomic theory; industrial
  organization; and the microeconomic aspects of international trade, political economy,
  and finance" and "publishes theoretical work as well as both empirical and experimental
  work with a theoretical framework"; **four issues per year** (quarterly).
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mic/editors** — AEJ: Micro editorial leadership.
  **Re-verified 2026-11-24 (unchanged):** **Editor: Navin Kartik (Yale)**; **Coeditors:
  S. Nageeb Ali (Penn State), Alessandro Bonatti (MIT), Kareen Rozen (Brown), Daniel Yi Xu
  (Duke)**; a 20-member Board of Editors; **Data Editor: Lars Vilhuber (Cornell)**; Managing
  Editor Michelle DeBlasi (AEA); Assistant Managing Editor Justin Shapiro; past coeditors
  including Alexander Wolitzky (2022–2025).
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mic/submissions** — AEJ: Micro Submission Guidelines
  (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: original work, no concurrent submission; CrossRef
  Similarity Check; AEA Disclosure Policy; AI may not be an author and AI use in preparing
  the manuscript, including drafting or editing text, "must be briefly described during
  the submission process"; "Prior to acceptance, authors of papers that contain **empirical
  work, simulations, or experimental work** must provide the data, code, and other details
  sufficient to permit replication. All submissions must comply with the **AEA Data and
  Code Availability Policy**"; field experiments registered in the AEA RCT Registry.
  **Submission fee** — AEA members **$200** (high-income) / **$100** (middle-income) / **$0**
  (low-income), nonmembers **$300 / $200 / $0**, by credit card; **papers rejected without
  review refunded 50%**; **publication fee $15 per typeset page** at page proofs (papers
  first submitted on or after February 1, 2024). **Length:** average article 36–37 typeset
  pages; "We strongly recommend that manuscripts not exceed 45 pages", including figures,
  tables, references, and in-paper appendices, with a minimum of **12-point font, 1.5
  spacing, and 1-inch margins** applied to the whole manuscript (references may be single
  spaced) and all pages numbered. **Manuscript preparation (changed fall 2026):** "The AEA
  has launched a three-year randomized controlled trial of the effects of author-anonymized
  peer reviewing, commonly referred to as 'double-blind'"; an **abstract of 100 or fewer
  words is required**; PDF under 10 MB when reasonably possible; style guide optional at
  submission; manuscripts **must be anonymized** — title and abstract at the top of the
  first page; author names, affiliations, contact information and the acknowledgement
  footnote in a separate PDF labelled "title page"; self-citations by name, never "my/our";
  no identifying locations, IRB approvals, or personal/institutional website links; no
  RCT-registration entry in the reference list; supplementary appendices and response
  letters anonymized. Submission on **ScholarOne Manuscripts**
  (https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/aej-micro). A cover letter "is not required and should
  only be used to provide substantive information" (coeditor conflicts, data-access
  limitations), preferably in the form field. Each (co)author's **Disclosure Statement**
  PDF must accompany the manuscript; submissions without them "will be considered
  incomplete and will not be reviewed." AEA templates optional (in "review mode");
  Chicago Manual author-date preferred; AER / AER: Insights transfer option.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mic/editorial-policy** — AEJ: Micro Editorial Policy
  (re-read 2026-11-24). The page **still says** reviews are **single-blind** — "the name of
  the author is revealed to the referee(s), while the referee(s) remain anonymous to
  authors" — i.e., not yet updated for the anonymization RCT. Also verifies coeditor
  assignment, return without referee review, AEA Data Editor review of conditionally
  accepted papers, and reviewer AI-disclosure rules.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/news/rct-announcement** — AEA announcement, 10 September 2026
  (re-read 2026-11-24): three-year RCT of author-anonymized ("double-blind") reviewing at
  the AER, AER: Insights and the four AEJs, designed by Amy Finkelstein and J-PAL North
  America; launch expected fall 2026 (the submissions page says it has launched).
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mic/reviewers** — AEJ: Micro Reviewer Guidelines
  (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies that reviewers remain anonymous to authors; the page no
  longer describes the process as single-blind.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mic/style-guide** — AEJ: Micro style guide (re-read
  2026-11-24). Verifies: abstract "must not exceed 100 words"; footnotes, not endnotes;
  Chicago author-date citations; tables no more than 9 columns wide; "Do not use
  asterisks to denote significance of estimation results. Report the standard errors in
  parentheses"; RCT registration cited in the acknowledgement footnote and reference list
  (accepted articles); appendices A, B, C with equations numbered (A1), (A2).
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mic/accepted-article-guidelines** — Accepted article
  guidelines (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: title and byline at the top of the first page
  **for accepted manuscripts**; native files; signed Data and Code Availability Form and
  Archive Agreement; individual disclosure PDFs; separate Supplemental Appendix PDF.

## AEA data / code regime (shared across AEA journals)

- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/data/data-code-policy** — AEA Data and Code
  Availability Policy, **February 2026 version** (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies
  pre-publication reproducibility checks by the Data Editor, trusted-repository deposit
  (AEA Data and Code Repository), README and Data Availability Statement requirements,
  and restricted-data exceptions.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/data/faq** and the **Office of the AEA Data Editor**
  (**https://aeadataeditor.github.io/**) — AEA Data and Code Availability Policy. Verifies:
  the AEA adopted a revised Data and Code Availability Policy (2019) with **pre-publication
  reproducibility verification** by the Data Editor team; **Lars Vilhuber** has served as
  AEA Data Editor since 2018; materials are deposited in the **AEA Data and Code Repository
  on openICPSR** (each receives a DOI and metadata including JEL codes). The policy applies
  to papers with data, code, experiments, or numerical results; pure-theory papers still
  deposit any numerical/simulation code. Accessed 2026-06.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/disclosure-policy** — AEA Disclosure Policy
  (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies a separate "Disclosure Statement" PDF per (co)author,
  the US$10,000 interested-party threshold over three years, publication of the statements,
  and IRB-status disclosure.

## Unverified / to-confirm (需复核)

- **Exact founding year (2009)** — AEJ: Micro launched with the other AEJ field journals in
  2009; the journal home and editors pages (re-read 2026-11-24) still do not state the
  founding year as static text (the editors page lists the first editor from 2007), so the
  2009 date is from general AEA history and is **需复核** against the official page.
- **JEL-code requirement** — AEA practice and openICPSR metadata use JEL codes, but none of
  the AEJ: Micro submissions, style-guide, accepted-article or editorial-policy pages
  (re-read 2026-11-24) states a JEL requirement. The pack treats JEL codes as
  **customary**; the ScholarOne form itself was not inspected — **需复核**.
- **How long the anonymized-submission rule lasts.** The RCT is announced as three years
  from fall 2026; whether the editorial-policy page's single-blind wording will be updated
  was not stated as of 2026-11-24.

of Editors is listed on the editors page.)
