## Verified ICML 2027 facts used in skills (re-verified 2026-11-23)

- Main-track submission uses OpenReview (`ICML.cc/2027/Conference`) and the `icml2027` LaTeX
  style; no non-LaTeX typesetting support (sources 1, 3).
- Submission main body limit is 8 pages; references, the impact statement and unlimited
  appendices follow in the same single PDF and do not count (sources 1, 3).
- Submission PDF limit is 50MB; camera-ready PDF limit is 20MB (source 3).
- Supplementary material has two classes: supplementary manuscripts and code/data. It is due with
  the paper (no separate deadline) and must be anonymized (sources 1, 3).
- Code/data are encouraged for reproducibility; anonymized repositories are allowed, but their
  branch must not be modified after the submission deadline (source 3).
- All submissions are double-blind; author identity leaks in paper or supplement can lead to
  rejection. Preprints and talks are allowed; advertising the work as an ICML submission during
  review is forbidden (sources 1, 3).
- **Two-stage review is new for 2027**: an area chair decides whether a paper proceeds, a senior
  area chair must confirm any first-stage rejection, and first-stage rejections (with a short
  justification) arrive by February 23, 2027 (source 1).
- **Contribution types replace the separate position track**: authors pick one of seven types,
  including Position Papers, and reviewers judge against that type's standard (sources 1, 8).
- Author response: three author-reviewer discussion rounds (rebuttal, reviewer follow-up, author
  follow-up), each capped at 5,000 characters; no revised-paper upload during feedback
  (sources 3, 7).
- Every paper requires an **impact statement** (separate section before References, not counted)
  and an **AI Disclosure Statement** (end of paper, not counted); accepted papers require a lay
  summary in OpenReview (sources 1, 3, 4).
- Quotas: at most 20 submissions per author; one designated reciprocal reviewer per submission,
  covering at most 6 submissions; authors with 3 or more submissions serve on the program
  committee; authors with multiple submissions must self-rank them (sources 1, 5).
- Reviewer LLM use: ICML 2027 applies Policy B (privacy-compliant assistance only) to all
  reviewing; the 2026 author-chosen Policy A option is not offered (source 6).
- Camera-ready main body allows 9 pages and must include appendices in the PDF; there is no
  camera-ready supplementary material; a financial Conflict of Interest Disclosure paragraph is
  required where applicable (source 3).
- Accepted papers choose in-person presentation or proceedings-only status; proceedings-only
  papers still need one virtual registration (source 1).
- Accepted papers' reviews and discussions are published on OpenReview; rejected papers may opt
  in (source 7).
- Concurrent ICML submissions with overlapping authors are treated as prior work (source 1).
- Generative AI may not be an author; authors remain responsible for all content; prompt
  injection is prohibited (sources 1, 4).

## Organizers and fee model (re-verified 2026-11-23)

- ICML is run by the International Machine Learning Society (IMLS). Like every CS/AI conference in
  this collection, it has NO standing editor-in-chief; the rotating analogue is the per-edition
  General Chair and Program Chairs. ICML 2027 General Chair is Marina Meila (University of
  Waterloo); Program Chairs are Julien Mairal, Andrew Wilson, Weijie Su, Furong Huang and Courtney
  Paquette (source 9). ICML 2026 General Chair was Tong Zhang; Program Chairs were Miroslav Dudik,
  Martin Jaggi, Alekh Agarwal and Sharon (Yixuan) Li (source H8).
- There is NO article-processing charge (APC). ICML funds the conference through registration fees.
  Accepted papers are published open-access in PMLR (Proceedings of Machine Learning Research),
  which charges no author fee (source 1).
