## Verified ASE 2027 facts used in skills

- **ASE 2027 is the 42nd IEEE/ACM International Conference on Automated Software Engineering**,
  **El Paso, Texas, USA, October 24-28, 2027** (source 3).
- **Research-track calendar (single deadline):** paper submission **10 March 2027**; early
  rejection notification **19 May 2027**; author response **19-24 May 2027**; initial notification
  **10 June 2027**; major-revision submission **8 July 2027**; final notification **27 July 2027**;
  camera-ready **11 August 2027**; all AoE, via `ase2027.hotcrp.com` (sources 1, 2, 5). The call
  describes an abstract stage (title, abstract, areas, conflicts) but the dates page lists no
  separate abstract date — **待核实**.
- **Format is IEEE for 2027** — the IEEE Conference Proceedings Formatting Guidelines: 24pt title,
  10pt text, LaTeX `\documentclass[10pt,conference]{IEEEtran}` **without** `compsoc` or
  `compsocconf` (source 1). ASE 2026 used ACM `acmart` sigconf (source 6): the template follows the
  dual IEEE/ACM sponsorship and flips between editions.
- **Page budget:** **10 pages** of main text (figures, tables, appendices included) **plus up to 2
  pages** containing references **and the Data Availability statement**; violations are
  desk-rejected. Camera-ready: **11 pages** + 2 (source 1). Unlike ASE 2026, the Data Availability
  statement no longer counts inside the 10 pages.
- **Data Availability section:** authors are asked to state the availability of a replication
  package (or why there is none) in a section named **Data Availability** after the Conclusion; it
  does not count toward the page limit and must stay anonymous (source 1).
- **Review is double-anonymous**, enforced by desk rejection; anonymity continues through the
  major-revision response letter (source 1).
- **Review model:** **early rejection** before rebuttal for papers with all negative scores; then
  **Accept / Major revision / Reject**. A Major revision is criteria-bound: reviewers specify
  concrete, actionable criteria needing up to four weeks and commit to accept in principle if they
  are met; authors return a revised paper with a **point-by-point summary of changes**, re-checked
  by the discussion lead (source 1). ASE 2026 called this outcome "Revision" (source 6).
- **Review criteria (2027):** Significance, Novelty, Soundness, Verifiability, Presentation (source 1).
- **Generative AI (2027 follows IEEE policy):** AI-generated content in the article must be
  disclosed in the **acknowledgments**, naming the system and the sections it produced; AI for
  editing and grammar is exempt from mandatory disclosure (recommended). Reviewers may not upload
  manuscripts to public AI platforms (source 1).
- **Human-subjects research:** IEEE policy requires an IRB/ethics statement (naming the body) and a
  consent statement, or an explanation of why not (source 1).
- **Publisher of record for 2027:** the official publication date is when the proceedings appear
  in the **IEEE Digital Library** (source 1); ASE 2026's was the ACM Digital Library (source 6).
- **Tracks (2026 pattern, 2027 calls pending):** Research Papers; **NIER** (4 pages; source 9);
  **Tools and Datasets** (≤4 pages; source 10); **Journal-First** (TSE / TOSEM / EMSE; source 11);
  Industry Showcase; Doctoral Symposium; workshops. **Artifact Evaluation** offered **Available** and
  **Reusable** badges in 2026 (source 12); ASE 2027 announces a separate artifact evaluation committee.
- **ASE 2026** (history) was the 41st edition, Munich, Germany, October 12-16, 2026; **ASE 2025**
  was the 40th, Seoul, November 16-20, 2025 (sources 7, 13).
- **The ASE Most Influential Paper award** revisits an influential paper from ~15 years prior
  (window transitioning to ~10 years); ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper awards are also given at
  ASE (source 14).

## The Accept/Major-revision/Reject model (honest reading of the "two-round" question)

ASE's research track is not a single accept/reject vote. Two mechanisms make it a two-stage
process: (1) an **early-rejection stage** that ends the process before rebuttal for papers with
uniformly negative initial scores; and (2) a first-class **Major revision** outcome (called
"Revision" in 2026) in which reviewers commit to acceptance-in-principle against explicit criteria
and re-read a revised paper plus its summary-of-changes. This is closer to a lightweight journal
revision than to a one-shot conference rebuttal. ASE 2027 has a single submission deadline; verify
the cadence on the current Important Dates page and never carry a previous year's structure forward.

## No editor-in-chief; dual sponsorship

ASE has **no standing editor-in-chief**; the rotating analogue is the per-edition General Chair and
Program Co-Chairs (for 2027: General Chair Marcelo F. Frias; PC Co-Chairs Jie M. Zhang and Aldeida
Aleti; source 4). ASE is **jointly sponsored by IEEE and ACM SIGSOFT**; which publisher's template
and digital library is "of record" alternates by edition (ACM for 2026, IEEE for 2027).
