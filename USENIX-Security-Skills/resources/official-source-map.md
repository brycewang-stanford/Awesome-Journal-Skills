## Verified USENIX Security '27 facts used in skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

- **USENIX Security '27** is the 36th Symposium, August 11-13, 2027, at the Hyatt Regency
  Denver at Colorado Convention Center, Denver, CO. Program co-chairs: Adam Doupé (Arizona
  State University) and Andrei Sabelfeld (Chalmers University of Technology) (sources 1, 2).
- **Two cycles, all deadlines AoE.** Cycle 1: registration Aug 18, 2026; submission Aug 25,
  2026; artifacts Aug 28; early reject Oct 6; rebuttal Nov 5-12; notification Dec 3; shepherded
  approval Dec 15, 2026; finals Jan 14, 2027. Cycle 2: registration Jan 19, 2027; submission
  Jan 26, 2027; artifacts Jan 29; early reject Mar 9; rebuttal Apr 8-15; notification May 6;
  shepherded approval May 18; finals Jun 3, 2027 (source 1).
- **Registration is binding.** One week before the deadline: fixed title, fixed full author
  list with ORCIDs, a tentative non-blank abstract, and fixed topics. By submission time every
  author must have an ORCID in their HotCRP profile and confirm the submission terms, or the
  paper is not reviewed (source 1).
- **Format.** Main body ≤ 13 pages, excluding references and appendices, in the USENIX
  Security template (US letter, two columns, 10-point Times on 12-point leading, 7" x 9" text
  block); unlimited references and appendices at submission, which reviewers need not read;
  whitespace tampering forbidden. Camera-ready papers must fit in **20 pages total**, including
  the Ethical Considerations and Open Science appendices, bibliography and any other
  appendices (source 1).
- **Open Science appendix is mandatory.** Every submission includes an "Open Science"
  appendix describing its artifacts and how to access them, or why they cannot be provided.
  Artifacts are shared through anonymous, untracked links (anonymous.4open.science with
  conference ID "SEC27" is recommended); URLs are final at the deadline, with a 3-day grace
  period to upload, after which artifacts are frozen. For shareable artifacts, acceptance is
  conditional on public availability, re-verified by the AEC (sources 1, 3).
- **Ethics appendix is strongly encouraged, no longer mandatory** (a change from '26).
  Reviewers evaluate the ethics of every paper; papers may be rejected on ethical grounds; the
  '27 call points to the '26 ethics guidance (sources 1, 5).
- **Outcomes**: Accepted; Accepted on Shepherd Approval (text changes, clarifications,
  explicit limitations); Rejected. Major Revision, retired in '26, does not return (sources 1, 5).
- **Rebuttal** periods exist in both cycles (Nov 5-12, 2026; Apr 8-15, 2027) (source 1).
- **Resubmission**: papers rejected from either '26 cycle may be submitted to '27; papers
  rejected from '27 Cycle 1 may not go to '27 Cycle 2 (source 1).
- **At most 7 papers per author per cycle**, checked right after submission; extras beyond the
  seven lowest submission numbers are dropped (source 1).
- **AI use** is permitted; authors are responsible for all content and must review AI output as
  if they wrote it; fabricated or hallucinated references, claims or results are academic
  misconduct and may be desk rejected (source 1).
- **SoK papers** are solicited, titled with the prefix "SoK: "; **Replication submissions are
  not accepted in '27** (source 1).
- **Double-blind**: no names or affiliations; third-person self-citation; artifact links must
  not reveal identity or track reviewers; preprints, talks and vendor disclosure are allowed
  during review (source 1).
- **Related and overlapping work**: own work under submission must be cited (anonymized, with
  non-anonymous versions emailed to the chairs); failure to explain overlap is grounds for
  rejection (source 1).
- **Two-phase artifact evaluation.** Phase 1 (availability) is mandatory for (conditionally)
  accepted papers, including shepherded ones; Phase 2 (Artifacts Functional, Results
  Reproduced) is optional after finals (source 3).
- **Attendance and publication.** One author registers at full (non-student) price per paper
  and presents. Cycle 1 papers go online after the Cycle 1 finals deadline, Cycle 2 papers on
  the first day of the symposium; embargo requests by Feb 11, 2027 (Cycle 1) or Jul 1, 2027
  (Cycle 2). Authors keep copyright; USENIX is first publisher. Final versions list all funding
  sources and relevant interests (source 1).
- **USENIX ATC ended after 2025**, making USENIX Security USENIX's flagship summer event
  (source 9).
- **Open access.** USENIX publishes Security proceedings (and talk recordings) open access with
  no author fee (sources 1, 10 and the USENIX open-access policy).

## '26 history (kept for orientation)

- USENIX Security '26 was the 35th Symposium, August 12-14, 2026, Baltimore Marriott
  Waterfront; program co-chairs Elissa Redmiles and Ben Stock. Cycle 1 submission Aug 26,
  2025; Cycle 2 submission Feb 5, 2026. Ethical Considerations and Open Science appendices
  were both mandatory (source 5).
