## VERIFIED facts injected into the skills (with source #)

- **Publisher / identity:** Oxford University Press; JCR is a multi-disciplinary journal founded 1974 whose overriding criterion is advancing understanding of consumer behavior or the conduct of consumer research, drawing on psychology, anthropology, sociology, economics, marketing, statistics, and communication. (#1)
- **Sponsoring structure:** owned by a nonprofit corporation governed by a Policy Board of representatives appointed by the journal's sponsoring professional organizations, institutionalizing the multi-disciplinary identity. (#1)
- **Submission system:** ScholarOne Manuscripts (ManuscriptCentral) at **mc.manuscriptcentral.com/jconres**. (#2, #3)
- **Fees:** no submission or publication fee; the only charges are optional (open-access licensing and print reproduction of colour figures at **$600 per figure**). (#8)
- **Length:** manuscript may not exceed **60 double-spaced pages**, inclusive of title, Consumer Relevance and Contribution Statement, abstract and keywords, main text (with tables and figures embedded), Data and Materials Availability Statement, appendices, and references. A web appendix (supplementary materials, max **40 MB**) is distinct from a manuscript appendix and does **not** count toward the 60-page cap. (#3)
- **Abstract and keywords:** abstract ≤ **200 words** (entered in ScholarOne and identical in the manuscript), followed by **3–6 keywords** in a separate paragraph. (#3)
- **Consumer Relevance and Contribution Statement:** ≤ **300 words**, required for new submissions only (not for invited revisions); entered at Step 6 and placed in the manuscript before the abstract. (#3)
- **Review model:** **double-anonymized** (masked); authors must fully anonymize (no author note, acknowledgments, funding or COI in the manuscript; anonymized repository links; generic phrasing such as "large public university"); self-citations by name are expected for publicly available work but must not signal authorship. (#3)
- **Style and files:** Word, Rich Text, or PDF upload (LaTeX users upload a PDF), ≤ 40 MB; follows the JCR style guide, and for matters not covered, the **Chicago Manual of Style** (e.g., title capitalization, author-date referencing). (#3)
- **Research transparency (policy revised 2026-09-24):** required confidential **Step-6 Data Collection Statement** on all submissions; required in-manuscript **Data and Materials Availability Statement** on all submissions including the original one; anonymized data available to the review team by default and to readers after acceptance, preferably in a third-party repository (e.g., Dataverse); stimuli, measures, protocols and code shared or non-sharing explained; data and materials kept accessible **≥ 7 years** after publication. (#5)
- **AI use:** disclosure per the OUP policy and the JCR AI policy — at Step 6 and in the relevant manuscript section; AI cannot be an author. (#3, #5, #7)
- **Editor in Chief:** Oleg Urminsky (University of Chicago Booth), term **Jan 1, 2025 – Dec 31, 2028**; Co-Editors Jacob Goldenberg, Ellie Kyung, Meg Meloy, Daiane Scaraboto, Stephen Spiller; Special Guest Editor Page Moreau (to June 30, 2029). (#4)
- **Expanded formats (Jan 1, 2025):** Registered Reports and Brief Commentaries in addition to the standard empirical paper. (#6)
- **Preregistration:** not required for standard submissions; encouraged for experiments that test specific hypotheses, with links included in the manuscript. (#5)

## Items flagged approximate or 需复核 (confirm via sources above)

- **Co-editor roster** — confirmed live on 2026-11-24 (#4); time-sensitive, verify before naming editors in a cover letter.
- **Manuscript routing / desk-assignment mechanism** — 需复核. JCR uses Co-Editors who handle full manuscript portfolios plus Associate Editors and an Editorial Review Board, and authors may suggest up to three associate editors in the cover letter (#3), but the official pages do not describe a formal fixed departmental "area-editor routing" structure.
- **Dominant methodology characterization** — characterization, not quoted policy. The pairing of multi-study behavioral experiments with qualitative/CCT interpretive work is an inference from JCR's stated multi-disciplinary scope and well-known publishing patterns; JCR's official guidelines deliberately state **no single preferred method**.
- **Final accepted-version file format** — 需复核. The 2026-06-01 reading of #2 said the final version is supplied in Word; the 2026-11-24 OUP landing page no longer states this, and #3 covers only review-stage uploads.
- **Impact factor / acceptance rate** — 需复核; not confirmed on an official page. Treat any single number as approximate.
- Removed 2026-11-24: the "standalone preregistration mandate" item — #5 now states preregistration is not required but encouraged.
