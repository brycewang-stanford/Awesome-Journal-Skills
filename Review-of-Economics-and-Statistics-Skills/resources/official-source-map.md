## Official MIT Press / REStat pages

- <https://direct.mit.edu/rest/pages/submission-guidelines> — REStat Submission
  Guidelines. Official search-index snippets on 2026-06-20 verified the load-bearing
  submission rules used by this pack; the full text was re-read on 2026-11-23 (access
  method above). It states: abstract **no more than 100 words** in a form suitable for
  the Journal of Economic Literature, **plus EconLit classification number(s)**; editor
  assignment by the paper's JEL codes (a specific editor may be requested); manuscript
  and cover letter in **PDF**; **12-point font** and **double-spaced throughout**
  (abstract, acknowledgments, footnotes, references); "Mechanical limits will not be
  applied to the length of articles" at submission, but **at acceptance papers must be
  45 pages or less** (double-spaced, 12-point, one-inch margins, counting text, tables,
  figures, references and all print parts) — encouraged, not required, at submission —
  and **online appendices have no size restrictions**; **Short Papers** (since December
  2019) strictly **<= 6000 words and <= 5 exhibits**, with up to 200 extra words per
  exhibit below five (**<= 7,000 words** with no exhibits), word count from the AEA
  word-count tool uploaded at Step 5, over-length papers returned unreviewed, and a
  Short-Paper **online appendix of no more than 20 pages**; **US$125 nonrefundable
  submission fee** for each new submission, **waived** if one author has a current-year
  individual subscription or a majority of authors primarily work in low- or
  lower-middle-income economies; **AI Disclosure Policy** — all authors must disclose
  whether generative AI was used, with tool(s), model(s), version(s) and all purposes,
  prompts encouraged if extensive; data, code and README posted before publication to
  permit replication, proprietary data indicated at submission; violators of the data
  or originality policies banned for five years; peer-review taxonomy "Identity
  transparency: **Single anonymized**".
- <https://direct.mit.edu/rest/pages/editorial-info> — REStat Editorial Info.
  **Re-verified 2026-11-23** (access method above): Editors **Will Dobbie (Co-Chair),
  Harvard University** and **Tavneet Suri (Co-Chair), MIT**, with Treb Allen, Dmitry
  Arkhangelsky, Michael Dinerstein, Ruixue Jia, Scott Kominers, Paulina Oliva, Kosali
  Simon and Stephen Terry; Managing Editor Heidi Morgans; editorial address Harvard
  Kennedy School. **Raymond Fisman is no longer listed** (he became Editor of AEJ:
  Applied in January 2026); the 2025 Annual Report still showed Dobbie and Fisman.
  History: official snippets on 2026-06-20 listed
  **Will Dobbie (Co-Chair), Harvard University** and **Raymond Fisman (Co-Chair),
  Boston University**. The same snippet family also surfaces current editors such as
  Treb Allen, Dmitry Arkhangelsky, Michael Dinerstein, and Ruixue Jia; this pack
  names only the co-chairs and sends users to the live page for the full board.
  **Re-verified 2026-06-22:** Dobbie and Fisman remain the sitting Co-Chairs per the
  MIT Press editorial-info page and the 2025 REStat Annual Report; the wider board
  (e.g., Allen, Alsan, Azoulay, Dinerstein, Hull, Suri, Terry) rotates — confirm on
  the live page before naming a handling editor.
- <https://direct.mit.edu/rest> — REStat journal home on MIT Press Direct.
  Use for publisher/journal-home context and current issue navigation.

## Operational notes for this pack

- The pack should state the **US$125** fee as a new-submission fee and keep the
  proprietary-data hold as an explicit pre-payment gate.
- The pack should state that empirical papers need data/code/README materials before
  publication and should route users to the **REStat Harvard Dataverse**.
- The pack should not claim a dedicated pre-acceptance reproducibility verifier unless
  the live MIT Press page states that mechanism.
- The live MIT Press guidelines now state the review model ("Single anonymized");
  the pack may call REStat single-blind and should not tell authors to anonymize.
- The pack should not name a full editorial board beyond the co-chairs unless the live
  MIT Press Editorial Info page has been consulted in the submission pass.

## UNVERIFIED / 待核实

- **Proprietary-data fee hold.** The 2026-06-20 snippets said proprietary-data papers
  should hold the fee until the editorial office confirms compliance. The full guidelines
  text read on 2026-11-23 says only that proprietary data must be indicated at submission
  and that authors with compliance questions should write to the editors; the hold is
  kept in the skills as a cautious practice but is not confirmed on the page.
- **"Notes" as a separate category.** The 2026-11-23 guidelines name regular papers and
  Short Papers only; the skills' Article / Note / Short Paper split is not confirmed.
