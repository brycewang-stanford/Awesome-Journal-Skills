# ES&T Official Source Map

Primary and authoritative sources behind the *Environmental Science & Technology* (ES&T)–specific
facts injected into this skill pack. Access date for all entries: **2026-06-01**. Article types, word
limits and the word-count procedure, abstract and keyword rules, cover-letter, reviewer-suggestion,
data-deposition, AI-use, conflict-of-interest and preprint policies **re-verified 2026-11-24** against
the live ES&T and ES&T Letters Author Guidelines on researcher-resources.acs.org (direct fetch with
`curl`; pubs.acs.org returns HTTP 403 to automated clients, so the Editor-in-Chief was re-checked via
search-engine rendering restricted to pubs.acs.org/acs.org). **Changed since 2026-06-01:** the live
word-count procedure counts the abstract, main text and figure/table titles, captions and footnotes,
and **excludes** graphics and tables themselves, references, title/author block, TOC graphic,
SI list and acknowledgments — there is **no figure/table word-equivalent charge** on the current page,
so the pack's earlier "~300/600 words per exhibit" guidance is withdrawn. Newly recorded: 150–200-word
abstract, 5–8 keywords, a cover letter with every submission, ACS "Fast Format" initial submission.
Re-verify volatile items (exact per-type word limits and what they include, submission-system name,
blinding model, current Editor-in-Chief, impact metrics, any open-access APC) on the official page
before relying on them — ACS journal pages change.

## Official author guidelines & submission

- **https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=esthag**
  — ACS Researcher Resources, ES&T Author Guidelines (page-verified; re-read in full 2026-11-24).
  Verifies: publisher =
  **American Chemical Society (ACS)**; manuscripts submitted through the **ACS Publishing Center**
  (the modern submission entry; the peer-review/submission environment is **ACS Paragon Plus**);
  manuscript types and word limits — **Research Article 7,000**, **Feature 5,000**,
  **Review (Critical Review) 10,000**, **Perspective 4,000**, **Policy Analysis 7,000**,
  **Viewpoint 1,000 + ≤5 refs**, **Spotlight 800 + ≤5 refs**, **Letter to the Editor 500 +
  250 words of refs**, **Correspondence/Rebuttal 1,000**; **a TOC graphic is required** for Research
  Articles, Features, Reviews, Policy Analysis, and Perspectives (not for Viewpoints/Spotlights/
  Letters); **Supporting Information** is submitted simultaneously as separate file(s), is available
  to reviewers, and is free to readers on publication; **data-availability statement** and public-
  database deposition of materials/data/protocols expected; ethical requirements (COI disclosure,
  **iThenticate/CrossCheck** plagiarism screening, coauthor consent, ARRIVE/STROBE where relevant,
  AI-use disclosure, safety-hazard reporting in Methods); editor screening with a significant
  fraction declined without external review, then typically **three** expert reviewers; authors
  suggest **≥4** non-conflicted reviewers. Re-read 2026-11-24, the page also states: **word count** =
  abstract + main text + titles/footnotes/captions of graphics; references, title/authors/
  affiliations, TOC graphic, graphics, tables, list of Supporting Information, acknowledgments and
  notes are **not** counted; over-length manuscripts are un-submitted (returned to Draft) or rejected,
  and any case for exceeding the cap belongs in the cover letter. **Abstract 150–200 words** (one
  paragraph, no references) for Research Articles, Reviews, Policy Analysis and Perspectives; Features
  a 3–5 sentence abstract; Viewpoints none. **5–8 keywords** for Research Articles, Policy Analysis,
  Reviews, Perspectives and Features. **A cover letter must accompany every manuscript submission**
  (rationale and environmental relevance; disclose preprints with DOI and link). ACS **Fast Format**
  for initial submission: complete standard sections, figures/tables embedded at the point of
  relevance, references in any style but complete with titles, Supporting Information as separate
  file(s). No footnotes (except the title-page author note and table footnotes); no separate
  Conclusion section. Database deposition of materials/data/protocols required for all published
  articles, with a statement in the submitted manuscript (ideally a data availability statement)
  giving repository and accession codes. AI tools cannot be authors; AI use for text or image
  generation disclosed in the **Acknowledgments** (substantial use detailed in Methods). Conflict-of-
  interest statement provided by the corresponding author during submission and published. Preprints
  with a DOI are acceptable but must be cited and declared in the cover letter. Pre-submission
  inquiries are not accepted. Contradiction noted: the Peer Review section says authors "must also
  submit" at least 4 potential reviewers, while "Providing Potential Reviewer Names" calls suggesting
  reviewers optional — follow the stricter reading.
- **https://pubs.acs.org/page/esthag/submission/authors.html**
  — Information for Authors (ES&T) on pubs.acs.org. The canonical author page (open in a browser if
  the crawler view is incomplete). Confirms scope, article types, and the TOC-graphic /
  Supporting Information requirements.
- **https://publish.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=esthag** — ES&T Author Guidelines via
  the ACS Publishing Center / **ACS Paragon Plus** submission entry.
- **https://publish.acs.org/entry.html** — **ACS Paragon Plus** submission and peer-review environment (current entry at publish.acs.org; the legacy `acsparagonplus.acs.org` address now redirects here)
  (the system referenced throughout ACS author guidance).

## Scope, aims & about

- **https://pubs.acs.org/page/esthag/about.html** — About ES&T. Verifies: ES&T "is an impactful
  environmental science and technology research journal that aims to be transformational and
  direction-setting, publishing rigorous and robust" work for a multidisciplinary audience; scope
  spans **environmental chemistry, environmental engineering, fate/transport/transformation in
  natural and engineered systems, treatment and resource recovery, ecotoxicology and environmental
  health, biogeochemical cycling, sustainability and energy, and the science–policy interface**.
- **https://pubs.acs.org/journal/esthag** — ES&T journal home (current issues, metrics).

## Bounded live-check rules

- Use the ACS Researcher Resources page as the controlling source for current article types and word
  caps (Research Article 7,000; Policy Analysis 7,000; Feature 5,000; Perspective 4,000; Review
  10,000 — all re-verified 2026-11-24). Do not charge figure/table word equivalents: the live
  procedure excludes graphics and tables but counts their captions.
- Treat **ACS Publishing Center** and **ACS Paragon Plus** as the same ACS submission flow for routing;
  use the live ACS entry link for actual upload.
- Do not assert a double-anonymous review model. The source-backed claim is editor-screened expert
  peer review with reviewer anonymity; the manuscript itself carries the author list (title and author
  list "must match between electronic entry and manuscript file"), so reviewers see author identities
  (single-anonymous in effect).
- Re-open the journal home/about page before naming the current Editor-in-Chief or executive/associate
  editors. Editor-in-Chief verified **2026-06-22** and re-checked **2026-11-24** as **Julie B. Zimmerman**
  (Yale University; named EIC 2020, succeeding David L. Sedlak) per the ACS ES&T EIC profile page
  https://pubs.acs.org/esthag/pages/eic-profile (search-engine rendering; HTTP 403 to direct fetch);
  re-verify before relying on it.
- Do not encode impact factor, CiteScore, acceptance rate, or time-to-first-decision as policy.
- Check ACS Open Science / APC terms only if the author chooses an open-access route. ES&T is a **hybrid**
  ACS journal (verified 2026-06-22): the **subscription route carries no author fee**, and gold OA is
  **optional** with the APC set by ACS's live, agreement-/license-/country-dependent pricing rather than
  a single static number — quote the live figure, not a fixed amount.
