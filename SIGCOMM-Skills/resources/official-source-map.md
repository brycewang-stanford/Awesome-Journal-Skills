## Verified facts used in the skills (SIGCOMM 2027, re-verified 2026-11-24)

- SIGCOMM 2027 is the **41st** edition, **August 8-12, 2027, Queen Sirikit National Convention
  Center, Bangkok, Thailand** (source 1).
- **One deadline: full paper February 2, 2027**, a hard deadline with no extensions (sources 1,
  3). The 2027 pages publish no separate abstract-registration date and no time zone; track,
  topics, ethics description, conflicts and the reciprocal reviewer are entered on the
  submission form (sources 2, 3).
- **Two tracks**, research and experience. Experience-track papers are judged on the **depth of
  the lessons learned** from deploying or operating a design, not on deployment size or
  duration, and their titles start with **"Experience:"**; surveys and tutorials are out of
  scope (source 2).
- **Format:** paper body (everything before references and appendices) **at most 12
  single-spaced pages including figures and tables**; pre-reference content longer than 12
  pages is **not reviewed**; references and optional appendices may run as long as needed, but
  reviewers are not required to read appendices and main-body claims cannot depend on appendix
  experiments. Letter paper, ACM double-column, **10-point** font or larger, **55** lines per
  column at most; numbered pages; an author block stating "Paper #N, 12 pages body, 14 pages
  total"-style counts; **abstract of no more than 200 words**; PDF only, **under 15 MB**;
  Banal format checking with desk rejection for substantial violations (sources 2, 3).
- **ORCID** for authors is required at submission time (source 2).
- **Double-blind**; experience-track papers may name the company or system but never the
  authors; arXiv/technical reports and talks are permitted; prior workshop papers are cited
  anonymously and explained on the form (source 3).
- **Review model (new for 2027):** "collaborative reviewing" — the reviewers of a paper discuss
  it from the start and, after the decision, write **one relatively short consensus review**.
  There are **no rebuttals or one-shot revisions** (source 2).
- **Three decisions (new for 2027):** accepted into the SIGCOMM conference (each paper gets a
  shepherd); **accepted into CCR** as a regular research article (authors may decline and
  resubmit elsewhere); or rejected from both (source 2).
- **Presentation (new for 2027):** every conference paper supplies a **video** and is presented
  by an author at an **in-person small-scale discussion session**; a subset is also given a
  single-track **plenary** talk with a talk shepherd and a respondent; remote "pods" continue
  (source 2).
- **Reciprocal reviewing:** every submission names an author willing to review; a reciprocal
  reviewer may be asked to review as many papers as they submitted and, except in exceptional
  circumstances, must hold a PhD (source 2).
- **Submission cap:** at most **eight** submissions per author across both tracks; surplus
  submissions are desk-rejected (source 2).
- **Ethics:** every paper describes any ethical issues on the submission form; papers with
  significant issues must discuss them in the paper, and IRB approval should be mentioned
  (sources 2, 3).
- **Generative AI:** governed by the ACM Policy on Authorship; authors state compliance on the
  submission form (source 2).
- **Publication date:** the official publication date for accepted papers is **July 18th,
  2027**, three weeks before the conference (relevant to patent filings) (source 2).
- **Artifact evaluation** is voluntary, for accepted papers only, run by a separate committee,
  and has no influence on acceptance; successful artifacts receive badges printed on the paper
  (source 2). The latest published Call for Artifacts (SIGCOMM 2025) awarded **two** ACM
  badges — **Artifacts Available** and **Artifacts Evaluated - Functional** (source 7); a
  "Results Reproduced" badge was not offered in that call.
- **Camera-ready** (2026 rule, carried on the 2027 page until it is rewritten): shepherd
  approval of the final version recorded via a HotCRP comment; PDF plus source through the
  **ACM Publishing System (TAPS)**; ACM `acmart.cls` or the ACM Word template (source 5).
- SIGCOMM confers a **Test of Time Paper Award** (10-12-year lookback) and a per-edition **Best
  Paper Award**; these anchor the exemplar canon (sources 8, 9).

## SIGCOMM 2026 (history, verified 2026-07-09)

- 40th edition, August 17-21, 2026, Colorado Convention Center, Denver (H1).
- Abstract registration **January 30, 2026** and full paper **February 6, 2026** (AoE; shown as
  6:59:59 AM EST the next morning on HotCRP) (H4).
- Review pipeline: early notification for consensus rejects; rebuttal for papers reaching the
  discussion phase; a shepherd-run **one-shot revision** — merits summary plus required changes,
  resubmitted about a month later, re-reviewed by the same reviewers where possible, ending in
  accept or reject only (H2). **None of these three mechanisms exists in SIGCOMM 2027.**
- Camera-ready due **July 3, 2026, 23:59 UTC** in HotCRP via TAPS (H5).

## Organizers and fee model

- SIGCOMM is the flagship conference of **ACM SIGCOMM** (the ACM Special Interest Group on Data
  Communication). Like every CS/networking conference in this collection, it has **no standing
  editor-in-chief**; the rotating analogue is the per-edition General Chairs and Program
  Chairs. **SIGCOMM 2027:** General Chairs **Kanchana Kanchanasut** (Asian Institute of
  Technology) and **Jim Kurose** (University of Massachusetts Amherst); Program Chairs
  **Ranjita Bhagwan** (Google), **Nate Foster** (EPFL and Jane Street), **Aurojit Panda** (New
  York University) and **Scott Shenker** (University of California, Berkeley) (source 4).
- The SIGCOMM 2027 pages state **no submission fee and no article-processing charge (APC)**.
  ACM proceedings are, however, published fully open access, and ACM charges an APC to authors
  whose institutions are not in ACM Open; the SIGMOBILE MobiCom 2027 CFP
  (https://www.sigmobile.org/mobicom/2027/cfp.html, read 2026-11-24) gives the ACM-wide 2027
  subsidized rates as $500 for ACM/SIG members and $750 for non-members. Whether and how this is
  applied to SIGCOMM 2027 papers is 待核实 (`acm.org` returned a Cloudflare block). Other costs
  are covered by **registration**; every conference paper must have an author present it in person
  at a discussion session (source 2). Proceedings are published by ACM through TAPS and appear
  in the **ACM Digital Library**; papers accepted into CCR appear as regular **ACM SIGCOMM
  Computer Communication Review** research articles (source 2).
