## Verified AISTATS 2027 facts used in skills (re-verified 2026-11-23)

- AISTATS 2027 is the 30th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and
  Statistics, in person in Montréal, Canada, May 3–6, 2027 (sources 1, 3, 6).
- Submission runs on OpenReview, group `aistats.org/AISTATS/2027/Conference`; every author
  needs an up-to-date OpenReview profile at abstract time, and incorrect profile information
  is grounds for desk rejection (source 1).
- Timetable (23:59 AoE): abstract September 29, 2026; full paper **including all
  supplementary material** October 6, 2026 — there is no separate supplement deadline this
  year; reviews, AI review and preliminary meta-review released November 16, 2026;
  author–reviewer discussion November 24 – December 3, 2026; decisions January 20, 2027;
  camera-ready to be announced (sources 1–4).
- Page limits: 8 pages of main text at submission and 9 at camera-ready; references, the AI
  Use Statement, the reproducibility checklist and appendices are not counted; main text over
  the limit is desk-rejected. Use the official `aistats2027` style from the paper pack
  (sources 1, 2, 7).
- Supplementary material may be appended to the main PDF (after references, AI Use Statement
  and checklist) or uploaded separately as one PDF or one ZIP (code, videos); it must not
  contradict the main paper (source 1).
- Review is double-blind: no names, affiliations, acknowledgements or identifying links;
  self-citation in the third person is allowed (sources 1, 2).
- **AI Use Statement is mandatory.** Every submission must include an "AI Use Statement"
  section before the references; a submission without one is desk-rejected; if no AI was
  used, it must say so. Generative-AI use is allowed with full author responsibility; LLMs
  cannot be authors; unclear, padded writing typical of unedited AI text is a rejection or
  desk-rejection risk; hidden prompts aimed at LLM reviewers are misconduct (sources 1, 2, 7).
- **AI review.** Every submission gets an LLM-generated factual-correctness review (no score,
  no recommendation), released to authors with the human reviews and shown to reviewers and
  ACs only during the discussion; authors may reply to it (sources 1, 4).
- **Quotas and authorship.** No author on more than 12 submissions; at most 3 for authors
  without an accepted ICLR/ICML/NeurIPS/AISTATS paper; excess submissions randomly
  desk-rejected. The author list is frozen at the abstract deadline (order may change after
  acceptance); major title/abstract changes are flagged for desk rejection (sources 1, 2).
- **Reciprocal reviewing.** Each submission nominates one author as a reviewer; missing
  nominations and negligent nominated reviewers can cause desk rejection (sources 1, 2).
- **Rebuttal (new limits).** One response per review, each at most 5,000 characters, plus a
  2,500-character answer to the preliminary meta-review; external links are not allowed
  (source 4).
- **Reproducibility checklist.** Part of the template, placed after the references and
  outside the page limit; leaving it out of the first submission does not cause desk
  rejection, but it is then requested during the author response and required for the
  camera-ready (source 7).
- **Citations.** Author–year or numeric at submission (used consistently); author–year
  required at camera-ready (sources 1, 2).
- **Preprints and dual submission.** arXiv/tech reports allowed and not prior publication;
  use a neutral (non-AISTATS) format and do not identify the preprint as an AISTATS
  submission during review; no concurrent archival conference/journal submission; workshop
  extended abstracts of 4 pages or fewer without proceedings do not count (sources 1, 2).
- **Presentation.** All accepted papers are posters, a subset also talks; at least one
  author should register and present in person, with exceptions for travel emergencies or
  visa problems; proceedings in PMLR (sources 1, 2).
- A confidential reporting (whistleblower) form exists for suspected policy violations, and
  a separate journal-to-conference track (deadline January 18, 2027) presents recent
  journal papers as posters (sources 1, 3).

## AISTATS 2026 facts (history, verified 2026-06-01)

- AISTATS 2026 was the 29th conference, Tangier, Morocco, May 2026, on OpenReview.
- Its timetable had a separate supplementary-material deadline one week after the full
  paper (abstract October 2, full paper October 9, supplement October 16, 2025).
- It allowed 8 submission / 9 camera-ready pages, required a reproducibility checklist,
  ran a text-only author-reviewer discussion without links, and required in-person
  attendance with no remote presentation.

## Organizers and fee model (re-verified 2026-11-23)

- AISTATS is run by the AISTATS board/community. Like every CS/AI conference in this
  collection, it has NO standing editor-in-chief; the rotating analogue is the per-edition
  General Chairs and Program Chairs. AISTATS 2027 General Chairs are Aaditya Ramdas and Arno
  Solin; Program Chairs are Quentin Berthet and Aymeric Dieuleveut (source 5). AISTATS 2026
  General Chairs were Emtiyaz Khan and Yingzhen Li, with Program Chairs Arno Solin and
  Aaditya Ramdas (source 11).
- There is NO article-processing charge (APC). AISTATS funds the conference through
  registration fees; at least one author should register and present in person. Accepted
  papers are published open-access in PMLR, which charges no author fee.
