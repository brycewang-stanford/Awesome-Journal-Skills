## Verified 2026-cycle facts used in the skills

- CIKM 2026 is the **35th International ACM Conference on Information and Knowledge
  Management, November 7-11, 2026, Rome, Italy** (main conference November 9-11; tutorials,
  Industry Day and PhD Symposium November 7; workshops November 8), hosted on a Sapienza
  University of Rome domain (sources 1, 2).
- The 2026 submission lineup is **five proceedings tracks**: Full Research (≤10 pages
  including appendices + ≤2 reference pages), Short Research (≤4 pages), Applied
  Research (≤7 pages), Resource (≤4 pages), and Demonstration (≤4 pages) — in every
  track the **appendix counts inside the page budget**, while the GenAI Usage
  Disclosure section and references sit outside it (sources 3-7).
- 2026 timetable (all 11:59 PM AoE): Full and Applied abstract **May 16**, papers **May 23**;
  Short/Resource/Demo abstract **May 30**, papers **June 6**; **notification August 7**;
  **camera-ready August 23**; authors' registration deadline **August 23** (source 2).
- Submission and review run on **EasyChair**, one named track selection per paper, and
  **every track requires nominating at least one author as a reviewer in EasyChair —
  skipping the nomination is a desk reject** (sources 3, 9).
- **Review mode differs by track.** Full Research and Short Research are **double-blind**
  (anonymized PDF, third-person self-citation, desk rejection for improper anonymization);
  Applied Research, Resource and Demo are **single-blind** — names and affiliations go in
  the manuscript (sources 3-8).
- For the double-blind tracks, a **non-anonymized preprint** (arXiv, institutional server,
  website) is allowed but must be declared on the EasyChair form, and its **title and
  abstract must be sufficiently distinct** from the CIKM submission — failure on either
  can desk-reject (source 8).
- Every submission carries a mandatory **"GenAI Usage Disclosure" section** — placed
  after the content/appendix, before the references, uncounted — declaring generative-AI
  use in the research, the code and data, and the writing; automated compliance checks
  are reserved, and reviewers are barred from AI-written reviews (source 8).
- Format is the **ACM proceedings template** (two-column `acmart` sigconf); the Short
  track states that **ACM CCS concepts and keywords are required for review** (sources 3, 4).
- **Dual/concurrent submission to any other conference or journal — or another CIKM 2026
  track — is prohibited**, and authorship is frozen at the abstract deadline under the ACM
  authorship policy (source 8).
- The full-paper **review schedule lists no author-response step**: bidding, primary
  review, reviewer discussion, SPC meta-review, PC chairs' decision meeting, notification
  (source 3).
- **In person, and Open Access with a possible APC:** at least one author registers by
  August 23 and presents in person (proxy only in exceptional cases; no pre-recorded or
  remote talks); ACM publications are Open Access from 2026, free for ACM Open
  institutions, otherwise an APC (2026 subsidy USD 250 members / 350 non-members) unless
  waived (source 8).
- CIKM 2026 chairs: General Chairs Antonella Poggi and Nicola Ferro; PC Co-Chairs Ioana
  Manolescu, Jiaxin Mao, Vanessa Murdock and Fabrizio Silvestri (sources 3, 15).
- CIKM proceedings are published by **ACM in the Digital Library** (CIKM '25 volume DOI
  10.1145/3746252), so acceptance leads into ACM e-rights and TAPS production
  (source 10).
- CIKM is co-sponsored by **ACM SIGIR and ACM SIGWEB**, and the venue's scope spans
  information retrieval, data mining, databases, and knowledge management — the
  tri-community identity the topic-selection skill is built around (sources 1, 13).
- Verified exemplar papers (see `exemplars/library.md`): Ferragina & Scaiella CIKM 2010
  (TAGME); Huang et al. CIKM 2013 (DSSM); Cao, Lu & Xu CIKM 2015 (GraRep); Guo, Fan,
  Ai & Croft CIKM 2016 (DRMM); Wang et al. CIKM 2018 (RippleNet, DOI
  10.1145/3269206.3271739); Sun et al. CIKM 2019 (BERT4Rec) — each pinned to a dblp
  record and edition-checked (19th/2010 Toronto, 22nd/2013, 24th/2015, 25th/2016,
  27th/2018 Torino, 28th/2019 Beijing).
