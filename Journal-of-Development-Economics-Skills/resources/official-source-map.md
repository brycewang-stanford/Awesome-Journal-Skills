## Official journal & policy pages

- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-development-economics**
  — ScienceDirect journal home (HTTP 403 on 2026-11-24; read via the Internet
  Archive capture of 2026-10-17). Verifies aims/scope, the Registered Reports
  track, the short-paper track (at most **6,000 words**, at most **5 exhibits**,
  online appendices of at most **20 pages**, decisions on refereed manuscripts
  generally within **6-8 weeks**; "Submission guidelines follow AER: Insights"),
  article publishing options (open-access **APC USD 4,400** excluding taxes;
  subscription route with no publication fee), timing metrics (11 days to first
  decision; 75 days to decision after review; 328 days to acceptance), the
  Editor-in-Chief (**K. Macours**), and current calls (special issue in honor of
  Elisabeth Sadoulet, deadline 31 December 2026; special issue in honor of Jere R.
  Behrman, deadline 15 November 2027).
- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-development-economics/publish/guide-for-authors**
  — Guide for Authors (HTTP 403 on 2026-11-24; read via search-engine rendering of
  this page and its mirror
  https://www.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-development-economics/0304-3878/guide-for-authors).
  Re-verified 2026-11-24: **USD 50** non-refundable submission fee for all original
  manuscripts, **USD 25** for full-time students, full waiver for corresponding
  authors in low- and middle-income economies per the AEA country designation (via
  a discount code from Elsevier support), no further fee for papers transferred via
  Elsevier's article transfer service, desk rejection keeps the fee; an author
  cannot submit more than **three papers within any 12-month period**; rejected
  papers are not reconsidered unless the decision letter leaves that open;
  **single anonymized** review with at least two reviewers after editor screening;
  abstract of at most **250 words**; **1 to 7** English keywords; **up to 6** JEL
  codes; highlights required at submission (**3 to 5** bullets of at most **85**
  characters, separate editable file); generative-AI use declared at first
  submission in a section before the references, AI tools never authors. From the
  2026-06-20 read (not re-rendered on 2026-11-24): Registered Reports, appeal
  policy, editable source files (PDF not an acceptable source file), Option C data
  policy, data availability statement at submission, reference policy, SSRN option.
- **https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-development-economics/about/editorial-board**
  — Editorial board (HTTP 403 on 2026-11-24; the board panel was read from the
  journal-home capture above). Verifies Editor-in-Chief **K. Macours** and the

## Checked facts

| Fact | Official source |
| --- | --- |
| JDE publishes original research on all aspects of economic development, emphasizing novel and relevant quantitative or analytical work | ScienceDirect home; Guide for Authors |
| The journal pre-screens submissions and immediately rejects some for fit, contribution, or methodological rigor | Guide for Authors |
| Approx. **1,300 submissions per year**, about **1/4 sent out for review**, and roughly **6-8% published** | ScienceDirect home; Guide for Authors (2026-06-20 read) |
| Under normal circumstances, an author cannot submit more than **three papers in any 12-month period** (re-verified 2026-11-24) | ScienceDirect home; Guide for Authors |
| Papers rejected by JDE will not be considered again unless the decision letter explicitly leaves that possibility open | ScienceDirect home; Guide for Authors |
| Submission is through **Editorial Manager**; the system converts uploaded files into a peer-review PDF | ScienceDirect home; Guide for Authors; editorialmanager.com/devec |
| Current review model is **single anonymized**; suitable papers typically go to a **minimum of two reviewers** (re-verified 2026-11-24) | Guide for Authors |
| Formal appeal possible if it meets Elsevier appeal-policy requirements; one appeal per submission and appeal decision final | Guide for Authors |
| Submission fee for original manuscripts: **USD 50 non-refundable**; **USD 25** full-time students; waived for corresponding authors in low- and middle-income economies (re-verified 2026-11-24) | Guide for Authors (search-engine rendering) |
| Open-access APC: **USD 4,400** excluding taxes (Internet Archive capture of 2026-10-17; was USD 4,760 on 2026-06-22); subscription route has no publication fee charged to authors | ScienceDirect home |
| Current Editor-in-Chief: **K. Macours** (Paris School of Economics) — re-verified 2026-11-24 via the 2026-10-17 capture; A. Foster no longer listed as Editor-in-Chief | ScienceDirect home; editorial board |
| Co-Editors: Anderson, Bazzi, Cai, Caunedo, Gonzalez-Navarro, Gulesci, Mahajan, McIntosh, Mejia Londoño, Romero, Vogl | Editorial board (2026-10-17 capture) |
| Short-paper route: at most **6,000 words**, **5 exhibits**, and **20-page online appendix**; manuscripts must be self-contained | ScienceDirect home; Guide for Authors |
| Short-paper refereed decisions generally occur within **6-8 weeks**; standard-format rejections may be invited into short format with a no-further-review commitment if instructions are followed | ScienceDirect home; Guide for Authors |
| Registered Reports / pre-results track available; Stage I and Stage II article types are selected in the submission portal | ScienceDirect home; Guide for Authors; BITSS/JDE pre-results guidance |
| Stage I pre-results proposal cap: **60 pages**; abstract cap: **150 words** | JDE pre-results author guidelines (2026-06-20 read) |
| Editable Word/LaTeX source files are required; PDF is not an acceptable source file | Guide for Authors (2026-06-20 read) |
| Regular article abstract must be **<=250 words** (re-verified 2026-11-24) | Guide for Authors |
| **1-7 English keywords** required; **up to 6 JEL codes** requested (re-verified 2026-11-24) | Guide for Authors |
| Highlights required at submission: **3-5 bullets**, **<=85 characters** each including spaces (re-verified 2026-11-24) | Guide for Authors |
| Generative-AI manuscript-preparation use must be declared at first submission in a section before references; AI tools may not be authors (re-verified 2026-11-24) | Guide for Authors |
| Preprint sharing is allowed under Elsevier policy; optional free SSRN posting after initial desk review has no effect on editorial outcome | Guide for Authors (2026-06-20 read) |
| Research-data **Option C** applies: deposit research data in a relevant repository and cite/link it, or explain why data cannot be shared | Guide for Authors (2026-06-20 read) |
| Data availability statement is required at submission and appears with the published article | Guide for Authors (2026-06-20 read) |
| References can be in any internally consistent style at submission; JDE style is applied after acceptance at proof stage | Guide for Authors (2026-06-20 read) |
| In-text citations are author-year; data references use `[dataset]` before the reference | Guide for Authors (2026-06-20 read) |

## Operational notes for this pack

- Keep the current **USD 50** original-manuscript fee (USD 25 students; waiver for
  low- and middle-income economies), while still asking users to re-check the live
  payment screen before filing.
- Keep the short-paper route current: **6-8 week** refereed decisions.
- Treat data policy as both venue-specific and Elsevier-current: JDE has a
  Mandatory Replication Policy referenced in the Registered Reports section and
  Elsevier Option C data deposit/cite/link-or-explain rules in the Guide.
- Re-check the ScienceDirect editorial-board page before naming editors in a
  cover letter.
