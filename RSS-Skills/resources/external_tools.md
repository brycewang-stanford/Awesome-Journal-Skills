# External Tools - RSS

Access date: 2026-07-08; re-verified 2026-11-24 (site now serving RSS 2027).

Open these surfaces after confirming which RSS edition is live. `roboticsconference.org`
is rebound to the newest edition each cycle: it showed 2026 content in July 2026 and
RSS 2027 (Athens) content by November 2026, with the 2026 pages moved under `/2026/` —
check the edition banner before trusting any date.

## Five author-side verification passes

1. **Edition pass** — confirm which year the site is currently serving, the host city,
   the conference dates, and whether the deadlines shown belong to the cycle you are
   planning for.
2. **Format pass** — reconfirm the page limits (RSS 2027: Stage 1 5 + 1 pages, Stage 2
   8 pages excluding references), the template, PDF-only main-file rule, and the
   self-containment requirement before deciding what moves to the Stage 2 supplement.
3. **Anonymity pass** — double-blind holds in 2027; sweep text, footage, supplementary
   archives, and OpenReview metadata for identity leaks, and remember external links
   are welcome only in the camera-ready.
4. **Review-mechanics pass** — verify the two-stage shepherding design is still in
   force: who is invited to rebut, the rebuttal template and length, and how the
   Extension Charter is set.
5. **Post-decision pass** — camera-ready deadline (May 14, 2027), registration and
   presentation obligations, and the proceedings-publication timeline on
   roboticsproceedings.org.

## Do not infer

- Do not project RSS 2026 dates or its single-deadline design onto RSS 2027; the 2027
  cycle starts on December 4, 2026 with a two-stage process.
- Do not treat any page ceiling as an RSS invariant — the 2021, 2022 and 2025 editions
  ran with no hard page limit, 2026 used 8 pages, and 2027 uses 5 + 1 then 8.
- Do not assume every submission gets a rebuttal; only papers invited past Stage 1
  write one (2026: only papers above a first-round threshold).
- Do not assume a demo-paper track exists; neither the 2026 nor the 2027 CFP lists one.
- If sources disagree, rank them: current CFP and Author FAQ, then OpenReview
  venue instructions, then chair email, then everything else — and record the conflict
  in your notes.
