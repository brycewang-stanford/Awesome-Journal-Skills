## Verified RSS 2027 facts used in the skills

- RSS 2027 is the 23rd edition, July 6–11, 2027, Athens, Greece (MAICC) (sources 1, 12).
- **Two-stage shepherding process** (source 2):
  - **Stage 1 — Extended Abstract, due December 4, 2026.** A semi-complete paper: problem,
    hypotheses and methodology fully fleshed out; experiments may be missing or
    preliminary. Strict maximum **6 pages = up to 5 pages of content + 1 page of
    references**; any appendix must fit inside the 5 content pages (source 3); nothing
    beyond the 6 pages (websites, appendices, videos, code) is considered; no
    supplementary material accepted at this stage; related work limited to directly
    relevant citations.
  - **Stage 1 decision, February 5, 2027:** reject, or invitation to rebuttal with a
    Metareview listing required clarifications, evaluations and extensions. **Rebuttal
    due February 12, 2027.**
  - **Stage 2 decision, February 26, 2027:** reject, or invitation to execute an
    **Extension Charter** (the AC-finalized roadmap of required experiments/proofs).
  - **Stage 2 final submission, April 16, 2027:** at most **8 pages excluding
    references**, executing the Charter; supplementary material (zip, 100MB max) by
    **April 19, 2027**; acceptance notification **April 30, 2027**; camera-ready **May
    14, 2027**. Papers are not rejected for well-founded negative results.
- **Signed reviews:** reviewer and chair names are made public for accepted papers, along
  with their reviews (sources 2, 4).
- **Anonymity:** no names/affiliations, no acknowledgments, no external links (anonymous
  ones discouraged too), anonymized metadata, self-citations indistinguishable from others;
  breaches are desk-rejected. arXiv preprints are allowed at the authors' own anonymity
  risk (source 2).
- **AI use:** LLMs permitted for authors and reviewers; disclosure not mandatory but highly
  encouraged; hallucinated citations or references lead to desk rejection, checked
  automatically (source 2).
- **Dual submission:** strictly forbidden, including a few days of overlap; a paper under
  review elsewhere must be withdrawn before the December 4 submission (sources 2, 3).
- **Authorship:** the Stage 1 author list is generally final; changes between stages need
  Program Chair approval and must be justified by the Extension Charter (source 2).
- Submission and review on OpenReview (`roboticsfoundation.org/RSS/2027/Conference`);
  all authors need an OpenReview account (source 2).
- Proceedings appear free and open-access at roboticsproceedings.org with
  `10.15607/RSS.*` DOIs; there is no APC anywhere in the pipeline. The cost model is
  conference registration (source 6).
- RSS is single-track: every accepted paper is presented to the whole audience.
- RSS has no standing editor-in-chief; the rotating analogue is the per-edition Program
  Chair (2027: Amanda Prorok; 2026: Matei Ciocarlie, who is 2027 General Chair) and Area
  Chairs appointed under the RSS Foundation (source 11).
