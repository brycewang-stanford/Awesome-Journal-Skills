## Official submission & policy pages (APS)

- **https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/psychological_science/ps-submissions**
  — Psychological Science Submission Guidelines (APS). Verifies: **Research Articles** cap the
  **Introduction, Discussion, Footnotes, Acknowledgments, and Appendices at 2,000 words combined**;
  **Method and Results do not count** toward that limit (typically ≤ 2,500 words); a **150-word
  abstract** (excluded from the limit) that **identifies sample sizes, participant populations, and
  important limitations of the research design**; roughly **40 references** suffice for most articles;
  **APA 7th edition** style with **tables and figures embedded in the main text**; initial submissions
  are **anonymized**; manuscript types: **Research Articles, Registered Reports (Stage 1 / Stage 2),
  Registered Reports with Existing Data, Commentary (≤ 1,000 words), Reply to Commentary (≤ 1,000,
  by invitation)** — Short Reports and Preregistered Direct Replications are **no longer accepted**;
  submission via **Manuscript Central** at `mc.manuscriptcentral.com/psci`. Re-read 2026-11-24:
  Table 1 gives **Research Article 2,000 words / 40 references***; **Registered Report 2,000 (1,000 for
  Stage 1), 1,000 words each for Introduction and Discussion**; **Commentary or Reply 1,000 words /
  20 references / 1 figure or table** (*reference numbers "are not hard-and-fast limits"); "for
  initial review, manuscripts must be anonymized as to authors and originating institutions" and an
  anonymized version is uploaded; files as .doc/.docx/.rtf/.pdf/.tex (with a PDF); the Research
  Transparency Statement must now also cover **conflicts of interest, funding, artificial
  intelligence and ethics**; manuscripts with **online samples** must state in the Method how
  automated/AI-generated responses were prevented or detected (or why not); AI use must be disclosed
  in the Research Transparency Statement. Statistical/disclosure
  norms: **effect sizes** with **a measure of uncertainty (e.g., confidence intervals)** for major
  results; **sample-size justification** and a **formal power analysis if appropriate**; **full
  disclosure** of excluded observations and all method/analysis details needed to replicate; analysis
  scripts and data dictionaries.
- **https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/psychological_science/preregistration**
  — Preregistration of Research Plans (APS). Verifies: preregistration guidance; preregistration
  status is reported and its quality is **factored into editorial decisions**.
- **https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/badges** — Open Practice Badges (APS).
  Verifies: badges were introduced in **2014**. (Re-read 2026-11-24: this page now describes only the
  2014 badge programme; the **1 January 2024** replacement — open data and materials required, a
  Research Transparency Statement required, no badges — is stated on the submission-guidelines page
  above, which is the source for it.)
- **https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/ms-structure-guidelines** — Manuscript
  Structure, Style, and Content Guidelines (APS). Verifies: structure/style detail; abstract **no
  longer than 150 words**; **five to seven (5–7) keywords** directly under the abstract; persistent
  identifiers (DOIs) for shared data/materials. **Conflict found 2026-11-24:** this page places the
  Research Transparency Statement **between the Abstract and the Introduction**, while the
  journal-specific submission guidelines place it **between the end of the Introduction and the
  start of the Methods** (the pack follows the submission guidelines; see 需复核).
- **https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/psychological_science/editorial-board** —
  editorial board (read 2026-11-24): **Editor-in-Chief Simine Vazire, University of Melbourne**.
- **https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/aps-editorial-policies** — APS editorial
  policies (read 2026-11-24): LLMs/generative AI may be used as productivity tools with human
  oversight; AI bots must not be listed as authors; editors and reviewers must not upload manuscripts
  to generative-AI tools.
- **https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/pss** — Psychological Science author instructions
  on SAGE (publisher). (SAGE pages may return HTTP 403 to automated fetch; the APS pages above are the
  primary source for the facts in this pack.)

## Open-science requirements (effective 1 Jan 2024)

- **Open data and materials are required** at submission, with **case-by-case exemptions** that must
  be justified; **"limits on transparency will be a factor in editorial decisions."**
- A **Research Transparency Statement** is a **required separate section between the Introduction and
  Methods** for all empirical manuscripts; it reports availability of data, analysis scripts,
  materials, and preregistrations and is shared with editors and reviewers during peer review.
- **Persistent identifiers (DOIs)** to all shared materials and data.
- **Preregistration** quality is considered in editorial evaluation (the separate "preregistered"
  badge is discontinued).

## Owner, publisher, history

- **https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_Science** — Verifies: Psychological Science is the
  flagship journal of the **Association for Psychological Science (APS)**, published by **SAGE**;
  founded **1990**; among the highest-impact journals in psychology.

## Re-verification 2026-11-24 (what changed)

- The **Research Transparency Statement** now also carries **conflicts of interest, funding,
  artificial-intelligence use and ethics** (template line "Conflicts of interest / Funding /
  Artificial intelligence / Ethics"), in addition to preregistration, materials, data and scripts.
- **New requirement:** manuscripts using **online samples** must state, in the Method, how automated
  or AI-generated responses were prevented/detected (or justify why no screening was needed).
- **Keywords: five to seven** under the abstract (Manuscript Structure page) — newly recorded.
- The badges page no longer documents the 2024 change; the source for it is now the submission
  guidelines page.
- Confirmed unchanged: 2,000-word Research Article limit (Introduction + Discussion + footnotes +
  acknowledgments + appendices), Method/Results excluded (≤ ~2,500 words advised), 150-word abstract,
  ~40 references, article types, anonymized initial review, Manuscript Central portal, EiC Vazire.
