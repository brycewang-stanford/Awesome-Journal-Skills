## Submission guidelines, review & article types

- **https://link.springer.com/journal/41267/submission-guidelines**
  — JIBS submission guidelines (re-verified 2026-11-24). Verifies:
  - JIBS publishes insightful, innovative, and impactful international-business research.
  - Manuscripts are evaluated for quality, fit with the Statement of Editorial Policy, and
    contribution-to-length ratio.
  - Full articles making strong contributions should be about **10,000 words** (about 30 double-spaced
    pages in 11-point Times New Roman); narrower contributions about **7,000 words**. Word count includes
    abstract, text, endnotes, and references; tables, figures, and online supplemental appendices can
    follow. Manuscripts considerably longer than these guidelines are returned immediately without
    review.
  - **Research Notes** should be **4,000-6,000 words including everything in the manuscript** and should
    have the same rigor, style, and tone as full-length articles.
  - Authors should not post submitted manuscripts, working papers, or prior drafts on easily discoverable
    websites where potential reviewers could find them.
  - Manuscripts involving human subjects should comply with the corresponding author's human-subjects
    protocol requirements.
  - Submit online through the JIBS submission tool at **mc.manuscriptcentral.com/jibs**; the Managing
    Editor contact listed is **Anne Hoekman**.
  - Title page uploaded separately (running title of no more than **40** characters; acknowledgements of
    financial or research assistance only on the title page); main manuscript starts with an anonymized
    abstract page.
  - Abstract is up to **200 words**, should not identify the authors, and should contain no reference
    citations.
  - Authors who have published from the same dataset or have closely related projects upload an
    **originality matrix** with the cover letter; a previously rejected manuscript must be flagged to
    the Managing Editor.
  - Generative AI: authors follow the AIB Guidelines for the Use of Generative AI; AI may support
    bounded tasks (language polishing, translation of author-written text, validated code) but may not
    generate theories, arguments, findings, citations, or conclusions, nor be used for idea generation;
    **the JIBS submission system requires an AI use disclosure with all submissions** (brief statement
    for proofreading; substantive description of tool, timing, and validation for other uses);
    hallucinated references or undisclosed AI use are grounds for rejection.
  - Keywords: three to six (**3-6**) keywords; first three required from the JIBS Keywords list, up to
    three more custom or from the list.
  - Formatting: **one-inch margins**, **11-point Times Roman**, **double-spaced** body text including
    abstract, references, and appendices; pages numbered from the Abstract Page; one space between
    sentences; left-aligned text.
  - **JIBS follows APA reference style** (author-date in-text citations, alphabetized and separated by
    semicolons; "et al." for three or more authors).
  - Reporting: actual p-values and effect sizes are expected in hypothesis-testing work.
  - Review model: **double-blind review**, with reviewer evaluations shared with the JIBS Area Editor.
  - Statement of Editorial Policy: JIBS is multidisciplinary, interdisciplinary, and seeks cutting-edge
    work that makes more than an incremental contribution; suitable work has substantial international
    business content as a primary focus; JIBS is paradigmatically and methodologically pluralistic.
  - DART: authors must comply with the AIB Journals Code of Ethics, disclose prior use of data and how
    the manuscript adds to prior data-based work, provide supplementary information when requested, file a
    data-sharing "comply or explain" statement at submission (share fully, share after an embargo of at
    most five years, or explain why not), write a **Data Accessibility Statement** on acceptance,
    and deposit fully available data in a reputable repository with a **DOI** where data are shared.
  - Subscription-route self-archiving has a **12-month embargo**; gold open access is optional on
    acceptance.
- **https://link.springer.com/journal/41267/how-to-publish-with-us**
  — "How to publish with us" page (re-verified 2026-11-24). Verifies: hybrid publishing model; submit link at
  **mc.manuscriptcentral.com**; open access is optional after acceptance; subscription route has **no
  APC charges**; current open-access APC is **GBP 3,090 / USD 4,990 / EUR 3,990**; CC BY is listed for
  open-access articles.

  concern.

## Pack-level guardrails

- Use **mc.manuscriptcentral.com/jibs** as the live submission route unless the Springer/AIB page changes it.
- Use APA reference style (the official guideline as of 2026-11-24), not a generic "JIBS house" style.
- Every submission needs the AI use disclosure in the submission system, even when AI was used only
  for proofreading.
- Treat **Rosalie L. Tung** as current Editor in Chief and **Anne Hoekman** as Managing Editor based on
  the live Springer journal home and editorial board (re-confirmed 2026-06-22 and again 2026-11-24; the
  Springer masthead still lists Tung). Note her originally announced term was 2023–2025, so re-verify the Editor in Chief
  on the Springer/AIB editorial-board page before submission in case of an editorial transition.
- Do not describe the full-article word count as including tables/figures/supplemental appendices; the
  current official wording includes abstract, text, endnotes, and references, while tables, figures, and
  online supplemental appendices can follow.
- Research Notes use the current official **4,000-6,000 words including everything** rule.
- Do not state a mandatory APC for subscription-route publication; the official OA APC applies only if
  authors choose open access after acceptance.
