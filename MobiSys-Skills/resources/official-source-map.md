## Verified MobiSys 2027 facts used in skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

- MobiSys 2027 is the **25th** Annual ACM International Conference on Mobile Systems,
  Applications, and Services, **June 21–25, 2027, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam** (source 2).
- **Calendar:** abstract registration **November 27, 2026** and paper submission **December 4,
  2026**, both **23:59 AoE** (HotCRP shows Saturday Nov 28 / Dec 5, 2026, 6:59:59 AM EST);
  early rejection **January 26, 2027**; rebuttal **February 8–12, 2027**; final decisions
  **March 6, 2027**; camera-ready **April 14, 2027** (sources 1, 2, 3). No extensions.
- **Registration abstract** of at least **100** words with a meaningful title; "TBD"-style
  placeholders desk-rejected; the author list is frozen at the paper deadline (source 1).
- **Format:** at most **12** single-spaced, numbered pages of main content including figures and
  tables; appendix and references unlimited and outside the cap, but reviewers need not read the
  appendix and over-length main content is not reviewed; font **≥10** points; two columns 9.25 x
  3.33 in, 0.33 in gap, at most **55** lines per column; US letter; PDF only;
  `\documentclass[sigconf,10pt]{acmart}` (source 1).
- **Review is single-blind** (since 2026): manuscripts carry the authors' names, affiliations
  and contact information; reviewers stay anonymous (sources 1, 5).
- **Two rounds:** papers not advancing get an **early rejection**; second-round papers get a
  **rebuttal of at most 500 words**, limited to correcting factual errors or answering reviewer
  questions — new experiments only if they directly address reviewer feedback, and results may be
  promised if ready by camera-ready (source 1).
- **Accepted papers:** conditional acceptance, final after **anonymous shepherding** that may
  request more evaluation; a **one-page public review** written by a TPC member appears on the
  website and in the proceedings; pitch-video and papers-preview details to be announced; at least
  one author registers at a **non-student rate** and presents in person (source 1).
- **Tracks:** regular papers plus an **Emerging Ideas** track (title prefix "[Emerging Ideas]",
  HotCRP checkbox) judged on originality, insight and potential rather than completeness
  (source 1).
- **LLM policy:** any LLM-generated text must be **clearly identified in the paper** (editing and
  proofreading exempt); hidden text aimed at LLM reviewers is forbidden; ACM Policy on Authorship
  applies (source 1).
- **Ethics:** authors of human-subject work, or work on non-public human-derived data, certify
  ethics-review (e.g. IRB) vetting during submission; non-compliant papers are rejected
  (source 1).
- **Prior work:** no concurrent submissions; a workshop paper counts only if published before
  the submission date and substantially extended; arXiv is not prior publication, but a different
  system name and no publicity during review are encouraged; an optional resubmission summary is
  shown to reviewers after they submit their reviews (source 1).
- **Open access and APC:** ACM Open institutions pay no APC; otherwise **$500** (ACM/SIG members)
  or **$750** (non-members) in 2027, the final year of ACM's subsidy (source 1).
- **Artifact evaluation:** optional, after acceptance decisions; badges awarded on evaluation
  (source 1). The 2027 AE call is not yet published; the MobiSys 2026 call offered three
  independent ACM badges (source 7).
- **Chairs:** General Chairs VP Nguyen and Anh Nguyen; PC Chairs Jun Han and Falko Dressler
  (source 4).

## Organizers and fee model

- MobiSys is organized by ACM SIGMOBILE. Like every CS conference in this collection, it has NO
  standing editor-in-chief; the rotating analogue is the per-edition General Chairs and PC
  Chairs (see above).
- Accepted papers appear open access in the ACM Digital Library. The July 2026 statement "There
  is NO article-processing charge" is **withdrawn**: authors whose institutions are not in ACM
  Open pay an APC (source 1). Registration funds the conference.
