## Author-side verification passes (run each cycle)

- **Clock pass.** Confirm the single paper-registration and paper-submission dates on the
  current `mobisys<yy>.hotcrp.com/deadlines` page, and the exact time-zone/AoE framing
  (MobiSys 2027: November 27 / December 4, 2026, 23:59 AoE, printed on HotCRP as 6:59:59 AM
  EST the next day). There is one hard December cutoff per year; a miss costs a full cycle.
- **Budget pass.** Confirm the page cap is still 12 double-column pages including figures and
  tables, and that references and appendices remain outside it. Re-read the ACM template
  version named in the CFP.
- **Review-mode pass.** Confirm the blinding mode on the live CFP — single-blind since 2026, so
  the PDF must carry complete names, affiliations and contact information — and that every
  conflict is declared; also confirm LLM-generated text is identified in the paper and the
  ethics-review certification is ready for human-subject work.
- **Status pass.** Confirm which round you are in and what the notification means: an early
  rejection after round 1 is terminal for this cycle; a round-2 outcome precedes the rebuttal.
- **Rebuttal-scope pass.** Confirm the current rebuttal length limit (500 words in 2027) and
  the rule on new results (directly-responsive only, or promised for camera-ready); accepted
  papers are conditional pending anonymous shepherding.
- **Artifact pass.** Confirm the three ACM badge names, the artifact deadline, and the
  single-blind script-first evaluation model on the current Artifact Evaluation page.

## Do not infer

- Do not infer future-cycle dates, host, chairs, or page caps from MobiSys 2027 (or 2026).
- Do not assume a MobiCom-style rolling two-deadline calendar or a MobiCom/NSDI-style one-shot
  revision channel; MobiSys 2027 runs one December deadline with a rebuttal and shepherded
  conditional acceptance.
- If official pages disagree, use the newest CFP, HotCRP deadlines page, or direct chair
  communication as controlling, and record the conflict.
