## Verified 2026-cycle facts used in the skills

- CoRL 2026 is the 10th edition (the 9th was Seoul 2025 per PMLR v305), held in
  Austin, Texas: **main conference November 9–11, workshops November 12, 2026**.
- Submission ran on OpenReview under group `robot-learning.org/CoRL/2026/Conference`,
  double-anonymous, with abstract registration **May 25, 2026** and paper deadline
  **May 28, 2026** (supplementary June 4), all 23:59 AoE; authors could not be added or
  removed after the abstract deadline.
- Submissions: 8 pages of main text including a mandatory Limitations section
  (counted); acknowledgments, references, and an optional appendix uncounted, in
  the same PDF or the supplement.
- Supplementary material encouraged, as a single zip; overview video capped at 250 MB
  (strict), ~3 minutes suggested length.
- At least one qualified author per submission must agree to review, and every author
  with 3 or more submissions must review; non-compliance can desk-reject.
- Generative AI: no AI authorship; generative-AI use briefly disclosed **in the
  submission form**; papers citing nonexistent references desk-rejected (GPTZero check,
  manually confirmed by ACs/SACs/PCs); near-duplicate multi-submissions desk-rejected.
- Reviews and early-reject notifications released **August 4, 2026**; rebuttal (one-page
  PDF on the official template, anonymous, no links) due **August 11, 2026**, 11:59 PM
  AoE; reviewer–AC discussion August 12–19, 2026; first-round rejection without
  rebuttal possible when no reviewer or AC score reaches weak accept.
- Acceptance notification **September 4, 2026**; accepted papers listed October 19.
- Accepted papers and their reviews are made public and de-anonymized after decisions.
- Camera-ready: 9-page main text under `corl_2026` final mode, appendix merged at
  the end of the PDF, signed PMLR permission form, due October 12, 2026, 23:59 AoE;
  videos hosted externally because PMLR takes no video supplementary.
- All accepted papers are presented as posters; selected papers get oral spotlights.
- Proceedings publish in PMLR. Verified volume anchors: v78 = 2017 (Mountain
  View), v164 = 2021 (5th edition; pages dated 2022), v205 = 2022 (Auckland),
  v229 = 2023 (Atlanta), v270 = 2024 (Munich), v305 = 2025 (Seoul).
