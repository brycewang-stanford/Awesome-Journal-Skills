## Verified facts used in the skills

- ICSE 2028 (50th edition) runs April 29 – May 7, 2028 at the Hilton Waikoloa
  Village, Hawaii; main conference May 3–5.
- The 2028 research track runs a **single submission cycle** (as in 2027):
  mandatory abstract June 24, 2027; paper July 1, 2027; both 23:59:59 AoE.
  ICSE 2025 and 2026 ran two cycles — the cycle count is cycle-volatile.
- 2028 pipeline: author response (3 days) September 24–26, 2027 → notification
  October 21, 2027 → Major Revision due November 18, 2027 → camera-ready of
  direct accepts November 25, 2027 → final MR decision December 19, 2027 →
  camera-ready of accepted MR papers January 26, 2028.
- Five posted review criteria: Novelty; Rigor; Relevance; Verifiability and
  Transparency (any attached or linked artifact is checked by one reviewer);
  Presentation (new for 2028).
- New 2028 process: reciprocal reviewing (each submission nominates one
  qualified potential reviewer, subject to area-chair approval), no bidding
  phase (automatic assignment checked by the chairs), and early rejection of
  papers whose initial reviews are all negative, before the response phase.
- Format: 10 pages main text inclusive of all figures/tables/appendices + 2
  pages references only; camera-ready receives one extra main-text page; **ACM
  Primary Article Template** (`sigconf`, `review`, `anonymous`) — ICSE 2027 used
  the IEEE template, ICSE 2026 the ACM one; format deviations may be desk-rejected.
- Review is double-anonymous; own prior work cited in the third person; company
  and institution names anonymized; preprints allowed but must not name ICSE.
- Generative AI: may not be an author; AI-generated content disclosed in the
  Acknowledgements; editing-only (Grammarly-like) use need not be disclosed;
  hallucinated or fabricated references are desk-rejected.
- Open science: sharing is the default and non-sharing needs justification in
  the submission form and the paper; artifacts via supplemental upload or an
  anonymous repository; sharing is not mandatory for acceptance. The ICSE 2027
  policy asked for a Data Availability section after the Conclusion.
- At least one author must register and present.
- Chairs: General Chair Rick Kazman; Program Co-Chairs Yuanfang Cai and Lars
  Grunske (ICSE 2027: Dongmei Zhang and Andrea Zisman).
- Artifact evaluation is post-acceptance and opt-in and follows ACM badging; the
  2027 round runs January 22 – February 26, 2027.
- Journal-first partners are IEEE TSE, ACM TOSEM, and EMSE (2027 window: journal
  acceptance October 11, 2025 – October 10, 2026).
- ICSE 2026 (48th) accepted 321 of 1,469 research-track submissions across two
  cycles, most via Major Revision (189 of 321).
- Most Influential Paper lineage facts used in `exemplars/library.md`:
  ICSE 2019 MIP to "Automatically Finding Patches Using Genetic Programming"
  (ICSE 2009); ICSE 2022 MIP to "On the Naturalness of Software" (ICSE 2012);
  ICSE 2024 MIP to "Coverage Is Not Strongly Correlated with Test Suite
  Effectiveness" (ICSE 2014).
