# AEJ: Macroeconomics Official Source Map

Primary and authoritative sources behind every AEJ: Macro-specific fact injected
into this skill pack. **Access date for the original entries: 2026-06.** AEJ: Macro is one
of the four *American Economic Journals* published by the **American Economic
Association (AEA)**; it is the AEA's quarterly broad-interest macroeconomics
journal (founded 2009). Submission rules, review mode, fees, style guide, data/code
and disclosure policies, and the masthead were **re-verified 2026-11-24** against the
live AEA pages listed below (all read directly). Changes found on 2026-11-24:
**submissions must now be author-anonymized** (AEA three-year RCT of author-anonymized
review, launched fall 2026) — the title page with names, affiliations and the
acknowledgement footnote is a separate PDF, so the pack's earlier "first page carries
title, byline, and affiliations" rule now applies only to accepted articles; the style
guide's **no-asterisk** rule contradicted the pack's earlier "AEJ: Macro accepts
significance stars" line; JEL codes are not stated as a requirement on the current
pages (see 需复核); the portal is **ScholarOne Manuscripts**. Editor, coeditors, fees
and length guidance were unchanged. Re-verify volatile items before relying on them —
AEA pages change. Items that were not confirmed from a primary fetch are marked
**需复核 (to be verified)**.

## Official AEA pages (publisher / editorial authority)

- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mac/submissions**
  — AEJ: Macroeconomics Submission Guidelines (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: original
  work not under consideration elsewhere; papers previously rejected by AEJ: Macro
  should not be resubmitted; CrossRef Similarity Check; AEA Disclosure Policy; AI may
  not be an author and AI use in preparing the manuscript, including drafting or
  editing text, "must be briefly described during the submission process"; data/code
  required before acceptance for empirical, simulation, or experimental work; field
  experiments registered in the AEA RCT Registry (laboratory experiments need not be).
  Submission fee scaled by AEA membership and country income tier (members US$200
  high-income / US$100 middle-income / US$0 low-income; nonmembers US$300 / US$200 /
  US$0), paid by credit card; papers rejected without review receive a 50% refund;
  publication fee of US$15 per typeset page assessed at page proofs (papers first
  submitted on or after February 1, 2024). Length: average article 34–35 typeset pages;
  manuscripts strongly recommended not to exceed 40 pages (11-point, 1.5 spacing,
  1-inch margins) or 45 pages (12-point, 1.5 spacing, 1-inch margins), the suggested
  length including figures, tables, references, and appendices. **Manuscript
  preparation (changed fall 2026):** "The AEA has launched a three-year randomized
  controlled trial of the effects of author-anonymized peer reviewing, commonly
  referred to as 'double-blind'"; an **abstract of 100 or fewer words is required**;
  PDF under 10 MB when reasonably possible; style guide optional at submission,
  required for accepted articles; manuscripts **must be anonymized** — title and
  abstract at the top of the first page; author names, affiliations, contact
  information and the acknowledgement footnote in a separate PDF labelled "title
  page"; self-citations by name, never "my/our"; no identifying locations, IRB
  approvals, or personal/institutional website links; no RCT-registration entry in
  the reference list; supplementary appendices and response letters anonymized.
  In-paper appendices count toward the page total; an optional Supplemental Appendix
  is posted unedited, and the paper must be self-contained. Submission is on
  **ScholarOne Manuscripts** (https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/aej-macro). A cover
  letter "is not required and should only be used to provide substantive information"
  (coeditor conflicts, data-access limitations), preferably in the form field. Each
  (co)author's **Disclosure Statement** PDF must be provided with the manuscript —
  submissions without them "will be considered incomplete and will not be reviewed."
  Field experiments supply the AEA RCT ID during submission. AER / AER: Insights
  transfer option; AEA templates optional (in "review mode"); Chicago Manual
  author-date preferred for bibliographic software; random-order coauthor option.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mac/editors**
  — Editors of AEJ: Macroeconomics. **Re-verified 2026-11-24 (unchanged since
  2026-06-22):** Editor **Ayşegül Şahin** (Princeton University); coeditors **Francesco
  Bianchi** (Johns Hopkins), **Şebnem Kalemli-Özcan** (Brown), and **Vincenzo Quadrini**
  (USC); a 21-member Board of Editors; Data Editor **Lars Vilhuber** (Cornell); Managing
  Editor Michelle DeBlasi; Assistant Managing Editor Justin Shapiro; past editors
  including Simon Gilchrist (2018–2023). Some third-party listings show stale
  affiliations (e.g., Texas/Maryland), so the AEA page is authoritative.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mac/editorial-policy**
  — AEJ: Macroeconomics Editorial Policy (re-read 2026-11-24). The page **still says**
  "All manuscripts submitted to the AEJ: Macroeconomics undergo single-blind peer
  review" (the author's name is revealed to the referee, while referees remain
  anonymous to authors) — i.e., it has not been updated for the anonymization RCT.
  Also verifies: editor assigns a coeditor; authors may request coeditors; return
  without referee review weighing expected probability of meeting AEJ: Macro
  standards, breadth of topic, and interest to the AEJ: Macro audience; a second
  coeditor is consulted before a first revise-and-resubmit; conditionally accepted
  papers are reviewed by the AEA Data Editor for the Data and Code Availability and
  Data Legality policies; reviewers must disclose AI use in their cover letter.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/news/rct-announcement** — AEA announcement, 10 September
  2026 (re-read 2026-11-24): three-year RCT of author-anonymized ("double-blind")
  reviewing at the AER, AER: Insights and the four AEJs, designed by Amy Finkelstein
  and J-PAL North America; treatment-arm reviewers receive no author-identifying
  information; launch expected fall 2026 (the submissions page says it has launched).
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mac/style-guide** — AEJ: Macroeconomics style
  guide (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: abstract "must not exceed 100 words"; Roman-numeral
  section headings; footnotes, not endnotes; Chicago author-date citations; tables no
  more than 9 columns wide; "Do not use asterisks to denote significance of estimation
  results. Report the standard errors in parentheses"; vector figures; appendices A, B,
  C. Title and byline at the top of the first page apply to accepted articles.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mac/accepted-article-guidelines** — Accepted
  article guidelines (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: no title page and title/byline at
  the top of the first page **for accepted manuscripts**; native files; signed Data and
  Code Availability Form and Archive Agreement; individual disclosure PDFs; separate
  Supplemental Appendix PDF; ScholarOne upload.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mac/reviewers** — Reviewer guidelines (re-read
  2026-11-24). Verifies that reviewers remain anonymous to authors; the page no
  longer describes the review as single-blind.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/mac** — Journal home (re-read 2026-11-24).
  Verifies scope (aggregate fluctuations and growth and the role of policy) and
  "four issues per year"; print ISSN 1945-7707 / online ISSN 1945-7715.

## AEA-wide policies (shared across AER / AEJs)

- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/data/data-code-policy**
  — AEA Data and Code Availability Policy, **February 2026 version** (re-read 2026-11-24,
  still current). Verifies: the AEA Data Editor assesses compliance, conducts
  reproducibility checks, and verifies the accuracy of the information provided
  **prior to publication**; use of the **AEA Data and Code Repository** is strongly
  encouraged (other trusted repositories may be allowed with Data Editor approval);
  code scope is broad — data transformation/cleaning, analysis/estimation,
  **simulation**, model solution, and visualization programs must all be included;
  restricted-access data (confidential / copyrighted / agreement-restricted) qualify
  for exceptions, with 5+ year retention, reasonable replication assistance, public
  code even when data are not public, and disclosure of sources; "upon request"
  access approved by the authors themselves is generally not acceptable.
- **https://aeadataeditor.github.io/** and **https://aeadataeditor.github.io/about/**
  — Office of the AEA Data Editor. Verifies: the AEA Data Editor (since 2018) is
  **Lars Vilhuber** (Cornell University; also listed on the AEJ: Macro editors page,
  re-read 2026-11-24); the office runs the pre-publication reproducibility
  verification service and curates the AEA Data and Code Repository on openICPSR.
  Accessed 2026-06.
- **https://www.openicpsr.org/openicpsr/search/aea/studies**
  — AEA Data and Code Repository on **openICPSR**. Verifies: openICPSR is where
  AEA replication packages are deposited and disseminated. Accessed 2026-06.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/disclosure-policy**
  — AEA Disclosure Policy (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: each (co)author prepares a
  separate "Disclosure Statement" PDF (stating explicitly if nothing to disclose);
  statements are available to referees and published with the article; the
  threshold for significant financial support from interested parties is
  **US$10,000** over the past three years; sources to disclose include
  grants/consulting/retainers, in-kind support (e.g., data access), relevant
  officer/director/board positions, close relatives/partners, and review-rights
  arrangements; IRB approval status must be disclosed for human-subjects data.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/low-income-country-fees** — AEA
  Country Designation for Submission Fees (read 2026-11-24). Lists the countries
  classed as low- and middle-income for the fee tiers.

## Unverified items (需复核)

- **JEL codes at submission.** The 2026-06 map listed JEL codes as required; on
  2026-11-24 none of the AEJ: Macro submissions, style-guide, accepted-article, or
  editorial-policy pages mentions JEL codes. The skills now call them customary
  rather than required; the ScholarOne form itself was not inspected.
- **How long the anonymized-submission rule lasts.** The RCT is announced as three
  years from fall 2026; whether the editorial-policy page's single-blind wording will
  be updated was not stated as of 2026-11-24.

(Resolved 2026-11-24: the Board of Editors roster is listed on the editors page; the
fee schedule was re-confirmed; the country-to-tier mapping is the AEA country
designation page above; the portal is ScholarOne Manuscripts.)
