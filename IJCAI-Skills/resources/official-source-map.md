## Verified IJCAI 2027 facts used in skills (re-verified 2026-11-23)

- IJCAI 2027 is the 36th International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Kyoto,
  Japan, August 7-13, 2027, with a satellite event in Hengqin, China, August 15-17, 2027
  (sources 1, 2).
- Main-track dates, all 23:59 AoE (UTC-12): abstract submission January 4, 2027; author
  information and full paper including appendix January 11, 2027; non-primary paper payment
  January 18, 2027; summary-reject notification February 25, 2027; author response March 30 -
  April 2, 2027; paper notification April 21, 2027; camera-ready May 7, 2027 (source 2).
- Page limit: no longer than 9 pages in total — 7 pages for the body including all figures
  and tables, plus up to 2 additional pages for references. The optional ethics statement may
  sit in the body or on the reference pages. Overlength papers are rejected without review;
  papers that shrink margins or font sizes are rejected without review (source 2).
- Template: IJCAI 2027 style from the Author Kit (FormattingGuidelines-IJCAI-27.zip, LaTeX and
  Word); submissions are PDF files (sources 2, 7).
- Review is double-blind: anonymize paper, supplementary material and code; no names,
  affiliations, acknowledgements, funding acknowledgements or contribution statements; refer
  to own prior work in the third person; remove identifying PDF metadata; anonymity
  violations are rejected without further review (source 2).
- Supplementary material: up to 50MB, PDF or ZIP, anonymized; appendices, proofs,
  derivations, data or code that directly support the paper. The appendix is part of the
  full-paper submission due January 11, 2027 (source 2).
- Submission process: submission site still "TBA"; real title and abstract at the abstract
  deadline (placeholder abstracts removed); author list final at the Author Information
  deadline; ORCID validation compulsory; each author may appear on no more than 8
  submissions across the Main Track and all Special Tracks; up to 3 content-area keywords
  chosen in the submission form (source 3).
- Reciprocal reviewing: each submission designates one qualified author as reciprocal
  reviewer unless no author qualifies; non-compliance, or a designated reviewer missing
  Phase 1 or Phase 2 duties, means desk rejection (sources 2, 3, 5).
- Primary Paper Initiative: USD 100 per main/special-track submission that is not a primary
  paper; waived when none of the authors appears on any other IJCAI 2027 submission
  (sources 3, 4).
- Two-phase review: papers with two insufficiently positive good-quality Phase 1 reviews are
  summarily rejected without author response (source 2).
- Reproducibility is a review criterion; code/data sharing is encouraged but not strictly
  required if results are reproducible in principle; a Reproducibility section is optional
  (sources 2, 6).
- LLM use: generative AI cannot be an author; LLMs may only polish text the authors wrote;
  LLM outputs that are part of the method or experiments are allowed if clearly labelled and
  described; prompt injection is forbidden (sources 2, 8).
- Preprints: non-anonymous preprints do not cause rejection; reviewers are told not to
  search for them; the submission itself stays anonymous (source 2).
- Dual submission: not accepted if under review, published or accepted at a journal or other
  venue with formal proceedings (DOI/ISBN/ISSN) at full-paper submission time; non-archival
  workshops are fine (source 2).
- At least one author must present in person in Kyoto or Hengqin (oral and poster), or the
  paper is excluded from the proceedings unless an exception is approved (source 2).

## Organizers and fee model (re-verified 2026-11-23)

- IJCAI is run by IJCAI Inc. Like every CS/AI conference in this collection, it has NO standing
  editor-in-chief; the rotating analogue is the per-edition Conference Chair and Program Chair.
  IJCAI 2027 Conference Chair is Sarit Kraus (Bar-Ilan University) and PC Chair is Bo An (NTU);
  Local Arrangement Chair Takayuki Ito (Kyoto University) (source 10). History: IJCAI-ECAI 2026
  Conference Chair was Francesca Toni and Program Chair was Diego Calvanese (H5).
- The Primary Paper Initiative fee (USD 100 on non-primary papers) is a submission-volume
  disincentive whose proceeds fund reviewer recognition — NOT a per-article publication charge;
  do not conflate it with a journal APC (sources 4, 9). Proceedings are the open IJCAI
  Proceedings and the cost model is registration fees; no APC appears on the 2027 pages, but
  the 2027 registration page is not yet published.

## UNVERIFIED / 待核实 (as of 2026-11-23)

- **Submission platform** for IJCAI 2027: the submission-process page says "TBA". The 2026
  edition used Chairing Tool; do not assume the same system.
- **Reproducibility checklist at submission**: the CFP says authors "must follow the
  reproducibility guidelines and checklist at the time of paper submission", while the
  reproducibility page says authors are not expected to submit it. Treat as unresolved; check
  the submission form when it opens.
- **Recent-rejection disclosure / Resubmission File**: required in 2026 (previous 12 months);
  neither the 2027 CFP nor the submission-process page mentions it, and no 2027 submissions
  FAQ exists (404). Not confirmed either way for 2027.
- **Author-response format** (2026: one-page template, no new results, no code links): no 2027
  author-response FAQ is published yet.
- **Abstract length**: no word limit stated on the 2027 pages.
- **Reciprocal-reviewer cap per person**: the submission-process page says at most two
  submissions; the reciprocal-review page gives no number. Re-check.
- Registration fees and any camera-ready page allowance for 2027.
