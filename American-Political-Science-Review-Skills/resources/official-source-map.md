# APSR Official Source Map

Primary and authoritative sources behind the APSR-specific facts injected into this skill pack.
Access date for all entries: **2026-06-01**. Submission rules, review process, fees/APC, transparency
and AI policies, and masthead **re-verified 2026-11-24** against the live APSA and Cambridge Core pages
listed below, all read directly. Changes found on 2026-11-24: the APSA "APSR Submission Guidelines"
page no longer carries the rules — it now only says "For all the information, please visit the APSR
Cambridge website", so the authoritative text is the Cambridge **Preparing your materials** page; the
desk-rejection grounds (remit / ethics / substance, two-editor rule) previously sourced to the APSA page
are no longer on any live page (moved to 需复核 below); and the Cambridge pages add several rules
(reference check, Chicago 17th author-date references, AI declaration, author-questionnaire ethics
affirmations — see below). Re-verify volatile items (editors and editorial term, exact word/abstract
caps, fee/APC, data-policy wording, accepted tracks) on the official page before relying on them —
journal pages change.

## Official journal, submission & author pages

- **https://apsanet.org/publications/journals/american-political-science-review/apsr-submission-guidelines/**
  — APSA APSR Submission Guidelines. **As of 2026-11-24 this page holds no rules**: it only points readers
  to the APSR Cambridge website (Preparing your materials, below), which now carries the same length,
  abstract, anonymity, ORCID, track and preregistration rules. Historical content (2026-06-01): most **Articles < 11,000 words**; **Research Notes
  < 7,000 words** (word counts exclude reference lists and appendices but include tables, figures,
  and footnotes printed in the text); **abstract ≤ 150 words**; a **word count on the front page**;
  manuscripts must **preserve author anonymity** (avoid unnecessary self-citation, remove obvious
  self-references); **ORCID iD required for corresponding authors** submitting after 1 Jan 2019;
  formatting follows the **APSA Style Manual for Political Science**; submission via **Editorial
  Manager** (`editorialmanager.com/apsr`); five tracks — **Regular Articles, Research Notes,
  Replications and Reappraisals, Syntheses, Registered Reports**; preregistration / pre-analysis
  plans are encouraged and must be shared anonymized (appendix or OSF) with registered vs.
  unregistered analyses clearly marked. No submission fee or APSA-membership requirement is stated.
- **https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/information/author-instructions/preparing-your-materials**
  — Cambridge Core "Preparing your materials" (**re-read 2026-11-24; now the authoritative APSR
  guidelines**). Verifies: "There is no strict word limit for APSR submissions", but **most completed
  articles are expected to be less than 11,000 words** and **research notes less than 7,000 words**;
  **abstracts must be 150 words or less**; word counts **exclude reference lists and supplementary
  material but include tables, figures, and footnotes** that appear in the published text; **word count
  on the front page**; five tracks — **Research Articles, Research Notes, Replications and Reappraisals,
  Registered Reports, Syntheses**; Comments to editors only for issues not handled in the manuscript (do
  not summarize the paper; "do not write a separate cover letter if it can be avoided"; naming specific
  reviewers discouraged); **references are checked before review — a citation to a work that cannot be
  found leads to desk rejection with no opportunity of resubmission**; no review of papers previously
  rejected at APSR without an invitation; preregistration encouraged (generally expected for
  experiments) with the Share / Signpost / Evaluate / Reconcile expectations (anonymized PAP in the
  online appendix or an anonymized OSF link; reconciliation report for deviations); reproducibility
  deposit for computational papers at conditional acceptance, and authors anticipating data-sharing
  problems must note this **in the Author Questionnaire at submission**; formal claims need complete
  proofs at submission; **ethics affirmations in the Author Questionnaire** (human-participants
  screening question with ethical practices discussed in the text or an appendix, compliance with APSA's
  Principles and Guidance for Human Subjects Research or a reasoned justification, conflicts of
  interest, **funding sources**, where data-collection procedures are explained, and a commitment to post
  quantitative data and code on the APSR Dataverse or a justification in the text/appendix); **AI
  policy** (AI cannot be an author; declare generative or analytic AI use — text in acknowledgments or a
  footnote, data work in the methods, images in captions — with tool name/version, dates, access and
  full description; minimal non-generative uses exempt); initial submissions need not follow APSR
  stylistic requirements; self-citation **sparingly and in the third person**, and "do not redact your
  self-citations"; 12-point font, double-spaced main text, page numbers, footnotes not endnotes;
  references in the **author-date system of the Chicago Manual of Style, 17th edition**, with authors'
  full first and last names and links for unpublished work; **ORCID required for corresponding
  authors**; WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility and alt text at publication.
- **https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/information/author-instructions/submitting-your-materials**
  — Cambridge "Submitting your materials" (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: "Review the APSA Style Manual"
  before submitting; electronic submission only at **Editorial Manager** (`editorialmanager.com/apsr`);
  opposed reviewers need specific reasons; an **anonymous manuscript file** that removes author
  identifiers (including collaborators/RAs), personal/institutional data links and acknowledgments, and
  uses third-person self-reference instead of redaction; supplementary appendices may be uploaded for
  reviewers (table of contents if more than one); optional APSR Overleaf template (submit the PDF).
- **https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/information/author-instructions/fees-and-pricing**
  — Fees and pricing (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: hybrid journal; Gold OA **APC £2,610 / US $3,655**
  (additional taxes may apply); colour charges £200 / US $320; no submission fee listed.
- **https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/information/journal-policies/open-access-options**
  — Open access options (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: hybrid Gold OA, institutional agreements and
  the Cambridge Open Equity Initiative; **preprints may be posted anywhere at any time, including before
  submission**.
- **https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review**
  — APSR on Cambridge Core (publisher home, current issues).

## Peer-review & editorial policy

- **https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/information/peer-review-information/review-process**
  — Review process (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: **double-anonymized** peer review; an initial
  suitability judgment, with **roughly two-thirds of all submissions not sent out for review**; the
  journal aims for **at least two external reviewers** per manuscript; editors may desk-reject authors
  who repeatedly decline invited reviews; **desk rejects may not be appealed**; appeals after external
  review wait fourteen days and must be filed within 60 days (handled via the ethics editor; decisions
  need at least two editors); review transfer out (within 4 weeks of rejection) to a listed set of
  participating journals and transfer in from journals with anonymous review.
- **https://apsanet.org/publications/journals/american-political-science-review/apsr-submission-guidelines/**
  — Historical (2026-06-01 reading; **not on the live page as of 2026-11-24**, see 需复核): papers
  were described as first screened for possible **desk rejection** on
  grounds of **remit** (not research / not political science / out of scope / not in adequate
  English), **ethics** (plagiarized or unattributed text; unethical procedures), and **substance**
  (missing a key element / insufficiently significant findings / failure to engage relevant
  literatures); **substantive desk rejections require at least two editors**; decision categories
  are **reject / accept / revise-and-resubmit**, with R&R **reserved for papers very close to
  publishable quality**; **strong support from all or nearly all reviewers** is generally necessary,
  but publication decisions are **at the editor's discretion**.
- **https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/information/peer-review-information/instructions-for-peer-reviewers**
  — Instructions for peer reviewers (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: reviewers must keep manuscripts
  confidential and may not share them with any AI tool; AI help with reviews limited to minimal,
  non-generative uses; authors may transfer reviews under the Review Transfer Policy.

## Research transparency & data policy

- **https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/information/journal-policies/research-transparency**
  — APSR Research Transparency policy (re-read 2026-11-24, unchanged). Verifies: authors of **conditionally accepted** manuscripts
  must deposit a **reproducibility package in the APSR Dataverse** (a dedicated repository with
  permanent identifiers); the editorial office **reviews the package to verify that the manuscript's
  tables and figures can be reproduced** and that the research process is documented; when data
  cannot be shared (ethical/privacy/legal reasons) authors must **explain why**, give **README
  instructions on how to obtain the data**, and where possible **provide synthetic data**; code must
  still be shared when data cannot; Data Availability Statements describe where evidence can be found.
- **https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataverse/the_review** — **American Political Science Review**
  collection on **Harvard Dataverse** (the APSR Dataverse). Verifies: replication materials are
  hosted on Harvard Dataverse.

## Owner, publisher, editors, history

- **https://apsanet.org/PUBLICATIONS/Journals/American-Political-Science-Review/About-the-Editors/**
  — About the Editors (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: Lead Editors **John Gerring (UT Austin)** and
  **Monika Nalepa (University of Chicago)**; Managing Editor **Dragana Svraka**; 14 current Associate
  Editors (several joining between November 2025 and June 2026) across
  multiple institutions; owner = **American Political Science Association**; publisher = **Cambridge
  University Press**.
- **https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Political_Science_Review** — Verifies: APSR founded
  **1906**; flagship general-interest journal of the discipline; published by Cambridge University
  Press for APSA.

## Live-check / 需复核 items

- **Current editorial term / start date of the Gerring–Nalepa team**: verified 2026-06-22 (APSA
  About-the-Editors; Political Science Now "Meet the New Editorial Team"). Co-Lead Editors **John
  Gerring (UT Austin)** and **Monika Nalepa (University of Chicago)** lead a team of sixteen editors
  at thirteen universities; the team **began 1 June 2024 and serves through 31 May 2028**. Still
  re-verify the masthead before submission, as associate editors rotate.
- **Research Note word cap (7,000 vs 8,000)**: the submission-guidelines page states Research Notes
  **< 7,000 words**; a separate "Notes from the Editors: Research Notes" piece references **8,000
  words including footnotes and references at final publication**. Treat **7,000 at submission** as
  the working cap and confirm the current figure and what it includes on the live guidelines page.
  On 2026-11-24 the Cambridge Preparing your materials page still says research notes "should be less
  than 7,000 words" at submission; the 8,000-word publication figure was not re-checked. 需复核.
- **Desk-rejection grounds and the two-editor rule** (remit / ethics / substance; substantive desk
  rejections need at least two editors; R&R reserved for papers very close to publishable quality;
  strong support from all or nearly all reviewers): taken from the APSA page on 2026-06-01, but on
  2026-11-24 the APSA page only links to Cambridge, the old `peer-review-policy/` URL returns 404, and
  neither the Cambridge Review process nor Preparing your materials page restates them. Kept as
  guidance; 需复核.
- **Desk-rejection rate, time-to-decision, and number of reviewers**: the Review process page now
  states that **roughly two-thirds** of submissions are not sent out and that the journal aims for **at
  least two** external reviewers (verified 2026-11-24); time-to-decision is not stated. 需复核.
- **Submission fee / APSA membership**: re-verified 2026-11-24 (Cambridge Fees and pricing and
  Open access options pages) — no submission fee is listed and no APSA-membership requirement is
  stated. APSR is a **hybrid** Cambridge journal; Gold OA after acceptance is optional, with an APC of
  **£2,610 / US $3,655** (unchanged; plus tax; institutional agreements and the Cambridge Open Equity
  Initiative may cover it). Re-verify the current APC before submission.
- **Full current Associate Editor roster and subfield coverage**: the roster changes with the
  editorial team; confirm the current masthead on the About-the-Editors page. 需复核.
