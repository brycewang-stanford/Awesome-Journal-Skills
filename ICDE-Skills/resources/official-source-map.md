## Verified facts used across the skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

- **Identity and lineage.** ICDE is the IEEE Computer Society's flagship data-engineering
  conference, one of the three top database venues alongside ACM SIGMOD and VLDB/PVLDB. It
  publishes classic conference proceedings in IEEE Xplore, not a journal-of-the-conference
  like PACMMOD or PVLDB.
- **Editions.** ICDE 2026 = 42nd edition, Montréal, Canada, May 4-8, 2026. ICDE 2027 = 43rd
  edition, Copenhagen, Denmark, May 17-21, 2027. ICDE 2028: not yet announced.
- **Two rounds per edition, Pacific-Time deadlines.** ICDE 2027 Round 1: submission June 11,
  2026, rebuttal August 8-15, notification September 10, camera-ready October 10, 2026.
  Round 2: submission November 11, 2026, rebuttal January 8-15, notification February 10,
  camera-ready March 10, 2027. All deadlines 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time (not AoE).
- **No revisions in ICDE 2027.** Each round ends in Accept or Reject after an author
  rebuttal; the ICDE 2026 two-phase Revise & Resubmit model (4-week window) was not carried
  forward. The July build's search renderings could not settle this; the live call can.
- **1-year wait.** Rejected papers cannot return to ICDE until a year after their last
  submission; Round 1 rejects go to Round 1 of the next edition, not to Round 2.
- **Single-blind review.** Author names remain on the submission; reviewers are anonymous to
  authors. This is the opposite of SIGMOD's double-anonymous model.
- **Assignment.** At least 3 reviewers per paper, coordinated by an area chair / senior PC
  member who writes a meta-review.
- **Format.** IEEE double-column template; research and EAB papers at most 12 pages excluding
  references and the AI-generated content declaration; no appendix; PDF; ORCIDs for all
  authors at submission. ICDE 2027 lists no short-paper category (ICDE 2026 had 6-page
  short papers).
- **Caps.** At most 5 research-track submissions (regular + EAB) per author per round.
- **Platform.** Microsoft CMT (https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/ICDE2027); each author must
  hold a CMT profile with domain and individual conflicts, or the paper is desk rejected.
- **Artifacts.** Supplemental material (code, data, artifacts) is expected via an openly
  accessible URL, its availability is considered in evaluation, and its absence must be
  explained; EAB papers must provide all artifacts.
- **Generative AI.** AI cannot be an author; AI-generated content (text, figures, images,
  code, including tools used for data processing or experiments) is disclosed in the
  acknowledgments section; editing/grammar help is generally outside the policy.
- **TKDE relationship.** Selected best ICDE papers are invited to submit extended versions to
  IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE). Separately, a TKDE Poster track
  lets recently TKDE-accepted articles present at ICDE.
- **Award lineage for exemplars.** The IEEE TCDE ICDE 10-Year / Ten-Year Influential Paper
  Award: 2025 to SpatialHadoop (ICDE 2015); 2023 to the Bw-Tree (ICDE 2013). ICDE 2024
  Industry and Applications Best Paper to Alibaba PolarDB.

## Maintenance protocol

- Re-anchor the whole pack when a new edition site appears; carry only the two-round pattern,
  the CMT platform, the single-blind default, the IEEE-Xplore publication model, and the TKDE
  relationship forward as priors — and re-read even those.
- If two official pages disagree (call vs. important dates vs. guidelines), prefer the newest
  statement, record the conflict, and treat the program chairs' email as controlling. (ICDE
  2027 example: the call says reviews are coordinated by a "senior PC member", the guidelines
  say "area chair"; the registration paragraph in the guidelines says presentation is not
  required, while the camera-ready instructions assume an author presents.)
- When updating exemplars, require an IEEE TCDE award citation or an IEEE Xplore record plus a
  second independent confirmation; exclude on any disagreement, and never attribute a paper to
  ICDE from a DBLP guess alone.
