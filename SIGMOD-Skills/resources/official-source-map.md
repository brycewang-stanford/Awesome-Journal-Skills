## Verified facts used across the skills (re-verified 2026-11-24)

- SIGMOD research papers are journal publications: accepted papers appear in
  PACMMOD issues, one issue per submission round, and authors are invited to
  present at the conference (sources 1, 6, 7).
- SIGMOD 2028 runs four research rounds with paper deadlines on January 17,
  April 17, July 17, and October 17, 2027, abstract + COIs a week earlier
  (January 10 / April 10 / July 10 / October 10), an author-feedback phase about
  two months in, a first verdict about three months in, a one-month revision
  window, and a final decision about five months after submission (sources 1, 2).
- Submissions: 12 pages excluding references, unlimited references, current ACM
  2-column `sigconf` template with fonts/margins/spacing unchanged, PDF at most
  10 MB, Microsoft CMT, strict double-anonymity including funding and colleague
  acknowledgements (source 1). An optional appendix may be uploaded only as a
  separate PDF; anything other than references after page 12 is a desk-reject
  format violation. Accepted papers get 13 pages excluding references in the
  camera-ready, with no appendix.
- Authorship mechanics (new for 2028): one CMT account per author across all
  submissions, at least one author with an institutional CMT address, ORCIDs for
  every author at abstract submission, conflicts declared per author by the
  abstract deadline (source 1).
- Revision mechanics: decisions are Accept / Reject / Minor Revision / Major
  Revision; one month to revise; +1 content page; revision letter up to 4 pages;
  per-reviewer colour highlighting with section/page/line locators (source 1).
- Rejection triggers a 1-year research-track embargo (12 months or 3 rounds,
  whichever is less); withdrawal is free only within one week after the
  deadline, later withdrawal counts as rejection; each author is capped at 10
  research submissions across all SIGMOD 2028 rounds and, new for 2028, 3 per
  cycle (source 1).
- Generative AI: ACM authorship and AI policies apply; AI used in conducting the
  research must be described in the methods section; AI used only to help write
  the submission needs no disclosure; AI cannot be an author (source 1).
- ARI is optional and post-acceptance, and awards the Artifacts Available /
  Artifacts Evaluated - Reusable / Results Reproduced badges; the Best Artifact
  award weighs reproducibility, ease of reproducibility, portability and
  replicability (sources 8, 9).
- SIGMOD 2026 took place in Bengaluru, May 31 - June 5, 2026; SIGMOD 2027 meets in
  Huntington Beach, California, June 13-18, 2027; SIGMOD 2028 meets in Seoul,
  June 17-23, 2028 (sources 3, 11, 12).

## Cycle-volatile by construction

Round dates, page budgets, revision-letter mechanics, embargo wording, the
author cap, platform choice, ARI edition details, and track structure are all
re-declared each cycle. This pack teaches the durable machinery — quarterly
rounds into PACMMOD issues, revision-letter culture, ARI badging — and treats
every number above as a dated observation, not a promise.
