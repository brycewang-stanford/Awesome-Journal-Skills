## Verified 2026-cycle facts used in skills

- ICDM 2026 is the **26th IEEE International Conference on Data Mining**, hosted by Northeastern
  University in **Shenyang, China, November 12-15, 2026** (source 1). The 2025 edition (25th) was
  in Washington DC (dblp, source 7); the count is consistent.
- **Single deadline, no abstract gate.** Full papers were due **2026-06-06** (11:59 PM AoE);
  there was **no separate abstract submission deadline**; notification **2026-08-16**;
  camera-ready **2026-09-09** for the Research Track (sources 2, 10, 11). The Applied Track call
  gives **September 16, 2026** for its camera-ready (source 3) — a conflict with the dates page
  recorded rather than resolved. ICDM does not run KDD-style multiple cycles.
- **Research Track is triple-blind.** ICDM has imposed triple-blind submission and review **since
  2011** (sources 2, 9): author identities and referee identities are both concealed; names are
  known only to the PC Co-Chairs; author names are disclosed only after ranking and acceptance
  are finalized. Authors must conceal identity and affiliation **throughout the whole paper**.
- **Preprints:** authors should not upload or publicize the submission on preprint servers
  during the review period; preprints posted before submission may stay up (source 2).
- **Reproducibility checklist required at submission**; the answers become part of the
  submission and reviewers weigh reproducibility in the decision (sources 2, 3).
- **Applied Track is new in 2026 and is single-blind** (source 3); a paper goes to one track
  only. Demo (4 pages) and BlueSky (4 pages excluding references) are also single-blind
  (sources 1, 13).
- **Blue Sky Track** (CCC-sponsored) solicits visionary/forward-looking papers (sources 4, 13).
- **Format:** IEEE two-column, **maximum 10 pages including the bibliography and any appendices**;
  submissions longer than 10 pages are **rejected without review** (sources 2-3, 9). Everything
  counts inside the 10 pages — there is no separate reference or appendix budget.
- **Regular/Short designation:** all papers are submitted in full-paper format and designated
  Regular or Short after acceptance. In 2026 both designations carried a **ten-page limit**
  (sources 2, 11), so Accept-as-Short was a designation, not a forced compression (earlier
  editions' short-paper lengths varied; source 8).
- **Proceedings** are published by the IEEE Computer Society Press and indexed in **IEEE Xplore**
  and dblp. At least one author must register and present in person for a paper to appear;
  authors who cannot travel join a designated online session with a recorded presentation as
  backup; every accepted paper also presents a poster (sources 2, 11).
- **Chairs (2026):** General Chairs Claudia Plant and Xiaochun Yang; PC Chairs Danai Koutra and
  Xiaofeng Gao (source 12).

## 待核实 register (verify live before stating as fact)

- **Author response.** The 2026 Research and Applied Track calls describe **no rebuttal or
  author-response phase** (the "responses ... become part of each submission" wording refers to
  the reproducibility-checklist answers, not to a rebuttal). Whether a future edition adds one
  remains 待核实.
- **Camera-ready date for the Applied Track** — September 9 (dates page) vs September 16 (Applied
  call).
- **Registration fees, workshop/demo/tutorial details, and any artifact badging** — 待核实 per

## Still cycle-volatile

Edition number aside, treat dates, track lineup (the Applied and Blue Sky tracks were new in
2026), review model per track, anonymity mechanics, response policy, page cap, short-paper
handling, presentation and registration rules, and any artifact policy as **re-set every year**.
If a call page and an IEEE Xplore or committee announcement disagree, prefer the newest official
instruction and record the conflict.
