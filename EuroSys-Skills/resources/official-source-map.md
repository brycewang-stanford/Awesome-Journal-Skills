## Verified cycle facts used in the skills (EuroSys 2027, re-verified 2026-11-24)

- EuroSys 2027: **Rabat, Morocco, April 2027**, workshops on Monday April 19; the official home
  page gives both "April 19—22" (banner) and "April 19 to 23" (text) (source 3).
- **Spring cycle:** abstracts **May 7, 2026**, papers **May 14, 2026**, reviews **July 29**,
  author responses due **July 31**, notification **August 21**, camera-ready **September 25,
  2026** (source 1).
- **Fall cycle:** abstracts **September 17, 2026**, papers **September 24, 2026**, reviews
  **January 6, 2027**, author responses due **January 8, 2027**, notification **January 29,
  2027**, camera-ready **March 5, 2027**. All deadlines AoE (sources 1, 3).
- **Budget:** at most **12** pages of technical content plus unlimited reference pages;
  **supplementary material** (proofs, additional results) may be uploaded as a **separate
  file**, optional for reviewers — the paper must stand on its own (source 1).
- **Format:** A4 or US letter; text and figures inside a **178 x 229 mm (7 x 9 in)** block;
  two columns **≥8 mm** apart; **≥10-point** font on **≥12-point** leading, captions included;
  numbered pages; the **SIGPLAN** LaTeX or MS Word templates are encouraged (source 1).
- **Per-author cap:** at most **three** submissions per author per cycle; excess may be
  rejected without review (source 1).
- **Double-blind**, good-faith anonymization: third-person self-citation, identity-free
  repository links; related work currently under review is cited anonymously and emailed to
  the PC chairs. arXiv, technical reports, talks and social media are not concurrent
  submissions, but the submitted version needs a **substantially different title and
  system/tool name** (source 1).
- **Author responses:** optional; limited to correcting factual errors or answering reviewer
  questions; no new experiments or data, no work done since submission, no promises; authors
  are strongly encouraged to stay within **500** words (source 1).
- **Outcomes:** accepted papers are **shepherded** and acceptance is tentative until the
  shepherd approves the revision; a paper close to acceptance may be offered
  **revise-and-resubmit** to the following cycle with a list of concerns (treated as a
  submission to that later deadline, no guarantee); a paper not accepted is **ineligible for
  the immediately following cycle**, and a rejected revision likewise (source 1).
- **AI use:** EuroSys follows ACM's authorship policy, under which **any use of AI tools must
  be fully disclosed in the submission** (source 1).
- **Other policies:** ACM policy on research involving human participants; ORCID for all
  authors; NDA-encumbered papers returned; at least **one full registration** (ACM member or
  non-member) per accepted paper (source 1).
- **Open access and APC:** ACM publications are 100% open access; authors whose corresponding
  author is not at an ACM Open institution pay an APC unless waived; 2027 subsidized rates
  **$500** for ACM/SIG members and **$750** for non-members, scheduled to end after 2027
  (source 1).
- **Artifact evaluation** (source 5): optional, single-blind, cooperative, between notification
  and camera-ready; badges Available / Functional / Reproduced in three allowed combinations;
  spring artifacts due August 31, 2026; fall artifacts due February 8, 2027 (the CFP marks the
  fall artifact dates "to be confirmed"); a **Distinguished Artifact** award for artifacts
  earning all badges.
- **Chairs:** General Chairs Rachid Guerraoui and Rafael Pires; PC Chairs Pramod Bhatotia,
  Lydia Chen and Andreas Haeberlen; AE Chairs Redha Gouicem and Solal Pirelli (sources 1, 4, 5).
- **Test-of-Time** recipients (source 7) include Dryad (2017), Coccinelle (2018), SnowFlock
  (2019), Delay Scheduling (2020), Omega and BlinkDB (2023), and GeePS (2026); **Borg** (EuroSys
  2015) is listed among past recipients without a year label — it is **not** the 2019 award
  (2019 went to SnowFlock), correcting this map's July 2026 entry.
- EuroSys 2026 was the **21st** edition, Edinburgh, UK, April 27–30, 2026 (source 8).
- EuroSys 2025 (20th edition): Rotterdam, March 30 – April 3, 2025, co-located with ASPLOS
  2025; best papers **CacheBlend** (DOI 10.1145/3689031.3696098, pp. 94–109) and **SpInfer**;
  85 accepted papers from 696 submissions were reported by secondary sources (treat counts as
  reported, not official).
- Organizer model: EuroSys the conference is run by per-edition General and Program Chairs
  under the EuroSys chapter of ACM SIGOPS; there is **no standing editor-in-chief**. Chairs
  rotate every edition; re-check the current organization page before naming anyone.
