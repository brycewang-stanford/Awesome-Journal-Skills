# AEJ: Economic Policy — Official Source Map

Primary and authoritative sources behind every AEJ: Economic Policy–specific fact injected into this
skill pack. **Access date for the original refresh: 2026-06-20.** AEJ: Economic Policy is published by the
**American Economic Association (AEA)**, which owns and operates the editorial and publication process.
Submission rules, review mode, fees, style guide, data/code and disclosure policies, and the masthead were
**re-verified 2026-11-24** against the live AEA pages listed below (all read directly). Changes found on
2026-11-24: **submissions must now be author-anonymized** (AEA three-year RCT of author-anonymized review,
launched fall 2026), so the pack's earlier "single-blind — the submitted manuscript carries the title,
byline, and affiliations; do not anonymize" rule is wrong for new submissions (title, byline and
affiliations at the top of the first page now apply to accepted articles only); **JEL codes and keywords
are not stated** on any current AEJ: Policy page (see 待核实); the portal is **ScholarOne Manuscripts**;
the average article is 33–34 typeset pages. Editor, co-editors, fees and the 40/45-page recommendation
were unchanged. Re-confirm volatile items on the official page immediately before relying on them, since
AEA pages change. Items that could not be confirmed from a primary fetch are marked **待核实 (to be
verified)**.

## Official AEA / AEJ: Economic Policy pages

- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/pol** — AEJ: Economic Policy journal home (AEA; re-read 2026-11-24).
  Verifies that the journal covers a range of topics, "the common theme being the role of economic policy
  in economic outcomes," including public economics; urban and regional economics; the public-policy
  aspects of health, education, welfare, and political institutions; law and economics; economic
  regulation; and environmental economics; that it publishes **four issues per year (quarterly)**; and
  the metadata **ISSN 1945-7731 (print) / 1945-774X (online)**.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/pol/editors** — Editors of AEJ: Economic Policy. Verifies the masthead
  used in this pack (see Editor facts below; re-verified 2026-11-24, unchanged since 2026-06-22).
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/pol/editorial-policy** — AEJ: Economic Policy Editorial Policy
  (re-read 2026-11-24). The page **still says** "All manuscripts submitted to the *AEJ: Economic Policy*
  undergo single-blind peer review—the name of the author is revealed to the referee(s), while the
  referee(s) remain anonymous to authors" — i.e., not yet updated for the anonymization RCT. Also verifies
  coeditor assignment and consulting-coeditor conflict rules, return without review, AEA Data Editor review
  of conditionally accepted papers, e-comment options, and reviewer AI-disclosure rules.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/pol/submissions** — AEJ: Economic Policy Submission Guidelines
  (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies: original work, no concurrent submission; CrossRef Similarity Check; AEA
  Disclosure Policy; AI may not be an author and AI use in preparing the manuscript, including drafting or
  editing text, "must be briefly described during the submission process"; data/code before acceptance for
  empirical, simulation or experimental work; field experiments in the AEA RCT Registry. Fee schedule
  (members USD 200 / 100 / 0, nonmembers USD 300 / 200 / 0 by country income; 50 percent refund if rejected
  without review); publication fee USD 15 per typeset page for papers first submitted on or after 1
  February 2024. **Length:** average article 33–34 typeset pages; manuscripts strongly recommended not to
  exceed 40 pages (11-point, 1.5 spacing, 1-inch margins) or 45 pages (12-point, 1.5 spacing, 1-inch
  margins), including figures, tables, references, and appendices. **Manuscript preparation (changed fall
  2026):** "The AEA has launched a three-year randomized controlled trial of the effects of
  author-anonymized peer reviewing, commonly referred to as 'double-blind'"; an **abstract of 100 or fewer
  words is required**; PDF under 10 MB when reasonably possible; style guide optional at submission;
  manuscripts **must be anonymized** — title and abstract at the top of the first page; author names,
  affiliations, contact information and the acknowledgement footnote in a separate PDF labelled "title
  page"; self-citations by name, never "my/our"; no identifying locations, IRB approvals, or
  personal/institutional website links; no RCT-registration entry in the reference list; supplementary
  appendices and response letters anonymized. Submission on **ScholarOne Manuscripts**
  (https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/aej-policy). A cover letter "is not required and should only be used
  to provide substantive information" (coeditor conflicts, data-access limitations). Each (co)author's
  **Disclosure Statement** PDF must accompany the manuscript; submissions without them "will be considered
  incomplete and will not be reviewed." AEA templates optional (review mode); Chicago author-date preferred;
  AER / AER: Insights transfer option.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/news/rct-announcement** — AEA announcement, 10 September 2026 (re-read
  2026-11-24): three-year RCT of author-anonymized ("double-blind") reviewing at the AER, AER: Insights and
  the four AEJs, designed by Amy Finkelstein and J-PAL North America; launch expected fall 2026 (the
  submissions page says it has launched).
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/pol/style-guide** — AEJ: Economic Policy style guide (re-read
  2026-11-24). Verifies: abstract "must not exceed 100 words"; footnotes, not endnotes; Chicago author-date
  citations; tables at most 9 columns wide; "Do not use asterisks to denote significance of estimation
  results. Report the standard errors in parentheses"; appendices A, B, C.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/pol/accepted-article-guidelines** — Accepted article guidelines
  (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies title and byline at the top of the first page **for accepted
  manuscripts**, native files, signed data/code forms, individual disclosure PDFs, and a separate
  Supplemental Appendix PDF.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/pol/reviewers** — Reviewer guidelines (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies
  that reviewers remain anonymous to authors; the page no longer describes the review as single-blind.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/data-code** — AEA Data and Code Availability Policy (applies to
  all AEA journals incl. AEJ: Economic Policy); the same policy is served at
  **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/data/data-code-policy** (February 2026 version, re-read 2026-11-24).
  Verifies that the **AEA Data Editor** assesses compliance, including by conducting **reproducibility
  checks** and verifying the accuracy of the information provided, **before final acceptance**; that the
  **AEA Data and Code Repository** (hosted at **openICPSR**) is the deposit venue and the Data Editor has
  automatic access to draft deposits; and that for data that cannot be made public, authors must preserve
  materials, make code public, and document sources, with the policy stating it is "generally not
  acceptable that data be provided 'upon request' if the request must be approved by the authors
  themselves."
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/disclosure-policy** — AEA Disclosure Policy (applies to all AEA
  journals incl. AEJ: Economic Policy; re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies that each author must disclose
  interested-party financial relationships (at least USD 10,000 over three years) and funding sources
  relevant to the research in a separate "Disclosure Statement" PDF, including IRB status.
- **https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/policies/low-income-country-fees** — AEA Country Designation for
  Submission Fees (re-read 2026-11-24). Verifies that the submission-fee tier depends on the income
  classification of the country in which the authors work.

## VERIFIED facts injected into the skills

- **Publisher / cadence / scope.** American Economic Association; quarterly (four issues per year); scope is
  the economic analysis of policy across public economics, urban/regional economics, the public-policy
  aspects of health/education/welfare/political institutions, law and economics, economic regulation, and
  environmental economics. ISSN 1945-7731 (print) / 1945-774X (online). (journals/pol)
- **Review model — author-anonymized submission (since fall 2026).** Every manuscript must be anonymized;
  names, affiliations, contact information and the acknowledgement footnote go in a separate "title page"
  PDF. Under the AEA RCT some papers are reviewed with author identities hidden from referees and some
  single-blind; the editorial-policy page still describes single-blind review. Referees remain anonymous
  to authors. (journals/pol/submissions; news/rct-announcement; journals/pol/editorial-policy)
- **Editor:** C. Kirabo Jackson (Northwestern University). **Co-editors:** Nathaniel Baum-Snow (University of
  Toronto), Lucas Davis (University of California, Berkeley), Naomi Feldman (The Hebrew University of
  Jerusalem), Brian Knight (Brown University), David Seim (Stockholm University), Amanda Starc (Northwestern
  University). Data Editor Lars Vilhuber. (journals/pol/editors, re-verified 2026-11-24)
- **Submission fee:** AEA members — USD 200 (high-income), USD 100 (middle-income), USD 0 (low-income).
  Nonmembers — USD 300 (high-income), USD 200 (middle-income), USD 0 (low-income). Papers rejected without
  review are refunded 50 percent of the submission fee. (journals/pol/submissions)
- **Publication fee:** accepted papers first submitted on or after **1 February 2024** are assessed **USD 15
  per typeset page**. (journals/pol/submissions)
- **Front matter / length:** abstract of **100 or fewer words**; title and abstract on the first page of the
  anonymized manuscript; recommended maximum equivalent to **40 pages at 11-point / 1.5 spacing** or **45
  pages at 12-point / 1.5 spacing**, including figures, tables, references and appendices; PDF under 10 MB.
  JEL codes and keywords are customary but not stated on the current pages. (journals/pol/submissions)
- **Portal / cover letter:** ScholarOne Manuscripts; a cover letter is not required and is only for
  substantive information (coeditor conflicts, data-access limitations). (journals/pol/submissions)
- **AI:** AI may not be an author; AI use in preparing the manuscript must be briefly described during
  submission. (journals/pol/submissions)
- **Data & code:** the AEA Data and Code Availability Policy applies; the AEA Data Editor (Lars Vilhuber)
  runs a reproducibility check on an openICPSR deposit **before** final acceptance. (journals/policies/data-code)
- **Disclosure:** AEA Disclosure Policy — each author submits a separate Disclosure Statement PDF with the
  manuscript (interested-party funding and relevant financial relationships, IRB status).
  (journals/policies/disclosure-policy; journals/pol/submissions)
- **House style:** AEA house style — report standard errors in parentheses (no significance asterisks),
  tables at most 9 columns, online appendix for long material, author–year references. (journals/pol/style-guide)

## Unverified / volatile items (待核实)

- **JEL codes and keywords.** The 2026-06-20 map listed "JEL codes and keywords" as required by the
  submission guidelines; on 2026-11-24 none of the AEJ: Policy submissions, style-guide,
  accepted-article, or editorial-policy pages mentions JEL codes or keywords. The skills now call them
  customary; the ScholarOne form itself was not inspected.
- **How long the anonymized-submission rule lasts.** The RCT is announced as three years from fall 2026;
  whether the editorial-policy page's single-blind wording will be updated was not stated as of
  2026-11-24.
- **Submission platform specifics** — submission is through ScholarOne Manuscripts; exact upload prompts
  (title-page file designation, AI-use field) are portal-rendered and should be confirmed in the system at
  submission time.
- **Founding year** — AEJ: Economic Policy launched with the other AEJs in vol. 1, 2009; the inaugural
  issue was **February 2009** (two issues in 2009, moving to quarterly in 2010), verified 2026-06 via the
  AEA journal issues page, JSTOR, and IDEAS/RePEc.

(Resolved 2026-11-24: the editor/co-editor roster, fee schedule, and AEA Data Editor were re-confirmed on
the live AEA pages.)
